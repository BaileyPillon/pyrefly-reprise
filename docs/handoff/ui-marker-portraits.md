## What changed, per game

### FFX only: the marker (`src/ui/ffx/targetCone.ts`, `target-cone.css`, `TargetCursor.ts`)

- The pointing glove (the 44 x 30 `HAND_SVG`, and the big `PRO_HAND_SVG` the pro look drew in the treated chapters) is replaced by **the retail game's cone in Ink & Gold**: an ivory cone, a gold rim, a gold collar with a red jewel, a warm glow, a gentle 8 px bob, docked on the figure's left with its tip toward the figure, in the glove's own place. Shape from the real game (`t1x.jpg`, `t2x.jpg`, `e15.jpg`; `research/observed-ffx-steam-2026-09-26.md` section 2.1: a small white cone with a red collar); the finish is ours, from the Ink & Gold look picked on 2026-09-19. The glove was never in the retail game.
- **Size** [ours]: 6.2 percent of the window wide (99 px at 1600), 64 px at the least, 168 at the most (the glove it replaces is 144 px at 1600).
- **Where it shows:** wherever FFX's target cursor drew the glove, desktop and phone alike: the cone is now the marker in every FFX chapter, in the treated chapters (with the pro look's three-line flag, which docks off the cone's own box exactly as it docked off the glove) and out of them (the one-line plate under the figure, kept clear of the cone). A group cast still draws no marker between allies, as before.
- **It docks on the other side when its own side is hidden.** The cursor's layer is clipped behind the command rows (`bracketClip.ts`, PR-0178), so a marker docked on the left of the leftmost ally (Tidus, standing against the command list) was mostly hidden, glove and cone alike (the first desktop capture of this lane showed the cone's collar behind the ITEMS rows). The cone now docks on the figure's right, tip pointing left, whenever 30 percent or more of its left box is behind the panels or the command rows and its right box is behind less (`targetCone.coneFlips`; `TargetCursor.setStackRows` is fed the command rows by the F1 HUD; the glove never flips). On the upright phone the layer is not clipped, so nothing flips there.
- **The way back:** `?marker=hand` (or `__pyrefly.marker('hand')` before the next draw) draws the glove exactly as release 76 did (the small hand, or the pro glove in the treated chapters). Pinned in `tests/unit/pro-look-markers.test.ts`.
- **FFX-2 is unchanged, byte for byte:** `TargetCursor` reaches the cone only when `chrome === 'ffx'`; the FFX-2 branch computes the same values as before (read line by line), and `pro-look-markers.test.ts` pins that the FFX-2 cursor's whole HTML is identical with either marker word, with no hand and no cone in it.

### Both games: the portrait (`src/engine/CutInMode.ts`, `BigMoves.ts`, `BigMoveCutIn.ts`, `TurnCutIn.ts`, `BattleMomentsHeld.ts`, ...)

- **No portrait at a turn's start, in either game.** `TurnCutInBeat.wants` answers false unless `?portraits=turns` (the old behaviour: each member's first turn of each battle, exactly as before; pinned by the three old test files now running under that word). `?portraits=off` shows none at all.
- **A big move slams the member's portrait in and names the move**: the same approved slab (`ui/inkgold/cutin.ts`), with the move's name where "YOUR TURN" and the name were: "TIDUS · OVERDRIVE" above "Swordplay". The first time a member does a big move in a fight the full slam (180 ms in, 450 ms hold); after that a quick flash (110 ms hold, a lighter veil). Keyed per member, per battle; a retry starts a new battle. Never at SKIP speed; never for a fiend's move (the fiends have their own banners); never over an open FFX-2 command menu (D-357).
- **One moment, not stacked.** For a party big move the portrait replaces the Overdrive's name slab (non-staged path: the opening waits on the portrait in its place), replaces the C6 splash (`FxStagePort.actionOpen({ splash: false })`: the port still hears which big move is on, so the blows' own effects keep working), and, where the pro staging plays its opening (Chapter I), the opening plays on with its light, push and glint while its small name stands aside (`OpeningCue.named: false`). A fiend's big move, the telegraph and the held breath are not touched.
- **Which moves are big (the sources; `BigMoves.ts` has the citations):**
  - **FFX:** command kind `overdrive` (a character's, or an aeon's) and `summon`. Source: `research/ffx-vs-ffx2-presentation.md` section 7 (the game's own Aeons setting plays "each aeon's full summon and Overdrive animations once, then the short version"; [verified: 2 sources]) and the task's list. The game's first-time-in-full rule is the model for the full slam then the flash (section 6 has the same rule for the Spherechange).
  - **FFX-2 (the narrowest sourced list; the sources do not settle a wider one):** the change into a special dressphere (Special Dress Up; "its own unique animation", `ffx-vs-ffx2-presentation.md` section 6, [single source]; **it rides the engine's `spherechange` event with its `special` field, because the engine emits no `action-start` for a dress change**: this lane's first portrait commit hooked an action-start that never comes for a dress change, and the commit after it moves the hook to the event) and the main body's top move of each special dressphere: Floral Fallal's **Great Whirl**, Machina Maw's **Vajra**, Full Throttle's **Sword Dance** (`ffx2-combat-core.md` sections 2.9.2 and 3.15: each is the 30 AP, long-charge, whole-field finisher; Sword Dance is "the strongest thing in the game per action", [single source]). The portrait shown is the owning girl's (Yuna, Rikku, Paine), never the part's. Not listed, so not a portrait: the ordinary dress change, Trigger Happy, Lady Luck, Mascot's moves, every other long charge, a special part's ordinary moves.
- **`?portraits=` modes:** `big` (default), `turns` (the old behaviour), `off`. `__pyrefly.portraits(...)` sets it live.

## Open questions for Bailey (the driver's picks, each with its alternative)

1. **FFX-2's list of big moves is the narrowest sourced one** (4 entries: Special Dress Up and the three finishers). The alternative is a wider list: the ordinary dress change (the "signature FFX-2 sequence", `ffx2-combat-core.md` section 6.6), Trigger Happy, Lady Luck's spins, Mascot's Moogle Beam and Cactling Gun, every long charge (`CT`). Each would bring the pop-up back more often; none is called out by the sources the way the special dresspheres are. In the shipped chapters a special dressphere is hard to reach (Chapter IV's Yuna owns Floral Fallal, has not learned Great Whirl, and her Protection Halo grid has five nodes to fill first), so in play FFX-2 may show no portrait for a whole fight. That is the cost of the narrow list; the alternative gives the player a portrait more often.
2. **The portrait replaces the Overdrive's splash painting and name slab** (one moment). The alternative is the slab first and then the splash painting, as before the slab was added; it is the stacking the card said not to do. `?portraits=turns` or `off` gives the splash back at once.
3. **Repeats get a 110 ms flash, not nothing.** The card says "repeats get a quick flash"; the alternative is no portrait on a repeat at all.
4. **The cone's size** (6.2 percent of the window) is mine, read off the card's faked frame. It is one number, `coneSize`.
