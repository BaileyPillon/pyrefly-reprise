### Method (`work/recut.py`, `work/parts.py`, `work/install-mirror.mjs`; numpy and PIL only, repeatable)

1. **Masters** (the six 2x pose masters of each boss, `recut.py`): binary mask at alpha 0.5; loose pieces and the grey/lilac strips beside Bahamut's right wing erased (per-pose polygons in `work/hints-<subject>.json`: inside a polygon only the light, low-saturation pixels go, so the black spars stay); interior holes under 12,000 px filled (Bahamut's pin and torso holes, the gaps between wing and body; in a pose PIECE the limit is per part, body 12,000, head 4,000, wings 3,000, tail 600, because the inside of a tail's loop is a real gap), the texture of armour holes cloned from the painting itself (offset search on the hole's ring of known pixels, low frequencies from the surroundings); narrow channels sealed (closing r 5, r 3 for Seymour so his hair tips stay); wing-membrane bays closed (closing r 46, only on membrane-coloured pixels, grown at most 60 px); outline smoothed (sigma 1, 2.6 on the membrane); eroded 2 px; a 1.6 px anti-aliased edge; translucent interior alpha kept (Bahamut's far wing), the old rim of a closed hole made opaque.
2. **Colour**: the pale matte colour is removed from the colour too, not only the alpha: the outer 5 px band and everything under alpha 0 take colour pulled (push-pull) from the painting 5 px or more inside the ORIGINAL outline (and not from beside a filled hole), so no white is left for a texture filter to bleed. This is the finding of the mockup lane, measured: the 2x idle master's colour under alpha 0 averages (94, 84, 99) and its outer 6 px band is 40 luminance brighter than the painting 10 px in.
3. **Rig parts** (`parts.py` for the idle parts, `variants2.py` for the pose pieces, then `despeckle.py`). Bahamut and Seymour are rigs (`rigs/ffx2-bahamut`, `rigs/seymour-flux-body`), so the pieces on screen are the rig's parts, not the paintings. The base parts are exact crops of the idle master, so they are rebuilt from the cleaned idle master (new alpha = cleaned alpha times the part's old share of the master's alpha; the pixels the clean-up added go to the nearest part whose mesh covers them). **The pose pieces (attack, cast, hurt, telegraph, ko) were first cleaned in their own pixel space (round 1: torn and bitten wing edges, blob bays; review major 2). Round 2 found what a piece is** (`work/registration-probe.py`): a plain crop of its pose master, resampled bilinear by the part's `tex` (0.33 wings and tail, 0.4 body, 0.55 head) at an integer offset, found by masked normalised cross-correlation (0.97 to 0.998 for 24 of 25 pieces), alpha = the master's alpha times the cut (the label partition with its underlap and feather). `variants2.py` therefore builds each piece as the same crop of the CLEAN master (colour and alpha), keeps the old cut as a share (old piece alpha over old master alpha), gives the pixels the clean-up added to the nearest part whose mesh covers them, keeps the rig tool's own joint fills where the master had nothing, and clips to the part's mesh as before. The clean masters are now what the rig draws, and the rig gate's recorded fit overlap is back up (attack 0.975, cast 0.980, hurt 0.977, telegraph 0.969, ko 0.971, against 0.949 to 0.957 in round 1 and 0.978 to 0.993 for the original art). **`head.cast.png` is the exception:** the motion lane re-graded it (the mouth glow), it is not a crop of the master (colour match 0.40), so it keeps its round-1 clean-up. `despeckle.py` then removes the isolated islands under about 100 px (49 over both the rig and the pose textures: the specks the review counted), and the 1x twins are re-derived. Strips beside Bahamut's right wing are erased in the masters by per-pose polygons (`work/hints-ffx2-bahamut.json`), so the pieces inherit that. Seymour's pieces were already clean and are unchanged.
4. **1x twins and 1x paintings** are derived by the repo's own tier rule (`tools/rig/rig-tiers.mjs`: `writeTiers`, `verifyTiers` PASS), never authored.
5. **`rig.json`** changes only in its stamp: the costume's two sha256 (the re-cut idle painting), the keys' recorded fit overlaps (`fit.iou`: the gate now reads attack 0.975, cast 0.980, hurt 0.977, telegraph 0.969 and ko 0.971 for Bahamut against 0.983, 0.993, 0.993, 0.978 and 0.982 recorded for the original art; Seymour's all within 0.02; every key is above the gate's bar of 0.75 and the rest pose above 0.98) and the gate's own stamp, by `node tools/rig-gate.mjs` on a private copy (`work/gate-try.mjs`). Nothing in its meshes, weights, keys or motion.
6. **Not touched**: rim masks (`*.rim.png`), normal maps (`*.normal.png`), the `splash` painting, the `ko.1/.2/.3` frames, the 3x and 4x paintings (a rig subject never draws them), every sidecar, the manifest.

Every number is [ours]: the erosion, closing radii, hole threshold and band widths (tuned against the two bosses' masters), the footprint fractions (`BOSS_FEET`, measured from the clean idle masters: the lowest 300 px band of Bahamut's 2048 px master, the lowest 160 px of Seymour's 2422 px master, 5th to 95th percentile), the card's filter.

## For the driver (install)

1. The art goes in at install time, **onto the playtest art state** (`D:/pyrefly-art-motion`; main's `public/art` lacks 143 of the 158 files and a re-cut file would land on nothing). First version the originals in the private art repo (`BaileyPillon/pyrefly-art`), then `node tools/boss-matte-install.mjs --check <art dir>` (every row `ready`, or `done` when it is installed), then `node tools/boss-matte-install.mjs --install <art dir> --from D:/pyrefly-art-boss` (each old file is unlinked first, so a hard link is never written through; it refuses any folder with a `missing` or `conflict` row and changes nothing). The old and new sha256 of every file (full, 64 hex) are in [boss-matte-files.md](boss-matte-files.md).
2. The pose-scale record pins the sha256 of every pose painting: `tests/unit/engine/pose-scale-art.test.ts` names the twelve (`ffx2-bahamut/*`, `seymour-flux-body/*`: "the painting changed since it was measured") until `measure` is run again on them. The registration numbers are unlikely to move (same size, same anchor, the silhouette moves by about 2 px), but the record is the art lane's.
3. The rig gate stamps its content (`gate.digest`) and the costume's sha: `node tools/rig-gate.mjs --rig <folder>` must be run on both rigs again before a deploy (`--verify` is what the deploy runs).

## Not done (disclosed)

- **The ink line.** The mockup's "one thin dark ink line instead of the glow" is NOT in the art. I built it (the outer 3 px of the master darkened toward the painting's own shadow colour, `recut.py --ink=0.55`) and judged the clean edge against it at the game's scale (about 0.47 of the 2x master at 1440p, `work/an/ink-compare.jpg`): the ink line reads slightly crisper on the wing membranes, the clean edge is already free of any pale line. The ink cannot be applied to the pose pieces without the rig tool's own registration (their cut edges run through the figure and would draw a dark seam), and an idle with an ink line crossfading to poses without one would pop. So the build ships the clean edge everywhere; the engine's own ink (the pro look's outline) is untouched.
- **The pose pieces keep their underlap** (the partition's overlap with the neighbouring part, covered by the neighbour). Round 2 builds each piece from the clean master, so the specks and torn edges of round 1 are gone; `head.cast.png` alone keeps its round-1 clean-up, because the motion lane re-graded it and it is not a crop of the master.
- **Mortiorchis** (Chapter I's second enemy) is not re-cut: it keeps its rim and matte, as in today's build. Not in the mockup, not asked.
- **The Mega Flare banner's `splash` painting, the `ko.1/.2/.3` frames and the 3x and 4x paintings are not re-cut.** The splash is its own cut-in painting with a soft glow and a hard rectangular notch at the left; it is drawn for about a second. A rig subject never draws the 3x and 4x masters.
- **The reflection is the engine's own** (a mirrored camera draws the boss with the room), not the mockup's synthetic one; it follows the clean figure. The bold pass's reflection and contact ellipse stand on `BOSS_FEET`.
- **The pose-scale records** (`docs/target/pose-measure.json`, the registration rows) pin the sha256 of the twelve re-cut paintings: `pose-scale-art.test.ts` names them until the registration is measured again. `node tools/art-install.mjs` does it at install (`measure.py measure <subjects> --write` with ComfyUI's python; this machine's has no scipy, so it was not run here). The silhouette moved by about 2 px. These art-pin tests (and `pose-install-0926`, `-0930`, `pose-stature-hold`, `held-defend-*`) stay red on the playtest art state whatever this lane does: a push gate for the install, not for the code.
- **Re-run after any further change to the art:** `node tools/rig-gate.mjs --rig <folder> --art <art dir>` (the gate stamp is for exactly the content it judged). The candidate `rig.json` files already carry the stamp for the files in the mirror.
- The frame cost is measured on this machine at 60 Hz in headless Chrome (2560 x 1440: +0.0 to +0.4 ms for the mask pass); 120 Hz is not measured. Pad to Victory was not driven by a pad bot (the engine's auto player plays whole fights through the same presenter, see Evidence).
