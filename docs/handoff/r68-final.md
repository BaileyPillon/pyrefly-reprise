### 2. FFX-2 only: leaving a battle with a command menu open frees the battle HUD (`FFX2BattleHud.unmount`)

- Cause (as SUMMARY 5.1 said): `openCommandMenu` puts a `keydown` listener on `window` and hands its teardown to `deps.onOpen`, which the HUD keeps as `closeMenu`. `unmount` never ran it; the listener's closure (`deps.onSelection`, `deps.panels`, ...) held the HUD and every detached node under it. Every way of leaving a battle (Chapter select, Restart, Quit, the end of the fight) ends in `BattleScreen.exit` then `releaseParts` then `hud.unmount()`, and every HUD decorator (`phoneBattle`, `withStatusLooks`, `OmnisGlowLook`, `OversoulLook`, `CoachLayer`) calls the inner `unmount`, so one change at the root covers the pause menu's three exits too.
- Fix: `FFX2BattleHud.unmount` closes the open menu first (`closeCommandMenu()`, else `applySelection(null)` as before), in two lines in place of two (the file is 1,442 lines and does not grow).
- Test `ffx2-hud-release.test.ts` (jsdom, the real HUD, a real menu): five visits leave the `window` and `document` listener count flat (before the fix 1, 2, 3, 4, 5); an answered menu and a battle with no menu are as clean; the HUD's root element is garbage-collectable after five visits (a `WeakRef` and a forced GC; before the fix all five stayed alive; after it at most the last, which jsdom's selector engine holds as `lastContext`, found by heap-snapshot retainers, so the test says "at most one"). `tests/unit/helpers/forceGc.ts` is the small helper.

### 3. Both games: the revive's first frame no longer pops the lying body

- Cause (as SUMMARY 5.2): `ProneLay.layProneFigures` cleared the slide on the frame `isProne` turned false; `isProne` reads the ACTIVE plane, and `setPose('idle')` makes the standing plane active at once while the KO painting is still on screen (fade 0.96 after the first frame). The slide went from 0.49 to 0 (0.74 in the measured Chapter I) in one frame.
- Fix: `PaintedActor.proneLingers` (a prone plane still showing) and, in `ProneLay.ts`, a `held` set: the slide is kept until that plane has faded (under 0.001), then cleared; a figure knocked down again while held is laid afresh; a hard cut (no crossfade) clears at once as before.
- Test `engine/revive-prone-hold.test.ts` through the real `PaintedActor` (Yuna's pose records, a synthetic underside), `setPose('ko')` then `setPose('idle')` and the stage's own `layProneFigures` every frame: the KO plane's x and the slide are unchanged on every frame it shows; before the fix frame 0 read slide 0 against -0.49.
