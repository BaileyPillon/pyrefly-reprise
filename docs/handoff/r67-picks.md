2. **The stale rules differ between the three lock tools, and I kept each as it was.** `measure/lock.mjs` and `rig-record-lock.mjs` take a lock over only when it is 15 minutes old (`lock.mjs` documents that a dead pid is still honoured, because the pid may be a wrapper whose job still runs); the lane recorder takes over a lock whose pid is gone at once (and a garbage file once it is 5 s old). The brief's parenthetical "older than maxMinutes or a dead pid" matches none of them exactly. A recorder taking a lock whose holder is a live job behind a dead wrapper pid is a second way two jobs can overlap that atomic creation does not close. Unifying the rule is a policy change (the pass's rule is "nobody removes another lane's lock before 15 minutes"); say if you want it.
