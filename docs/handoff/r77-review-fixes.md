## M1. Tidus's hurt key and Rikku's cast key read low for one reason, and the reading is what was wrong (both games, critic plumbing)

**What the review found.** The critic's rig path (`critic/runner/lib/continuity-rig-probe.mjs`, `rg.tu`) read a rig's stature head to soles from the `crown` landmark to the feet. The crown is ONE point of the head, carried by the head bone, so a head turned by a key carries it round the head and below the top of the silhouette: Rikku's cast (head `rot` -0.6061, 35 degrees) 2.3 percent of the body, Tidus's hurt (`rot` -1.1106, 64 degrees) 7 percent. The rig gate reads the silhouette (0.909 for Tidus's hurt, 0.910 for Rikku's cast, the paintings' own) and passes both; the critic read 0.8596 and 0.8873 against the 0.885 floor (0.90 less the two slacks). The integration moved Rikku's art to clear the one key and could not do the same for Tidus's (a 76 rig px head lift would detach the head from the neck).

**The fix (the reading, not the art).** `rg.tu` is now the head read as a DISC: the head landmark's height plus the posed distance from the head landmark to the crown landmark, straight up the idle plane (`topOfHead` in the probe). A turn of the head leaves that where it was; a head carried down or up (a bow, a crouch, a fall: what a stature is) still moves it. Untilted it is the crown landmark; with no head landmark it is the crown as before; a rig with no crown (every other shipped rig) is read by its box exactly as before. Only the two party rigs carry a `crown` landmark, so no other figure's reading moved.

**Offline, from the rigs' own bones** (`tools/rig/rig-pose.mjs`, crown or disc top to the feet landmark, over the idle's; the scratch scripts are `rig-model.mjs` and `rig-top.mjs` in the pass's folder, not committed):

## M2. Both rigs go live by default and no browser had been run on them (both games)

**The smoke** (the review-browser lock taken for each job with `tools/rig-record-lock.mjs`, `maxMinutes` 10, released after; headless Chromium on SwiftShader; a scratch dev server on a scratch config that answers `/art/**` from the r77 mirror and nothing else, so the art played is the art r77 would ship; seed 1, `skipCutscenes`, `autoBattle('intended')`, a per-frame sampler of each party actor's key and idle-plane world position through the stage's own camera; the scripts are in the pass's folder, not committed). - **S2, Chapter IV (FFX-2), no address word.** Console: `[rig] rikku-dark-knight on (gate ok, 8 parts, 13 bones, 11 keys, 1x maps, lit)`, the prediction of `SMOKE-SPEC.md` word for word; `__pyrefly.rigs()` reports the switch `on` with reason `default`; 0 failed or missing `/art` requests in the run. Her cast key and her hurt were looked at in the frames (a 960 x 540 screenshot each, the cast one enlarged): the recut head sits on the shoulders with the sword raised, no detached head, no seam at the neck at that size.
- **The two vertical steps of 110 and 82 px, explained.** At a turn start with the idle key showing (no swap), the whole figure goes up 0.15 world units over three frames and comes down over four (plane y 0.852, 0.987, 1.033, 1.015, 0.963, 0.901 with the rig; 0.890, 0.967, 1.018, 1.054, 1.031, 0.983, 0.924, 0.890 with the painting set), the head riding with the plane (`dHeadWorldY` equals `dPlane`): **it is FFX-2's run-in leap (MF-04, `RunEase`), stage motion, and the painting set makes the same hop at the same moment (t = 30.9 to 31.3 s of scene time) when `?partyrigs=off` plays the same seed.** The critic reads a painted figure's plane in the idle plane's own coordinates (the stage's hop cancels) and a rig's head and feet on the screen, so it was counted as a step for the rig and not for the painting. It is not a defect and the rig adds no motion of its own to it. (The sampler read 20 frames a second, the game's frame clamp, not the lane's 60: per-frame sizes are three times the lane's.)
- **A difference worth knowing, not a defect:** with the paintings, idle to cast moves the plane 0.19 world units sideways in one frame (the registered offset between two paintings' anchors); the rig has no plane swap and no such move.
- **S1, Chapter I (Tidus, FFX), no address word, 960 x 540.** Console: `[rig] tidus on (gate ok, 9 parts, 13 bones, 14 keys, 1x maps, lit)` (the prediction of `SMOKE-SPEC.md` word for word, and `[rig] seymour-flux-body on` for the boss beside it); the switch `on`, reason `default`; 0 failed or missing `/art` requests. - **S5, the phone, 390 x 844 (a coarse-pointer upright window, so the game's phone tier), Chapter I.** `[rig] tidus on (gate ok, 9 parts, 13 bones, 14 keys, 1x maps, lit)` on the phone tier too (the 1x maps, the budget the lane measured at 96.0 of 110 MB), the three figures and the boss inside the field above the party cards, 0 failed `/art` requests. **Not measured here:** the phone's memory peak (a headless desktop Chromium has none; the JS heap read 190 MB on the desktop browser and means nothing for a phone). **Decision (the driver's, his to overturn): both pilots stay ON by default, the switch is untouched.** The one thing that said "keep Rikku off" (the two unexplained steps) is explained as the run-in leap the painting set makes at the same moment; the recut head and the first turn were looked at and are clean; Tidus's rig line, art requests and phone layout read as predicted. The alternative the review named, a per-subject default (`partyRigAllowed`, `PartyRigsSwitch.ts`) that ships Tidus only, was not needed and not built; `?partyrigs=off` still gives the paintings back, and flipping `PARTY_RIGS_DEFAULT` to `'off'` still does it for both. **Not covered by this smoke, still owed to the focused review:** a real GPU at 60 Hz (the sampler ran at the game's 20 Hz frame clamp on SwiftShader), a real phone's memory peak, WebKit.
