## What the code needed (small, because the chapters already named every key)

- **Wiring that was a stand-in.** `chapter-meta-dark-aeons.ts` and `chapter-meta-penance.ts`: the snapshots now show the chapters' own idles (Dark Anima and Dark Bahamut; the disc and the Right Arm) where the player's Anima and Bahamut and Tidus's idle stood in; comments say the plates exist. `heroArt` (`pause/ch34-dark-aeons`, `pause/ch35-penance`), the sprite keys (`PENANCE_SPRITES`, the Dark bodies' `spriteKey`) and the portrait ids were already the right names. - **The Separation's lift (Penance only, FFX only).** The engine plants a painting's lowest opaque row on the floor, and the half disc's torn edge (`baselineY` 633) is 280 rows above the whole disc's rim (913) at the same pixel scale, so left alone the half that stays would drop 30.7 percent of the figure's height (about 1.7 world units at the stage's 5.6) and read as the whole body falling. `scenes/penance.ts` names `PENANCE_FORM_LIFTS = { 'penance-split': PENANCE_SPLIT_LIFT }` (`(913 - 633) / 913`, in `data/ffx/penance-ids.ts`); the new optional `SceneStaging.formLifts` carries it; `engine/FormLift.ts` turns it into world units; `PaintedActor.setPerch` stands the plane that high (the same sum as the hover and the hop, so the shadow shrinks with it and `contentQuad` moves with it; the aim points the HUD reads keep the figure's original height, so the numerals and cursors stay on the upper half); `BattlePresenterStage.add` and `.setArt` call it only on the way into or out of a lifted form (under the form change's flash and fade). Proved on the real actor: the whole disc's top 5.6, the half disc's 3.88 unlifted and 5.6 lifted; and on the pixels: laid on the whole disc's idle with that lift, the half disc's silhouette matches the first 480 rows at alpha IoU 0.995 (0.99 or better is a test; the rows below are the part the torn seam took away), so the number is the right one and not only a number. Contract entry in `docs/CONTRACT-CHANGES.md`.
- **Registration (hard rule 16), through the repo's own installer** (`node tools/art-install.mjs --target D:/pyrefly-art-r76 --plan <ten subjects, register only>`: `measure.py measure ... --write`, `measure.py table`, the pose gate; the interpreter is spawned by the tool). Ten subjects in `tools/posescale/subjects.json` (`game: foe`, `rowFromBaseline`, no face box, as the player aeons and the Keeper), the `standing: true` rows of the wide-but-standing poses (Dark Valefor, Dark Ixion and the half disc: a painting wider than 1.15 times its height is read as a body lying down otherwise; the player Valefor and Ixion carry the same rows), `docs/target/pose-measure.json` and `src/data/art/poseRegistrationFoes.ts` (50 rows; **additions only**: existing subjects are unchanged; the stale `vegnagun-tail` ko line that the generator would also flip to `waived` is left as it was, as in release 75), and the 74-pin set `driver:2026-11-06-dark-aeons-penance-art (items 220i and 220j, delegated by Bailey)` in `docs/target/approved-hashes.json` (the D-number is owed by the records pass).
