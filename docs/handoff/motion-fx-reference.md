## 1. What both games share, and where they split (timing grammar from the research)

Rule 14 case for this whole section: the cast structure is FFX only (it is read from the FFX motion banks); FFX-2 has its own banks and its own measured numbers (1.2). Do not carry an FFX number into Chapter IV.

### 1.1 FFX (Chapter I): how an action is built [all sourced: R1]

* Every motion clip of Lulu, Tidus and Auron plays at 30 frames a second; one frame is 33.33 ms if the update tick is 30 Hz (the tick itself was not read; the live frame counter ran about 31.7 a second) [sourced: R1:18-19].
* An action is three programs: a start clip (`_00`, once), a hold clip (`_01`, loops until something replaces it) and a release clip (`_02`). The tag functions start `_00`, wait for it, start `_01`, and a later tag (magic launch, item launch) swaps in `_02` [sourced: R1:28 and R1:95-96]. **How long the hold lasts is set by the spell effect, not by the clip** [sourced: R1:95, R1:120].
* The release clip carries the hit signal ("battle signal 0"); the game applies the planned hit on the tick the played frame reaches it; the end signal (signal 2) clears the target's flag [sourced: R1:23, R1:25]. So: an effect's visible start is the release frame, and the numeral follows it.
* Release frames, counted from the start of the whole cast: Lulu type 1 frame 73 (2.43 s), type 2 frame 64 (2.13 s), type 3 frame 75 (2.50 s), each plus the dwell the effect sets; Tidus's only cast type (type 2) 32 frames in (1.07 s); his Skill clip 36 frames in (1.20 s); Auron's cast type 2 59 frames in (1.97 s), his Skill clip 33 frames in (1.10 s) [sourced: R1:41-43, R1:63-64, R1:81-82]. After the release the figure is not free for 15 frames (Lulu type 1), 33 (type 2) or 70 frames (type 3, 2.33 s); Tidus's cast has 27 more frames after the hit, Auron's 51 [sourced: R1:41-43, R1:63, R1:81, R1:121].
* Which spell uses which of Lulu's three cast types is **not read** [gap: R1:131, open item 3].
* A normal melee attack is a run-in (Tidus RUN_00 15 frames then RUN_01 looping), then ATTACK1_00 (21 frames; **his hit signal is on its last frame, frame 20, 0.67 s in**; the clip also carries the hit twice, so the signal is delivered twice on that tick, what the second delivery does is open), then ATTACK1_01 (end signal at frame 10, 31 frames from the strike start, clip end 15, so the strike clips total 36 frames = 1.2 s), then the way back (ATTACK1_02 14 frames, ATTACK1_03 11) [sourced: R1:60-61, R1:71]. Auron: a 10-frame step, then ATTACK1_01 with the hit on its frame 12 = 22 frames from the strike start (0.73 s), end signal at 42 frames (1.40 s), clip end 49 [sourced: R1:78]. Stand-still strikes (ATTACK2) hit at frame 26 of 86 (Tidus, 0.87 s), 20 of 80 (Auron), 20 of 60 (Lulu's doll throw) [sourced: R1:39, R1:62, R1:80]. "Tidus and Auron use ATTACK1 on ground targets and ATTACK2 in the place-and-strike case [I: flag meaning not read]" [sourced: R1:94].
* Hurt is one clip with no signal: Tidus 30 frames (1.0 s), Auron 40 (1.33 s; heavy 65 or 60), Lulu 50 or 47 (1.67 s) [sourced: R1:45-46, R1:66, R1:84-85]. KO is a fall of 61 to 65 frames with the floor sound at 43 to 57 percent, then a one-frame hold forever [sourced: R1:47, R1:67, R1:86]. Victory is a one-shot of 3.67 to 4.0 s then a loop [sourced: R1:49, R1:69, R1:88].
* **Items** are slower than attacks: start clip, a hold loop, then a release with a sound event 1 to 3 frames before the release signal; two release clips, the first when the target group overlaps the party's front line (party targets), the second otherwise. Command to release is about 1.5 s (Tidus), 1.8 to 2.0 s (Lulu), 2.1 s (Auron) plus the hold [sourced: R1:44, R1:65, R1:83, R1:96, R1:121].
* **What the screen showed for an item (live, the Steam copy) [M]**: a Potion used on oneself: the camera closes in on the user, the staff stays in her hand, no item object is drawn, green glow particles gather round her, the gesture ends with the hands to the chest; the heal lands about 5.8 s after the command is taken (wall clock, camera move included). A Phoenix Down used on an ally: the camera closes in on the user, a green glowing orb rises from his hand, he makes an overhand toss toward the target, sparkles fall on the target, no item model at any point; the revive lands about 3.5 s after the command [sourced: R1:106-113]. Still open: an enemy target, Lulu's and Auron's gestures, frame counts [sourced: R1:115, R1:131].

### 1.2 FFX-2 (Chapter IV): how an action is built [all sourced: R2, R3, R4, R8]

* The logic runs at **29.97 steps a second** (60 / 1.001 / 2); 1 step = 33.37 ms [sourced: R2:18, R2:59-75].
* A girl's plain Attack hits on a clip frame from the game's tables: Mascot clips Yuna 12, Rikku 4, Paine 11; Bahamut 41; a set with no hit read takes the end of its attack clip as an upper bound [sourced: R3:14-17, R3:29-30]. (White Mage has no Attack command at all [sourced: src/engine/tactics/ffx2-bahamut.ts:208 comment citing research/ffx2-bahamut.md section 3.4 to 3.6].)
* **Effects**: Paine's Fire: the countdown reached 0, her MP was paid in that step and the effect began; it played for **75 steps (2.50 s)**; the command completed with recovery 0 and her menu opened the next step; the whole command (choice to menu) 175 steps = 5.84 s [sourced: R2:139-141]. Darkness (a Dark Knight, two targets, hit in the same step): 81 steps from its begin event, which comes about 2 steps after the release when nothing waits, so 83 steps (2.76 s) from release [sourced: R3:21-22]. A Potion: 79, 79 and 80 steps (about 2.66 s), taken as 80 for every item [sourced: R3:22-23, R3:32-33]. A monster cast of effect 176: 94, 94 and 94 steps (3.14 s; a fourth 114 while it waited behind another effect); effect 368 (Strength Up) 84 steps (2.80 s) [sourced: R2:143-144; R3:19-20].
* **Effect animations do not stop the gauges** (Attack, Fire and the monster casts seen); only the Wait flag does [sourced: R2:20, R2:151-160]. So an FFX-2 effect may run while other girls' ATB gauges fill and menus open: it must never hold the fight.
* **Haste speeds animations in FFX-2** (attack animations and casting animations, victory poses) **but not the spell animations themselves**; it turns the girl's ATB gauge red [sourced: R7:115 (single source there: FF Wiki *Haste (Final Fantasy X-2 status)*)]. In FFX the research says Haste speeds the victory pose [sourced: R7:102].
* A charging FFX-2 character "takes a visible stance / aura" while the purple CTIM bar fills; an enemy charging a magic attack is "surrounded by an aura"; one charging a special physical attack has an odd stance and speeds up; an ordinary physical attack fires with no wind-up [sourced: R8:52, R8:3243-3245 (single source there: Split Infinity guide)].
* Hurt clips for the girls are about 24 to 35 frames (dmg1), the same length for a critical in all but one set [sourced: R4:78-80].

### 1.3 Pyrefly dispersal, for a defeated unsent or fiend (both games, from R7)

Fiends and unsent disperse into pyreflies when vanquished; Seymour Flux "dissolves into pyreflies and vanishes, unsent, no catharsis" (do not fade him out); Mortiorchis "dies repeatedly" and re-forms (see 2.6); Bahamut is "a possessed aeon" and the doc says only "assume a machina death" is wrong [sourced: R7:133, R7:146-147, R7:151]. Presentation of KO and victory belongs to the other lanes; it is listed so the spell notes below do not contradict it.

### 2.1 Cure, Cura (and the party-wide Curaga look): the green column

* **Game case: FFX only.** (FFX-2's White Mage Cure is 3.3 and is not drawn from this.) In the demo it is Yuna's Cure and Cura on a hurt member, and Kimahri's White Wind is a Cura-strength party heal [sourced: `research/ffx-combat-core.md:403`].
* **Shape and motion [sourced: V2 41.0 to 45.0 s, which is the trailer's original-resolution 4:3 sample (pillarboxed); the same column is in 16:9 HD at about 138 to 140 s of V2 and in V7, seen at 2 s steps].** A tall, narrow **column of pale mint-green and white crystalline shards** rises from the target's feet: it appears with a white splash at its base (42.0 s), grows taller and narrower with fine vertical streaks like a beam (42.5 s), is brightest and whitest in the first 0.5 to 1 s, then thins to a faint shimmering sheet with dust or sparkle at its base (43.5 and 44.5 s). A **flat ripple ring** lies on the ground at its foot (visible at 44.0 s). A few **cyan darts** (short bright dashes, trailing sparks) fly sideways from the burst (43.0 and 43.5 s). The column is about one character tall at first and up to about two characters tall at its peak.
* **Numerals.** The heal numeral (white, small) appears at the lower third of the column when it is brightest and stays while the column lives (42.0 to 44.5 s).
* **Several targets.** The clip heals four characters in a row (numerals 741, 697, 713 and 672), one column about every 1 s (columns start near 42.0, 43.0, 44.0 and 45.0 s), the first two on the left of the party and the next two on the right. A party-wide heal therefore draws **one column per character in turn**, not all at once. The picture alone does not tell Cure from Cura from Curaga; the caption was not read [sourced: V2 41-45 s].
* **Colour.** Mint green, white core, cyan accents; no pink or gold [sourced: V2].
* **Timing.** Each column lives about 1.5 s (pop to gone) [t: V2]. The gap between targets is about 1 s. For the **cast** before it: there is no Yuna motion table; Lulu's white or black magic casts release at 2.1 to 2.5 s after the command (cast types 1 to 3) and Tidus's and Auron's at 1.1 and 2.0 s [sourced: R1:41-43, R1:63, R1:81]; Yuna's is [gap] (R1:115).
* **Layering.** Additive light in front of the figure, a ground ring in the floor plane, the numeral on top. **The scene around darkens to a dusk grade within about 0.5 s of the effect starting** (V2 41.0 s is a bright field, 41.5 s onward the same shot is dark) while the characters keep their light [sourced: V2 41.0 to 41.5 s]. This is the only layering fact I can source; I did not see the darkening in the Blizzaga and Overdrive frames (their backdrops are different shots).
* **Caster and target.** The caster's side of a Cure is not in the frames (the clip is a wide shot) [gap]. The target is not seen to react (no recoil, no flash other than the column) [sourced: V2 41-45 s].
* Today's build: a green decal, a soft green column behind the target and 100 rising spiral motes, mint bloom, mark 0.5 s, end 2.1 s (`effects-light.ts:73`) [ours]. The retail picture is a **crystalline column with a ground ring and cyan darts, not motes**.

### 2.6 Seymour Flux and Mortiorchis: what each attack looks like (FFX only, Chapter I)

**Plain statement: no research file and no official video shows any of these attacks. Every shape is [gap]. What can be given is who animates it, how many hits, who is hit, and the timing the data carries.**

Who animates what [sourced: R5:37-39, R5:239, R5:356]: Cross Cleave, Full-Life, Slowga and Total Annihilation are rows in Seymour Flux's own action list (monster record m142) but are **animated on Mortiorchis**; Command 150 ("Auto-Attack Mode") and Mortibsorption are Mortiorchis's own rows (m143). The research's ship rule: the Mortiorchis actor owns the turn slot and the animation, the Seymour actor owns the stats [sourced: R5:356].
