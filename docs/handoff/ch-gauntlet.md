### Findings (for the bench stage and the integrator; each is in a test)

1. **F1: the Ribbon is no Death ward in this engine** (`types.ts`: Ribbon is Zombie, Petrify, Poison, Confuse, Berserk, Provoke, Sleep, Silence, Darkness, Slow, Doom; **not** Death, the Breaks, Curse, Delay, Eject). The preflight's section 4 ("a Death ward is already the Ribbon") and the note's line on Earth Eater are wrong for this engine: **Earth Eater's Megaton Punch (Death 100) kills the member it lands on** (Auto-Phoenix pays a Phoenix Down), and it answers every hit Earth Eater takes standing. `deathproof` on the armour stops it (tested: 30 casts, 30 survivals, each still hurts). That is a party lever for the build/bench stage, not a creature change.
2. **F2: Evasion 255 + Luck 60 makes the two accuracy-2 Attacks harmless.** Neslug's Attack (0x6000, byte 90) and Jormungand's (0x40BA, byte 150) never land on the preset (0 damage and 120 misses in 120 casts each; with Evasion 0 Neslug's lands 60+ times in 120). Same lever as Dark Ixion's blade.
3. **F3: Zanmato byte.** The note and the preflight say "3 on the area three, 4 on the Originals". The file says **Neslug is 3** (Cactuar King, Don Tonberry, Jormungand, Neslug: 3; the other five: 4). The data carries the file's bytes (`zanmatoLevel` 4 for Neslug).
4. **F4: Don Tonberry's Attack is power 20** (row 0x4062, the same row as Dark Valefor's), not the 16 the arithmetic (`calc-54`, "row not read here") assumed: Protect-halved it is 451 to 509 on a member, not 361 to 408. Voodoo is 2,049 to 2,314 a hit.
5. **F5: the party's physical hits are about 1.3 times the note's arithmetic.** Tidus's Attack on DEF 255 is 18,167 to 20,438 in the engine; the note's section 4 has 13,975 to 15,780 (its calc ignores the weapon's Strength bonus). The note's "hits to empty" for the Armored creatures and the Arms is therefore a little pessimistic; the capped cases (99,999) are unchanged. The bench measures.
6. **F6: Doom.** The extra-immunity word 0x301 is Scan, Eject and Auto-Life: **only Neslug (0x4301) is immune to Doom**; Cactuar King, Don Tonberry and Jormungand carry a Doom countdown of 200, the other five bodies 255, Neslug and the parts 3. The data carries `doomTurns` for every one (the engine throws on a Doomable enemy without one).
7. **F7: the parts' Distill immunity** (word 0x22F) has no status of ours; Scan and Auto-Life are modelled, Eject is (correctly) not in the word.

### The three scripts, as the tests pin them (RE n 3.6, 3.2, 3.1)

- **Th'uban** (`m282`): a step counter `v1` that starts at **3** and goes 3, 4, 1, 2, 3...: Step 3 **Attack** on the living front-line member with the **most HP** (a tie is one picker draw); 4 **Rainbow** on the whole front line; 1 a **buff on itself**: Protect if it lacks Protect, else Shell if it lacks Shell, else Regen if it lacks Regen, else nothing (a lost turn); 2 **Convergence** on one random living member (formula 4: 99,999, or 55,000 to 63,000 through a Shell: a certain KO on a 9,999-HP member). **No GetRandomValue decides a move** (pinned over all 65,536 values); the picker alone is drawn. **Its answers** (the script tests no damage, no counterattack gate and no attacker): after any hit it reads the used command's record (`readCommandProperty` affectCTB, affectHP) and queues, in this order, **Haste on itself** for a CTB command (Slow, Hastega on it...) and **Condemn on the front line** for an HP command; the game's queue drops a second command of the same owner, so one answer a hit (no party command has both bits: pinned by a record override). The blow that kills it is answered by nothing; nothing from inside a reaction (the engine's one rule that a counter never triggers a counter, which is also what stops its own Haste from answering itself for ever; the bytes have no gate of their own).
- **Neslug** (`m105`): state `v1`; the mix is **one draw `mod 3`**: 0 **Attack** on the front-line member with the **most MP** (the two-call idiom: `findMatchingChr(front, alive)`, whose pick is drawn and dropped, then `findMatchingChr(MatchingGroup, MP, Highest)`), 1 **Megaton** on a random member, 2 **Slime** on the whole front line when at least one living member is not Slow (two `findMatchingChr` calls over the same matched set, each a picker draw), else the Attack of draw 0. **If Valefor is in the front line the Megaton draw is an Attack at `v0`, the last target it picked** (no new pick: the bytes never write `v0` on that branch; initially 0, Tidus): a lost turn when that actor is not a living target. **A hit** (after the damage, before the death check): out -> HP **below 1/4** of the maximum (strictly, integers): forced **Broken shell.**, Agility 120, state 2; else **below 1/2**: forced **Hides in shell.**, **NullPhysical on** (the engine's `immune-to-physical-damage`, the special word's bit 0x20), **Regen 255** (a permanent status, not dispellable), state 1. In the broken shell, a hit by command **0x203F, the Purifying Salt** (the item) sets Defense and Magic Defense to 1. **Its turn in the shell: Curaga on itself** until HP is **above 3/4**, then Emerges (the writes off, state 0). - **The Ultima Buster** (`m280`, Head `m272`, Arms `m273` twice, formation `zzzz02_80`): the body's counter `v10`: with it below 2 a draw `mod 2` (32,768 each of 65,536) picks **Attack** on one member or **Ultima** on the front line; at 2 or more **Ultima on the front line and, behind it, an Attack** on one member (a queued free action of the same turn), and the counter starts again; it goes up by one after every turn: coin, coin, forced, coin, forced... **The gate** (`onTargeted`, before any damage, for every command that names the body): Defense is written 0, Defend and Armored cleared; then **if the command affects HP, its damage type is physical (1; 3 would too) and an Arm lives: Defense 255, Armored and Defend on for that command**; `onHit` puts Defense 50 and both off.
