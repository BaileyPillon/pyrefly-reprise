## 1. The cause (neither of the two the brief offered)

**The harness reads the rig head exactly. The rig draws the head at different sizes in different keys.** Run on round 33b's recorded data (`critic/rounds/round-33/continuity/<chapter>/continuity/continuity.json`, read only; harness `5e1bbdec47c9`):

- Lulu's rig swaps: 569 recorded by her rig's name in six chapters (568 of them carry a head reading; the first handoff said 568, the review recounted 569), **508 counted, 397 over 3 percent** (Mirror Aeons 164, Sin core 113, Sin face 46, Braska 42, Evrae 19, Yojimbo 13: the round's 397 of 415, to the unit). Worst 19.7 percent, smallest failure 3.1.
- The five other shipped rigs on the same run: Bahamut 0.7 percent worst, Seymour's body 0.5, Ifrit, Valefor, Pterya and the possessed ones 0.0, **0 head failures**. Only Lulu drifts. Painted figures' 18 other head failures are Braska/Flux (PR-0584, not this lane).
- The harness's number (the skinned area of the `head` part, square-rooted) is **the engine's own**: posing the installed `rig.json` through `RigSkeleton` reproduces the recorded ratio to four places for every pair seen often (cast to idle 0.9119, ready to cast 1.1543, idle to ready 0.9500, idle to ko 0.8800, ko to critical 1.1958 ...; 488 of the 506 mesh-read counted swaps equal the key-to-key ratio to 0.0006; the other 18 (six small pairs) were read before the figure had settled out of an earlier move).

The fitter scales her spine (limit 0.95 to 1.1, to reach each painting's stature; `tools/rig/subjects/lulu.json` `fit.scales`), and the head part inherits it. The head bone's own scale is 1 in every key but `ko` (as the fit config says), and nothing in the pipeline looks at the *inherited* one. (`ko` is the exception: `root` 0.8 times `head` 1.1 is 0.88. The lock overrides that authored 1.1: the KO head is drawn at 1.25 times the body's scale, where the file had 1.10. The painted KO's in-game head is 1.00 of the idle's, so this is closer to it, and it is the one key where the lock changes an authored head scale and not only an inherited one.)

## 2. Why the fix is in the engine and not in the rig file

The right layer is the rig data (the head shell bone gets the inverse of the spine's scale in each key). I built it, gated it and it is **not installable today**: Lulu's rig is refused by the full rig gate *as it stands, before any change of mine*, for four reasons that have nothing to do with the head:

| refusal (same on the installed rig and on the held candidate) | installed | held |
|---|---|---|
| `lulu/attack key-iou` overlap with the painting under 0.75 | 0.742 | 0.740 |
| `lulu/defend key-fit-stale` (claims 0.615, the gate reads) | 0.669 | 0.669 |
| `lulu/od-fury key-fit-stale` (claims 0.695) | 0.622 | 0.622 |
| `lulu/sleep key-landmark` head 0.068 of the idle's height from the painting's, limit 0.06 | 0.068 | 0.068 |

(`node tools/rig-gate.mjs --rig D:/pyrefly-art-rigs/lulu --art D:/pyrefly-art-r50 --check`, and against `D:/pyrefly-art-r64`: identical.) The rig was stamped `ok` on 2026-10-18; the pose table moved after it (the W3 sizing rule re-scaled the paintings the keys are held against); the deploy only runs `--verify` (the stamp's digest and coverage), so the stale stamp ships, but **any new content needs a new stamp and `planRigInstall` runs the full gate**. So a refit or re-install of Lulu is blocked until those four are cleared (claims: `--fit-claims`; attack: authored or refit; sleep: refit the head; none is this lane's call). The candidate (a copy of the rig with the shell bone's inverse scale in 10 keys) otherwise passes: 247 poses scanned for holes at 9 joints, none; overlap changes at most 0.006 except the KO's 0.010 (cast 0.754 to 0.756 and critical rise: the painting's head is smaller than the rig's was), no new refusal. That is evidence the same transform has no hole and costs no overlap; the candidate itself stays in scratch.

So the lock is applied **where the keys are compiled** (`src/engine/rig/RigHeadHold.ts`, called from `compileKeys` in `RigKeys.ts`): for a rig named in `RIG_HEAD_HOLD` (`lulu: 'head'`), each key's channels get the shell bone's scale set to the inverse of the scale it inherits, so the head part is drawn at 1.0000. It leaves alone the idle, a key that hides the part (v2), a key that already scales the shell itself (the two front-view keys) and a part not rigid on one bone. It is the rig counterpart of the painted head lock (D-420), keyed by rig name, and it composes with a later refit: a file that carries the counter-scale in its own keys is skipped as `explicit`. It needs no art install and no new stamp (the digest is the file's, unchanged). Blends: head within 1.46 percent of the idle's at the worst point of a straight blend between any two keys (item to ko, half way). **That is a straight-line proof and the game moves the bones with `RigTransition`** (carried velocity can overshoot, and a move can be interrupted by another): measured on seeded chains of up to three interrupted moves, the locked head strays at most **1.53 percent** from the idle's (pinned `< 2` in `rig-head-hold.test.ts`; 12 percent without the lock). A SETTLED swap (the key itself on both sides, `rig.settled === true`) reads exactly 1.0000. A swap read with both ends still mid-move can pass 3 percent in a rare chain (review of this lane: 5 of 40,218 constructed unsettled before/after pairs, worst 4.15 percent, ko to victory), so **"never over 3 percent" holds for settled swaps only** (J-1 below says so).

**What the lock is not (disclosed, review finding 2).** It exists only in the engine. Everything that reads the rig from its FILE still describes the unlocked head: the rig gate's own pose math (`tools/rig/rig-pose.mjs`) and the silhouettes it stamped in `rig.json` (`gate.silhouettes`: box, profile), which the continuity harness's height, feet and jerk readings and the `contentQuad` brackets, `poseShape` and `rigProfile` take from. So "height unchanged" is true by construction and cannot see this change. What the drawn figure's mesh bounds do (stature over the idle's mesh, unlocked then locked; the reviewer's stature probe): cast and item **-1.92 percent** (the head top moves 43 px; 1.0307 to 1.0116 and 1.0248 to 1.0057), critical -0.99, victory **+1.00**, ko +0.65, follow +0.28, attack -0.21, hurt -0.11; ready, sleep, defend and od-fury unchanged. The stamped numbers (cast 1.0308, victory 0.9701) are the file's; a later re-stamp or gate run measures the unlocked head again. **To do when the four gate refusals above are cleared (a Lulu rig lane): move the lock into the data (the shell bone's inverse scale in `rig.json`'s keys, which the engine already accepts as `explicit` and skips), re-stamp, and delete the `lulu` entry of `RIG_HEAD_HOLD`.** Until then the table entry is the only thing holding the head.

**The target is the idle KEY's size (review finding 3).** The lock first held the head to the bind pose's size (1), which equals the idle's only because Lulu's idle inherits no scale; it now holds each key to the size the shell bone has in the `idle` key (1 when the rig has no idle key), so a future table entry for a rig whose idle carries a scale is held to the idle's, as the docs said. Pinned by a synthetic rig with an idle chest of 1.1 (`rig-head-hold.test.ts`; it fails with the target at 1).
