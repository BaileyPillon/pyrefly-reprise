## 2. What it is

Twelve thousand HP, **Armored** (a plain blow is divided by three), and it takes damage from **one element only**: one of Fire, Ice, Thunder, Water, drawn at the start and again every time that element is struck. It **absorbs the other three and Holy** (what you cast of those heals it). The player is never told which: the game's own tell is the spell it answers with.

| What happens | What it does (the game's own script, RE 4) |
|---|---|
| its turn | an **Attack** on a random living member (about 240 to 280); **below half HP (strictly under 6,000), half of its turns (32,786 of 65,536) are Press**: half of everyone's current HP, physical class (Protect halves it), never lethal by itself |
| a **plain physical blow** (no elemental weapon) | answered with the **opposite element's spell on the attacker alone**, the player-side Fire, Blizzard, Thunder or Water (167 to 231): Fire answers a weakness to Ice and Blizzard a weakness to Fire; Thunder and Water are the other pair. **That spell is the clue** |
| an elemental **weapon** blow | of its weakness: no spell, it **shifts**; of another element: it heals it, and the attacker takes the answer |
| **magic of a wrong element** (or Holy) | healed; the **monster-side** spell of the opposite element on **all three** (235 to 324, half with Shell); with an aeon on the field, the weaker player-side spell on the aeon alone |
| **magic of its weakness** | **no spell**: the weakness is redrawn uniformly over all four (one time in four the same), the new rows are written at once, and Spherimorph plays **Elemental Shift** on itself |
| element-free magic (Flare, Demi, Ultima), Scan | the monster-side spell on all three |
| Bio, Dispel, Slow, Haste, Esuna and the rest that do not touch HP | no answer |
| **a Doublecast** | the first spell of the pair is ignored (no answer, no shift); the second is handled normally |

It cannot be put to Sleep, Silenced, Darkened, Confused, Berserked, Provoked, Petrified, Slowed or Doomed; Poison lands (600 a tick); the four Breaks and Zombie land (an **Armor Break takes the Armored division away**: Auron's 382 to 431 becomes about 900 to 1,016); Threaten starts at 25 percent; it is immune to Delay and Bribe and Sensor (the HELP bar prints "Immune to sensors." [observed]). Rewards: 4,000 gil, 3,240 AP (4,860 on an overkill, a last blow of 2,000 or more), a Lv. 2 Key Sphere (two on an overkill), a steal of Ether (common) or Turbo Ether (rare).

**The design fact:** the fight is about reading one clue and acting on it. Probe with a plain blow, cast the opposite of what answers, and read it again after every hit that lands. Everything else (Nul spells, Magic Break, Protect against Press, Armor Break to unlock Tidus and Auron) is support.

**T0 findings (each pinned by a test):**
1. **The starting draw and the shift are exactly the interpreter's**: `GetRandomValue() mod 4`, 16,384 of 65,536 each; the shift from every state is uniform over all four, the old one included (600 weakness hits on the engine: 19 to 31 percent into each, and the old state among them).
2. **The reply matrix of the note is reproduced cell by cell** on the real damage path: 17 commands in each of the four states (Attack with no weapon element and with each of four elemental weapons, Fire, Blizzard, Thunder, Water, Firaga, Holy, Flare, Scan, Wakka's Fire and Water Shots and his Power Shot; Element Reels by its reading), the answer row, who it is aimed at and whether the state moved. The sign of `lastDamage` (heal or damage) is the kernel's, not assumed.
3. **A hit with the weakness is answered by no spell** (only the Elemental Shift): the guides' "it counters everything" is slightly off, as the note says.
4. **The gates**: HP 0, Threaten and a reaction in progress each end the handler before any state change; a Spherimorph that cannot act (asleep, forced onto it in a test) does not move its state either (`isCounterattackAllowed` is the script's first test); its own spell bounced back at it is not an event of the script.
5. **A Doublecast of two weakness hits shifts once**: the first is ignored and the second handled (an aeon or Lulu with Doublecast can take two hits for one redraw).
6. **Press end to end**: 632, 591 and 425 off Tidus, Yuna and Lulu from full HP (half of current HP, exactly the note's), halved by Protect, never lethal, drawn at the right rate below the line (about 50 percent of its turns). **Defend halves it too**: Press and the Attack are physical class (the engine tests use an idle Potion turn and not Defend to read the undefended figures).
7. **The advisor's dry runs are safe**: the state lives in `state.flags`, which a forked context clones, so a preview never spends the live draw.

## 6. Difficulty: measured, not tuned (rule 15)

`tests/unit/chapters/spherimorph-bench.test.ts` (8 always-on, 1 behind `SPHERIMORPH_BENCH=200`), the harness `tests/unit/helpers/spherimorphBot.ts` and the tool `node --experimental-transform-types tools/spherimorph-bench.mjs --seeds 200 --front tidus,yuna,lulu|tidus,auron,lulu [--from 1001]`. **No boss number was touched**; the one lever is the preset's front line. **It is a puzzle fight, so the preflight's target differs from the chain's 70 percent band: the informed line wins about 95 percent or more, and a line that guesses loses almost always. Both hold.** A *line* is a scripted player that reads only what a player reads (the menu's rows, the statuses, the HP bars, **the spell the boss answers with, from the battle's log**) and never the script's hidden state: **careless** (everyone Attacks), **blind** (the skeleton's care, Tidus Attacks, Lulu casts the highest tier of a random element every turn), **advisor** (the shipped tactic, decision for decision), and the advisor with one piece removed.

**The table of record, 200 seeds a cell (seeds 1 to 200), no god mode:**

| Line | Tidus, Yuna, **Lulu** (shipped) | Tidus, Auron, Lulu |
|---|---:|---:|
| careless | **0** of 200 | 0 |
| blind | **5** (2.5 percent): 117 defeats, 78 stalls | 5 (2.5): 195 defeats |
| **advisor** | **200 (100 percent)**, median 23 decisions, 5.00 shifts a fight, no spell taken in, nobody falls | **200**, median 17 decisions, 3.81 shifts |
| no re-probe (trusts the first clue for ever) | 1 (0.5): 199 stalls | 2 (1.0): 198 defeats |
| **no healing at all** | **88 (44 percent)** | 200 (100) |
| no Protect | 200 | 200 |

Seeds 1001 to 1200 on the shipped line: advisor **200**, blind **4** (2.0 percent), no healing **78** (39 percent). A fight is about 0.03 s of engine time.

**What that says, plainly.** (a) **The preflight's prediction held**: a blind cast is a quarter chance of being right and a three-quarter chance of healing it and costing the line (the engine's wrong-element -ra cast heals about 1,300); the guess wins 2 to 2.5 percent, and the careless line, whose every plain blow draws a counter on the attacker and whose caster swings a staff, loses all 200 in about 14 decisions. (b) **Re-reading the clue after every hit is the whole of the skill**: the line that trusts its first reading wins 0.5 to 1 percent, because the weakness moves on every hit that lands. (c) **Once read, the fight is gentle**: six Fira casts and five probes end it, nobody falls, and Protect adds nothing (the Press only begins under half). Healing matters on the shipped line (without Cure, Cura and potions the informed line wins 39 to 44 percent), not with Auron in front, who ends it in 17 decisions. **Disclosed, not nerfed**: the numbers are the game's, and a player who understands the puzzle wins it, as in the game. **Not tried (named):** Lulu's Doublecast (not in the preset), the Overdrives, an aeon, Nul spells and Magic Break, a Switch mid-fight, a player who reads the clue but casts tier one (about 13 casts instead of six).
