## 2. What it is

Twelve thousand HP, **Armored** (a plain blow is divided by three), and it takes damage from **one element only**: one of Fire, Ice, Thunder, Water, drawn at the start and again every time that element is struck. It **absorbs the other three and Holy** (what you cast of those heals it). The player is never told which: the game's own tell is the spell it answers with.

| What happens | What it does (the game's own script, RE 4) |
|---|---|
| its turn | an **Attack** on a random living member (about 240 to 280); **below half HP (strictly under 6,000), half of its turns (32,786 of 65,536) are Press**: half of everyone's current HP, physical class (Protect halves it), never lethal by itself |
| a **plain physical blow** (no elemental weapon) | answered with the **opposite element's spell on the attacker alone**, the player-side Fire, Blizzard, Thunder or Water (167 to 231): Fire answers a weakness to Ice and Blizzard a weakness to Fire; Thunder and Water are the other pair. **That spell is the clue** |
| an elemental **weapon** blow | of its weakness: no spell, it **shifts**; of another element: it heals it, and the attacker takes the answer |
| **magic of a wrong element** (or Holy) | healed; the **monster-side** spell of the opposite element on **all three** (235 to 324, half with Shell); with an aeon on the field, the weaker player-side spell on the aeon alone |
| **magic of its weakness** | **no spell**: the weakness is redrawn uniformly over all four (one time in four the same), the new rows are written at once, and Spherimorph plays **Elemental Shift** on itself |
| element-free magic (Flare, Demi, Ultima), Scan | the monster-side spell on all three |
| Bio, Dispel, Slow, Haste, Esuna and the rest that do not touch HP | no answer |
| **a Doublecast** | the first spell of the pair is ignored (no answer, no shift); the second is handled normally |

It cannot be put to Sleep, Silenced, Darkened, Confused, Berserked, Provoked, Petrified, Slowed or Doomed; Poison lands (600 a tick; **the Woods' bag carries no Poison Fang, section 5**); the four Breaks and Zombie land (an **Armor Break takes the Armored division away**: Auron's 382 to 431 becomes about 900 to 1,016); Threaten starts at 25 percent; it is immune to Delay and Bribe and Sensor (the HELP bar prints "Immune to sensors." [observed]). Rewards: 4,000 gil, 3,240 AP (4,860 on an overkill, a last blow of 2,000 or more), a Lv. 2 Key Sphere (two on an overkill), a steal of Ether (common) or Turbo Ether (rare).

**The design fact:** the fight is about reading one clue and acting on it. Probe with a plain blow, cast the opposite of what answers, and read it again after every hit that lands. Everything else (Nul spells, Magic Break, Protect against Press, Armor Break to unlock Tidus and Auron) is support.

## 3. The data and its sources (rule 6: nothing invented)

Every number is `research/re-ffx-spherimorph.md` (the game's own files: monster file `m121`, formation `mcfr03_00`, the Atel script run in the earlier lanes' interpreter; "RE n" in the files), tagged `[bytes]`, `[run]`, `[arith]`. The six move rows are pinned **word for word** to the kernel fixtures `tests/fixtures/parity/ffx/command_records.json` and `command_status.json` (rows 0x6036, 0x6039 to 0x603C and 0x603F: type, flag words, damage class, rank, chance and duration bytes, power, hits, shatter, element byte) by `tests/unit/chapters/spherimorph-data.test.ts` (28 tests); the Attack is the game's row 0x6000 (`POSSESSED_PLAIN_ATTACK`); the four spells it casts on the attacker are the player's own rows (0x3041 to 0x3044), used by their ids and pinned equal to the note's state table (12353, 12354, 12356, 12355 and 24634, 24633, 24636, 24635). **Rule 5:** the four spells and Press have accuracy formula 0 and never miss (`canMiss: false`); the Attack rolls.

**Gaps, named (not invented):**
- **The gear drop** ("always one piece of gear": four Strike weapons, Piercing on two of the fourteen rows, four Ward armours) is not modelled: the loot record of `m121` is not in `loot/records.ts` (its digest is not in the repo), as for the Keepers. The chapter pays the sourced fields (gil, AP, the Key Sphere, the Ether and Turbo Ether steal); the loot tests list it as a body without a record. Pilfer Gil 16 and the arena price 6,000 have no engine field.
- **The steal's `baseChance`** is 100 (the engine's convention for a steal that lands); the note does not give the chance byte.
- **The party** is the Macalania preset (`[estimate]` over a sourced build point, one region later: the Temple, with Shiva removed and Auron's Shooting Star taken out, the preset's own note says defeating Spherimorph unlocks it); the Overdrive and aeon gauges on arrival, the exact bag and the armour abilities are not sourced for this fight. Nothing was tuned toward a result. The preset's Shell Targe on Rikku and O'aka's gear are the Temple's, one region later, and are kept (flagged).
- **Height and position:** no stature row exists for `m121`; Spherimorph's 3.0 is an `[estimate]`, on the spot Macalania's layout B pins Seymour to.
- **MP:** a monster pays no MP in this engine, so the four player-side spells cost it nothing; whether the game charges its 100 MP (4 a cast) was not read (RE 8).
- **Whether the "for actor" read adds the weapon's element unconditionally** (RE 8, `[inferred]`): the engine's rule is its own damage's: the weapon's element counts only for a command that carries the weapon's properties (a plain Attack, a Break), so Auron's Overdrive in the hands of an elemental weapon is element-free here. The list of Wakka's commands whose elements the script recomputes without the weapon (13 record ids) is ported as is.
- **The two banners** play once each, not on every later absorbed spell as the game's scene does (presentation only: no draw, no number).
- **The launch details** (the field-event gate, the defeat flag, whether the body or the orbs show the element) were not read; the formation leaves the default, a defeat is the retry card, and the element is not shown (SP7).

**T0 findings (each pinned by a test):**
1. **The starting draw and the shift are exactly the interpreter's**: `GetRandomValue() mod 4`, 16,384 of 65,536 each; the shift from every state is uniform over all four, the old one included (600 weakness hits on the engine: 19 to 31 percent into each, and the old state among them).

The sign of `lastDamage` (heal or damage) is the kernel's, not assumed.
3. **A hit with the weakness is answered by no spell** (only the Elemental Shift): the guides' "it counters everything" is slightly off, as the note says.
4. **The gates**: HP 0, Threaten and a reaction in progress each end the handler before any state change; a Spherimorph that cannot act (asleep, forced onto it in a test) does not move its state either (`isCounterattackAllowed` is the script's first test); its own spell bounced back at it is not an event of the script.
5. **A Doublecast of two weakness hits shifts once**: the first is ignored and the second handled (an aeon or Lulu with Doublecast can take two hits for one redraw).
6. **Press end to end**: 632, 591 and 425 off Tidus, Yuna and Lulu from full HP (half of current HP, exactly the note's), halved by Protect, never lethal, drawn at the right rate below the line (about 50 percent of its turns). **Defend halves it too**: Press and the Attack are physical class (the engine tests use an idle Potion turn and not Defend to read the undefended figures).
7. **The advisor's dry runs are safe**: the state lives in `state.flags`, which a forked context clones, so a preview never spends the live draw.
