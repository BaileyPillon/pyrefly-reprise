## 2. How it is built

- **Kernels** (`src/battle/ffx2/kernel/`, pure, no DOM, no `three`, deterministic; each takes a `draw(stream)` callback and draws only when and in the order the game does):
  - `confusion-pick.ts` (164 lines): the candidate filter (`0x00649240`: the blank entry, the three blocked bitfields, the Confusion bit, the Item sub-menu count) and the pick (`0x00649350`) in the game's order: candidates, draw 1 modulo the count, draw 2 modulo the usable sub-entries, the target set, draw 3 for the all-targets choice, the dispatcher.
  - `target-pick.ts` (146): the valid-target set (`0x0063f920` with the set builder `0x006117b0` and the filter `0x0063fa10`, mode 5: by the row's cursor flags, in the battle, not out, selectable, not hidden unless the row is far, alive unless the row may target the dead, not Ejected) and the dispatcher in its "for mode" form (`0x0063fc60` with the criterion picker `0x00634e40` and the team split `0x0063fba0`).
  - `item-rows.ts` (53): the 68 item rows' target word and misc word (numbers read from the game's item table).
- **Adapters** (`adapt/`): `confusion.ts` (144) turns the engine's units, the bag and the field into the kernel's world (the 31 slots as `Chr` fields, the stock as the game's id table) and the kernel's answer into one of our commands; `menu.ts#confusionLists` asks the menu kernel (lane X2-B, proven) for the two lists the game files for her worn dress and what she has learned; the lists are cached per (dress, what she has learned, level) because a Confused girl who finds nothing to pick asks every step.
- **Engine**: `statuses.ts#isConfusedGirl` (a party member with Confusion on, behind the `CONFUSION_PICK` switch in `constants.ts`); five sites follow it (`active.ts#awaitsPlayerInput` and `heldStillPending`, `atb-engine.ts#kernelUnit` feeding the ATB kernel's existing `confused` input so the girl enters state 7, `logic-step.ts#settleGauges` closing an open menu when Confusion lands, `engine.ts#nextDecision` and `tick`); `engineHooks.ts#runAutopilot` (Berserk's Attack, or the pick) replaces `runBerserkTurn` in `engine-core.ts#runAutopilotTurn`; `resolve.ts` builds the pool of an `exactTargets` command from the units it names that can be hit (the same pool for the one earlier user, the Paragon copy at the attacker alone), `simulate.ts` passes the flag for an item as for an ability.

## 3. Sources and the proof against the game

The pick was run for real before any engine code was written (the gate of the addendum: the row rested on a reading, "the candidate filter is proven [E 10,000]; the pick itself is [R] only").

- **The harness**: the live machine code of `FFX-2.exe` in the Unicorn emulator (`D:\Tools\ffx-parity\harness`), the random generator hooked and logged. One vector is one random battle world (a character slot, her two command lists, the party stock, the three blocked bitfields, the field state of all 31 slots, a command queue with 0 to 8 records) and one stream of random draws; the game's function and an independent model written from the disassembly alone must agree on the return value, every draw (which stream, in which order, how many), the command and the target mask that were queued, the all-targets byte, the item reservation and the queue count (scripts `D:\Tools\ffx-parity\rc9\x2-confusion\emu_pick.py`, `pick_model.py`).
- **Result**: 20,000 worlds on each of four seeds (101 to 104; seed 104 was re-run for this note) and the 20,000 of the fixture's pool: **0 mismatches** in all five. Coverage of the seed-104 run: a command queued (return 9) in 14,568 worlds and nothing queued (return 0) in 5,432; the Item command picked in 2,924 (each with its second draw), a direct skill in 11,537, the plain Attack in 2,124, no candidate in 3,008; a single target drawn on stream 4 in 11,338, a team split drawn on stream 5 in 3,303, a row with the all-targets choice in 667; a monster picking in 7,042 and a girl in 12,958; blocked commands in 12,084; an empty target set in 74; the player-side monster preamble in 407; a full queue in 1,957; the lists the game's own list writer made for the three girls in 5,996 (the menu fixture of lane X2-B).
- **Sensitivity of the proof** (`mutate_pick.py`, re-run for this note: 3,000 worlds, 28 deliberate faults in the model): **27 are caught** (from 6 to 2,587 worlds each: the stream of the first draw, the blocked bitfields, the Confusion bit, an empty sub-menu kept, the monster skip, the shortcut list, the modulo, the sub-pick index, the all-targets bit and when it is drawn, the two target streams, the dead, the hidden, the far flag, the not-selectable test, Eject, the team-split selector, the preamble, the full queue); the survivor `no_ptr0` is an equivalent mutant (a `Chr+0x0` test the set builder makes unreachable).
- **What was new beyond the note**: the note FFX2-G06 read "first draw modulo the candidates, a second modulo the usable sub-entries, a third for the all-targets bit" and that reading is right; it was incomplete on the target pick, which is now emulated too (`0x0063f920`, `0x0063fc60` and what they call: one draw of stream 4 for a single target, one of stream 5 for a team split).
- **The fixture** `tests/fixtures/parity/ffx2/confusion_pick.json` (460 vectors, 711 KB) is a stratified subset of the 20,000-world pool (seed 20261018): by who picks, what is picked, how many draws and on which streams, with and without a mask, one candidate or several. Every vector is the game's own answer (draws and the queued record); only the candidate list in it is model-derived. Worlds where the player-side monster preamble could fire or the queue is full are left out (the engine's part).

### 5.4 Why nothing moves on the shipped line (the causes)

No girl is Confused in any run of 5.1 and 5.2 (`seedsWithAGirlConfused` 0 in every row), so the new rule has nothing to act on. The causes, each checked:

1. **Only two enemy strikes in the data put Confusion on anyone.** A survey of every ability with a Confusion chance (`statusEffects` or the game record's status byte for slot 6) finds, on the enemy side, `paragon-attack-confuse` (Paragon's third normal attack, chance 120) and `x2-tawrich-bloody-breath` (chance 128, with Silence, Darkness and Poison at 254). Everything else that names Confusion is a cure (Esuna, Full Cure, White Wind, Remedy, the Highwind and Full Life lists) or an immunity (`confuse: 255` on Bahamut, Valefor, Vegnagun, Angra Mainyu and the Leblanc syndicate). Of the party's own abilities that inflict it, only the Dark Knight's Confuse can be aimed at a friend (Lady Luck's Tantalize and Full Throttle's Fright aim at enemies only); in the 6,000 chains of 5.2 no girl was Confused.
2. **Paragon's Confusing attack is not in the shipped chapter.** It belongs to the normal form of the Cloister Paragon (`cloisterParagonGroup`); Chapter XIII opens with the Oversoul form (`TREMA_PARAGON_FORM = 'oversoul'`, `cloisterParagonOversoulGroup`), whose moves (`paragon-os-*`) carry no Confusion. Measured over 20 seeds of the whole chain: 190 `paragon-os-attack`, 19 `paragon-os-final-impact`, 10 `paragon-os-thundaga`, a handful of Big Bang and Genesis, and **0** `paragon-attack-*`.
3. **Tawrich's Bloody Breath cannot land its Confusion on these girls.** In the Angra Mainyu chapter Tawrich (level 38) used it 76 times in 20 seeds: Silence, Darkness and Poison landed 136 times each and **Confusion 0 times**. The game's landing rule (`kernel/statusTypes.ts#statusLands`) is: lands when the chance byte is 255, or, unless the target's resist is 255, when the chance is 254 or when `roll < chance + 5 x (attacker level - target level) - resist` with the roll 0 to 100. With chance 128 against girls at level 66, 68 and 70 (the chapter's party-side raise of 20, `ANGRA_MAINYU_LEVEL_BONUS`) the right side is -12 or less, below every roll. At the Chapter V preset's own levels (46, 48, 50) it would be 88, 78 and 68: Confusion would land on 87, 77 and 67 percent of strikes. The raise is what keeps it out (section 5.5c measures that case).

## 6. Limits and decisions made on the way (each is a boundary or a stand-in, not a game number)

1. **The stock order.** The game's item table lists its slots in the order the items were first gained and the pick draws a slot modulo the usable ones; the engine lists the bag in the build's order. The draw reaches the same items with the same odds (uniform over the usable slots), not the same item on the same seed. The seed-level agreement is with the engine's own oracle, not with the game's save.
2. **The blocked bitfields** (`Chr+0x87c`, `+0x910`, `+0x9a8`) are written only by scripts the engine does not run: nothing is blocked (`blocked: () => false`). The kernel honours them (the vectors exercise them in 12,084 worlds).
3. **Monsters are not wired.** Who fills a monster's command lists is unread (G06 open question), so a Confused monster keeps running its script as before; the kernel already skips a monster's first list entry as the game does. A Confused monster's log is identical to an unconfused one's in the 12 seeds checked (section 4).
4. **The player-side monster preamble** (`0x00648e70`: a party slot whose save index is 15 to 22 queues its plain Attack before any of this) is not ported; the engine has no player-side monsters.
5. **The queue add and the item reservation** are the engine's `performCommand`, as for any command. On this base (`r49-rc`) the RC8 item ledger (lane X2-F, XF1) is not present, so an item pick is spent when it lands; once XF1 lands the pick is reserved with no further change (the merge check above passes with it present).
6. **An empty target set** (a row nobody can be aimed at): the game's pick draws what it draws up to that point and queues nothing; the engine does the same (the draws are spent, `confusedTurn` returns null, she goes back to idle). Without any candidate it draws nothing.
7. **A move of the game's that our data cannot run** is passed on as a Defend (the turn is spent doing nothing, no draw lost). None of the 14 dresses at the new-game lists hits it: every candidate maps to an ability of the build (checked for all 14 dresses).
8. **A command held for a chain lock** (Active ATB only) is dropped when the girl turns Confused, as it already is for Berserk (`heldStillPending`). The note says applying Confusion "closes the menu only", so the game keeps a queued command; the engine's held command is a model of the chain lock, not a queue entry, and the lock needs Active mode, human pace and a Confusion landing in the same second. No shipped line can meet it (section 5.4). Kept as the engine's reading; pinned by a test.
9. **Spare Change and Bribe.** A Confused Samurai can pick Spare Change (`x2-samurai-spare-change`); the pick queues no gil amount and the engine, which has no amount picker (lane X2-F, XF2), takes the amount as 0 (the command is "free" and throws no gil). What the game does with the amount of a Confused girl's Spare Change is unread.
10. **A hidden target.** The kernel lets a far row (flag 0x400) aim at a hidden character as the game does; the engine's targeting never hits a hidden one (`isTargetable`), so such a command would land on nobody. No unit in the data carries the `hidden` flag today (nothing sets it), so it cannot arise.
11. **Confusion's clock** is the existing one (the status clock process: a timed status falling by the raw speed, 70.5 seconds by default); nothing here changes it.
12. **Items without a game row.** 16 of the 40 battle items in the data have an effect with no game row id yet (Dispel Tonic, Chocobo Wing, the two Hourglasses, Star Curtain, Candle of Life, Farplane Shadow, Poison Fang, Budget Grenade, the gems, Dark Matter, Stamina Spring, L-Bomb, Twin Stars), so they never become a candidate. None is in any shipped chapter's bag (checked for all twelve: every usable item in every bag has a game row), so none is dropped today.
13. **The start gate's usability check.** The game's candidate filter does not look at MP, HP, Silence or Itch, and the start gate (`0x006314C0`, note G06 3.6) then refuses a command whose usability result is negative (a cost she cannot pay, Silence on a silenceable skill, Itch): the turn is lost. The engine's pick checks none of them either and resolves the command, spending the MP she has (a Black Mage at 0 MP still casts MP Absorb; a scratch fuzz over all 14 dresses, 8 extra statuses on the girl (none, Silence, Itch, Darkness, Haste, Slow, Poison, Berserk) and 30 seeds, a third of them at 0 MP: 3,360 runs, 15,136 Confused actions, no exception and no menu while she stood Confused; an earlier pass at full and zero MP found every pick to be one of her listed commands or an item). Not modelled: it needs the usability answer at the gate and the game's time cost of a refused command (unread). The shipped menu never offers an unaffordable command, so only the Confusion pick (and no shipped line) can reach it.
14. **No screen.** A Confused girl simply acts; the HUD draws her actions as it draws a Berserked girl's. Whether the battle banner or the status chip should say more is a screen choice (rule 9), not made here.
