## 8. CHANGELOG paragraph (FFX-2 only; for the release driver to paste; lanes never write CHANGELOG.md)

- **FFX-2, how long an action takes (provisional):** every command now holds its user for the time its own animation takes, taken from the game's files, instead of taking no time or, in six fights, a flat three seconds. A girl's Attack is the wait for her idle pose to break, the run to the target and the swing up to the blow (about two seconds); a spell, an item or a special is the effect from the moment it is released to its end. Three lengths were measured in the real game (Fire 75 steps, an item 80, Darkness 83; a step is 1/30 of a second) and the frame each plain Attack lands on was read from the game's own data (Yuna 12, Rikku 4, Paine 11; Nooj 17, Trema 6, Tawrich 34, Bahamut 41, Leblanc 8, the Oversoul Paragon 33). **Still estimated:** Cure, a Hasted Fire, Angra Mainyu's Full-Life and Tawrich's Punch (they use the nearest measured length), the run to the target, and how long a cast waits in the game's queue. A boss that casts now holds itself longer than a girl's action holds her, so the girls get more turns between a boss's moves: the first-try win counts of 500 (Wait pace, Active, bench) are Vegnagun and Shuyin 463 -> 490, 417 -> 459, 482 -> 489; Leblanc 500 -> 500, 472 -> 497, 500 -> 500; Trema 288 -> 357, 167 -> 302, 336 -> 363; Tourist Trap 423 -> 481, 339 -> 460, 464 -> 484; Angra Mainyu 383 -> 491, 24 -> 348, 447 -> 500; and the Den of Woe 498 -> 482, 452 -> 487, 499 -> 499. **Den of Woe:** with actions taking time, the two Dark Knights are still in their Darkness cast when Nooj's Lightfall (5,000 to everyone) lands, so the suggested line now drinks its Hero Drink when Nooj is down to about half his health (12,000 HP, not 4,500), the guide and the chapter tip say so, and the fight stays level at the bench and Active paces and 3 percent harder at the Wait pace. **This replaces six of Bailey's 3-second picks** (Fallen Aeons' Road links D-187 and D-197, Trema's Cloister links D-151, Djose Ixion D-269, Tourist Trap and Valefor at Besaid D-846, Angra Mainyu D-857), adopted as a delegated pick under his advance yes ("I'll go with all of your recommendations"); he can veto it, and it is one line (`ACTION_TIME_PER_COMMAND`) plus the Den of Woe constant.
