## What the paintings are
Made 2026-11-02 and 03 (`D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-11-02/art-poses/set/`, its `MANIFEST.md`): every pose of the five bosses is a Method W warp of its idle (the idle's own pixels, a part turned about a joint, no repaint, the way the approved Paragon cast was made), binary alpha, a sidecar each; the @2x masters are RealESRGAN with the colour drift removed; the plates are composites of the approved backdrops and the idles; the glyph is procedural, the rock procedural plus one painterly pass. 43 images, 38,242,254 bytes, plus 39 sidecars. ## Registration (hard rule 16)
`tools/posescale/subjects.json` gained the five subjects (`game: foe`; the four FFX-2 ones `rowFromBaseline`; the Keeper reads its soles from the silhouette because its attack's thin talons hang 4 percent below the claw), `overrides.json` the `standing: true` rows of the paintings wider than tall (as Paragon's) and the hand stances below, `reviews.json` the Keeper hurt's scale 1.02 (stature 0.891 to 0.91, over the 0.90 floor). `docs/target/pose-measure.json` and `src/data/art/poseRegistrationFoes.ts` were regenerated with the repo's rig (`measure.py measure --write`, `table`, the stamp) on a rehearsal art folder (the release mirror hard-linked plus the staged subjects): only additions. Result with the art in place: **22 poses, 18 ok, 4 waived, 0 blocked; the full gate over all 1,128 paintings: 847 pass, 83 waived, 198 exempt, 0 fail.**

**Four waivers** (`docs/target/pose-waivers.json`, source D-1444, the driver's call under delegation, his to overturn): Keeper attack and Aranea attack, cast, hurt. The poses are the idle's own pixels, so the true stance of a pose is the idle's plus the offset its sidecar records. The gate's model reads the stance off the pixels instead, and in these four a raised blade (Keeper) or a lifted front leg (Aranea) leaves the ground-contact band, so the reading jumps (Aranea 144 px, Keeper 210 px to the one claw that is left) and the gate says `feet-x` 0.21 (and, for the Keeper, `feet-sunk`). Registered by the offset the idle-to-pose overlap is 0.46 (Keeper attack) and 0.79 to 0.81 (Aranea); registered by the reading it would be 0.07 and 0.38, a double image at the swap (CHK-027 calls 0.2 or under one). Chac's offsets agree with its readings to 14 px, so its hand stances pass without a waiver. To undo: `stage/overrides-proposed.json` in the install folder carries every number and the steps; the Keeper attack stays blocked either way (`feet-y` 0.03) and needs a waiver or a repaint.

## Code that went with it (all inert where the art is not installed)
- **`FloorDecal` in `src/engine/PartAnchors.ts` (FFX only; no other chapter has a `decal`):** a floor ring may name a painting per state (unlit, occupied, mined). `PartRings` takes an injected loader (`BattlePresenterStage.ts` passes `tryLoadTexture(artUrl(path))`), lays a flat plane under the ring once the plain picture has loaded and hides the ring; a missing file, a failed load or no loader leaves the ring exactly as it was. The picture follows the battle state (`decalStateOf`, pure): the mine's while `plat.armed.<circle>` is up, else the occupied one while a member's `plat.at.<member>` equals the platform, else the plain one; a decal with no mine picture (the rock) brings the amber ring back when armed. `src/scenes/spectral-keeper.ts` names the glyph for platforms 1 to 6 and the rock for 7; the ids and paths are in `src/data/ffx/spectral-keeper-ids.ts`.
- `chapter-meta-spectral-keeper.ts` and `chapter-meta-ffx2-fallen-maesters.ts`: the stand-in snapshots (Yunalesca's idle, Paragon's idle) became the Keeper's and Aranea's own idles; the comments say the plates exist. `thumbnailKey` of the Keeper is its own (`chapter-spectral-keeper`; a key no code loads). The sprite keys already named the files (`characters/spectral-keeper`, `x2-aranea` ...): no code change was needed for the figures, and a tree without the art draws the presenter's fallback figure as before.
- Tests: `tests/unit/chapters/boss-art-r75.test.ts` (every file the two chapters name is a pinned file of the set; every pinned pose has a registration row stamped with the pinned painting's sha; the four waivers; and, where the art is installed or `BOSS_ART_ROOT` names a folder, every pin's sha256 and every sidecar) and `tests/unit/engine/part-anchors-decal.test.ts` (15: the states, the fade, the fallbacks, the race with a removed ring, the Keeper's anchors, the flag prefixes against the battle layer's).
