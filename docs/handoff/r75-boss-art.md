**Three waivers** (`docs/target/pose-waivers.json`, source D-1444, the driver's call under delegation, his to overturn): Aranea attack, cast and hurt. The poses are the idle's own pixels, so the true stance of a pose is the idle's plus the offset its sidecar records. The gate's model reads the stance off the pixels instead, and in these a lifted front leg leaves the ground-contact band, so the reading jumps (144 px) and the gate says `feet-x` 0.21. Registered by the offset the idle-to-pose overlap is 0.79 to 0.81; registered by the reading it would be 0.38, a double image at the swap (CHK-027 calls 0.2 or under one). Chac's offsets agree with its readings to 14 px, so its hand stances pass without a waiver. To undo: `stage/overrides-proposed.json` in the install folder carries every number and the steps. (The fourth waiver, the Keeper attack's, went out with the painting, 219u: the pose was blocked either way, `feet-y` 0.03, and its overlap stays under 0.5 with the waiver and under 0.1 without; the rows are in commit f714c7435 if a repaint brings it back.)

## Not wired, and why (open questions for the driver are in the fix report)
1. **Concherer's cracked-shell idle** is registered and listed in the manifest but nothing draws it. Searched for a mechanism that swaps an enemy's idle on an event: (a) `form-change` events swap an enemy's whole art id (`BattlePresenterBeats.formChange` to `stage.setArt`: Yunalesca's `yunalesca-1/2/3`, Braska's final aeon, FF7's guard scorpion), with a flash, a shake, a roar, objectives and story triggers attached; (b) Sin's states (`BattleScreenSinPoses.followSinPoses`) swap the resting painting from `state.flags`, attached to the airship screen only. The Fallen Maesters engine emits neither: at the break Concherer's script emits **one system `message`** ("Concherer's shell breaks!") and sets `aiMemory.conchererShellBroken` on the unit; no `form-change`, no status, no flag in the battle state. Wiring (a) means the engine emitting a `form-change` for a pose that is not an art id (a new subject folder, and the roar and flash), wiring (b) means a follower attached to the chapter-30 screen reading the unit's memory: each is new presentation, not an existing hook, so it stays unwired.
2. **The Black Elemental stands on its baseline.** The sidecar's only lift is `anchorY` (a ground-line row, clamped into the image: the most it can lift the figure is the painting's 16 px bottom margin, 1.9 percent of its height); nothing passes an enemy `hover` to the battle stage (`PaintedActorBase` takes it from callers that build their own actors). A quarter of its height needs either a new sidecar field (and `SIDECAR_FIELDS`, its reader guard and `PaintedArt`) or a repaint on a taller canvas.
3. **The glyph's number (1 to 6)** is not drawn.
4. The card and thumbnail are read by no code until the board lists the chapters.
5. The Keeper tile's `ipd` (0.17) is an estimate.
6. **The Keeper's attack** is held back (above); its physical moves draw the cast painting until a repaint.
