Branch `perf-trim` from `origin/r63-int` (7ded72b57), tree `D:/pyrefly-anim-proto` (dev port 5374, stopped). Nothing deploys. **Game case: BOTH games for the plumbing (the profiler, the governor, the skipped copy passes, the breathing-field read-ahead); every change acts in Chapter I (`seymour-flux`, FFX) and Chapter IV (`ffx2-bahamut`, FFX-2) only, behind the pro lanes (the governor) or pixel-identically (the skipped passes); the aeon victory frame is FFX only (Chapter I): the sources withhold FFX-2's victory pose.** Every other chapter, screen and the address with no key at all are release 62; `?pro=off`, `?govern=off` and the master key `0` still give the r63-int frame (the levers rest at 1; pinned by `ident2-ch1` and `ident2-ch4`: the canvas at a frozen frame is the same FNV with the governor off, held at level 0, and back at 0).

Bailey's words (2026-10-23): "The motion and animations need to be top notch, even above visuals." His pick of the same day (item 195c): trim the frame cost of the integrated build before tonight so the 120 Hz screen does not stutter, the most expensive extra first, "a soft background if the frame rate holds", measured before and after.

## The short of it

1. **What costs what (Chapter I, 2560x1440, GPU time per composer pass, everything on):** the scene pass with its supersample 40 to 44 % (about half in Chapter IV), the look's compound pass 22 to 27 %, bloom 8 to 10 %, the bold pass 7 to 10 %, the two blur passes 10 to 12 %, sharpening 1 to 3 %, the grade 1 to 2 %. **The three pro lanes add no steady-state frame cost that this harness can see**, in either chapter: everything on against everything off, paired inside each round, is +0.7 ms GPU in Chapter I (`fin`; -0.6 in `ab2`) and 0.0 to +0.5 in Chapter IV, with a spread between rounds of 1 to 2 ms (tables below). The integration lane's gap (p95 11.3 ms against 6.8) is a tail effect; the one ON-only main-thread cost I found is the living paintings' alpha read at each pose's first sight (`getImageData`, 769 ms in 21 s of ON, 0 in OFF), which is now read ahead behind the title for every picture the cache holds (it still happens for the few runtime-built canvases, about 2 per fight).
2. **The extras Bailey asked to make conditional are almost free; the one lever that moves the number is the scene's size.** Holding each level in one session (3 interleaved rounds each): level 1 (soft background off) and level 2 (particles halved) change the GPU time by 0 to 0.5 ms (Chapter IV: -0.5 ms for level 1, the follow camera's wider blur); level 3 (the supersample times 0.8) takes **-1.1 ms (-17 %) in Chapter I and -2.1 ms (-24 %) in Chapter IV** and turns 25.8 % of frames over 8.33 ms into 14.6 % (I) and 32.0 % into 17.6 % (IV).
3. **The governor** (`src/engine/governor/`) takes the three levels in Bailey's order when the display misses its refresh and gives them back by probing; with the frame rate holding it does nothing and the frame is r63-int's, bit for bit (pixel check at a frozen frame). It also fixed two things on the way: the two copy-only tilt-shift passes (pixel-identical, 0.1 ms), and the breathing-field read.
4. **The aeon victory** frames the aeon whole, authored and labelled [ours] (the sources give no aeon victory shot); below, "The aeon's victory frame".

## What was found (attribution)

**Method.** One browser session per run; the configurations are flipped live in the page by the switchboard model's own setters (the code the keys run) and interleaved (A/B/A/B; the Latin-square order in the final runs, so no configuration is always first), so the shared machine's drift hits both sides. Headless Chromium on the real GPU (RTX 5070 Ti, shared with other lanes' jobs), 2560x1440 at device pixel ratio 1, uncapped (`--disable-frame-rate-limit --disable-gpu-vsync`), the fight played by the game's own `intended` strategy with every combatant at 900,000 HP, seed 11, the demo warm-up waited for (as at the booth, where the title stands while the stages build), the first 1.5 s after a flip skipped (6 s in the runs that include the governor, which needs about 4 s to settle). Per pass GPU time from `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` (it works in headless Chromium here); per system CPU from `performance.now` around the call; per function CPU from the CDP profiler (250 microseconds); draw calls from `renderer.info`. A frame interval or step over 50 ms is a load stall (art decode, a shader link, the GPU queue on a shared GPU): counted apart, never in a percentile. The tool is `__pyrefly.perf` (`src/debug/perfApi.ts`: `start`, `mark`, `data`, `stop`, `gpu(on)`, `burn(n)`; nothing wraps until `start()`, `stop()` puts the originals back). **The GPU timer's absolute numbers are inflated on a shared GPU (the pass sum is 5 to 9 ms while frames are issued every 3 to 6 ms): read shares and pairs, not absolutes.**

## The governor

`src/engine/governor/` (pure `FrameBudget.ts`; the lever cells `GovernorLive.ts`; the host `DemoGovernor.ts`, ticked once a frame from `LookRig.update`, so it adds no hook anywhere else). It watches the 95th percentile of the frame interval over about 2 s (not less than 60 frames, and a full window) against the display's budget: 8.33 ms for 120 Hz, or what the page's own frame probe (`eyeCandy.probe`: the last 600 intervals, the title and the picker included) shows at battle start, snapped to 8.33 / 11.11 / 13.33 / 16.67 ms so a 60 Hz screen is not trimmed for missing 120 Hz; `?govbudget=<ms>` names it by hand. Over budget = p95 above 1.2 times the budget. Levels, in Bailey's order (the extra he named first):

| level | what goes | lever | how it fades |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the camera's soft background | `govLive.soft` 1 to 0, a multiplier on the shot's weight (`ProBand.softScaled`, read in `LookRig.frame`) | 450 ms for the whole range |
| 2 | the particles halved: the new effects' ink drops, sparks and dust (`FxDrawList.dens`), the item toss's sparks, the hit sparks' dial | `govLive.particles` 1 to 0.5; the lesser of it and the effects guard's own share (they do not stack) | 450 ms for the whole range |
| 3 | the scene drawn a fifth smaller (the supersample 0.8 times, `CrispRig.setGovernorCut`; it multiplies with the effects guard's own cut and keeps its 1.05 floor) | `govLive.heavy` 1 to 0.8 | one render-target resize, only after levels 1 and 2 have settled: the one step that is not a fade (a size is a size) |

**Level 3 is not the brief's "bold and blur passes at half resolution": changed on the data.** The blur and bold passes are 17 to 20 % of the GPU frame, the scene's supersample 41 to 50 %; halving the blur's resolution needs a new composite pass and saves about 0.5 ms, cutting the supersample a fifth is one existing, tested, guard-owned call and saves 1.1 to 2.1 ms. If the driver wants the brief's version, it is a new pass (half-size blur targets, a full-size composite that keeps the figures' own pixels), not built.

A step from rest needs a full window over budget; once in trouble each next level needs half a window (about a second: the saving is in the last level, so a stutter should not be sat through for four seconds on the way to it). Recovery is a probe: after 8 s inside the budget (with a margin) one level comes back; a step down again within 6 s of a recovery doubles the wait before the next probe (up to 96 s), so a machine that truly cannot hold the full look settles on the reduced one and stops probing. A new scene, a stall over 250 ms (a tab coming back, a shader compile) and the step's own hitch are not readings. It runs only in the two demo chapters while the pro look or the pro camera is on (`?govern=off` never, `?govern=on` always); with the lanes off, or when the battle is left, every lever goes to 1 at once and the consumers multiply by 1. REDUCE MOTION and LOW EFFECTS are multiplied into, never overridden. `__pyrefly.govern()` reports it (mode, levers, the governor's last p95, its log of steps); `__pyrefly.govern(level)` holds a level (`null` lets it decide); `.enable(false)` and `.tilt(false)` are the measuring switches.

## Not done / for the driver

- **Level 3 is not a fade.** It is one render-target resize (about a frame) and a slightly softer edge (supersample 1.6 asked, 1.28 with the effects guard's own cut); it only happens when the display has missed its refresh for about 4 s. It could be held back to the next shot cut or the next menu to hide the frame; not built. **Open for the driver:** the data say level 3 is the lever and levels 1 and 2 are almost free, so the order Bailey picked costs about 2.6 s of stutter before the lever that works; I shortened the dwell (half a window once in trouble) and kept his order. Putting the scene's size first is a one-line change of `LEVEL_TARGETS`.
- **The brief's version of level 3** (the look's bold and blur passes at half resolution) is not built: the passes are 17 to 20 % of the GPU frame and the saving about 0.5 ms (a half-size blur and a new full-size composite that keeps the figures' pixels); the supersample is 41 to 50 % and one existing call.
- **The p95 target (at or under 8.3 ms) is not met in a busy moment of this machine** (11.5 ms in Chapter I, base 13.4); it is met in a calm one (every configuration 5.5 to 7.7 ms). The harness cannot be vsynced at 120 Hz headless: the real display (a 120 Hz monitor, G-Sync or not) was not run; the governor's quantisation argument (a display that waits for its refresh shows 8.33 or 16.7 ms, so a p95 over 10 ms means one frame in twenty missed) is by design, not by a run on such a display. **Run it once on the booth's own screen** (`?govern=on` shows the levels in `__pyrefly.govern()`).
- **Hitches** (frames of 50 ms or more: 9 to 30 a minute in every configuration of the uncapped harness, mostly inside `renderer.render`, the GPU queue on a shared GPU) are not the extras and are not fixed here; whether they show at the booth depends on whether the booth PC's GPU is shared.
- **The breathing-field read** is still done at the first sight of the few runtime-built canvases (about two a fight; `getImageData` of a GPU-held canvas waits for the GPU queue); `LookBold.extentsOf` does the same per texture (0.1 ms a frame, in both states).
- **The HUD's per-frame DOM reads** (`getBoundingClientRect`, `querySelectorAll` in `hudPanels.menuOpen`, `EnemyIntent.layout`, `placeAdvisor`, `hudScale`, `DamageNumbers.update`; about 0.5 ms a frame, a tenth of the main thread, in both states) are the HUD lane's files; a cache keyed on the layout's version would take most of it.
- **A downed party member's `KO` chip lands on the aeon's feet** while the party is off stage (pro-look markers: `ProFieldMarks.update` projects the hidden actor's feet); seen in a steered fight, not in the pad run. The pro-look lane's file; the fix is to skip a mark whose actor is not staged.
- **An aeon's killing-blow shot** is still the Tidus finisher laid on the aeon (`finisher.run.tidus ... for ifrit`): not looked at, outside item 4.
- **Not run:** a real 120 Hz display, 1920x1080 and 1280x720, REDUCE MOTION and LOW EFFECTS in the governed build in a browser (the levers are multipliers; the unit tests cover the arithmetic), the touch layout, Chapters II and V, the full `npm test` (the families above only).
