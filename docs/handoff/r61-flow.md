## What a visitor sees

1. **Title** (The Echo or the Farplane painting, whichever is set): a small ivory QR plate in the top right corner ("Play it yourself / echoesofspira.com"). Start (Enter, or the pad's Start or Cross).
2. **The fight picker** ("Choose your fight"): the chapter board's own cards, plates, dossier and controls strip with exactly two cards, FINAL FANTASY X: Chapter I, Seymour Flux (Mt. Gagazet), FINAL FANTASY X-2: Chapter IV, Bahamut (Bevelle Underground). Left/Right or Up/Down (the D-pad) cross between them, Enter (Cross) fights, Esc (Circle) goes back to the title. The strip is worded per device (`LEFT/RIGHT CHOOSE · ENTER FIGHT · ESC TITLE`, `D-PAD CHOOSE · CROSS FIGHT · CIRCLE TITLE`). The QR plate is in the bottom right corner.
3. **Straight into the fight.** No party prep and no story scenes before or after (`demoRunDefaults`). Why prep is skipped rather than kept as one confirm: prep is a full menu (Party, Sphere Grid, Equipment, Items, Overdrive; the Garment Grid for FFX-2) and the picker's confirm already is the one decision; a stranger on a pad should be in a fight within seconds, not in a menu. Measured (dev server, real GPU, 2560x1440): Enter on the title, the picker 1.2 s later, **the first command menu 19 s after the first Enter** for Seymour (13 s to 25 s over several runs) and 21 s after the picker's Cross for Bahamut; a production build loads faster than the dev server.
4. **The cleaner HUD:** the command menu, the HP/MP plates (and the turn list, as always) and the advisor's **one-row tip** ("NEXT  Tidus  Hastega → the party  IN WHITE MAGIC  30 MP"); the strategy guide and the enemy-move panel start folded to their chips (G and E open them, N the advisor). Nothing a visitor toggles is written to the save.
5. **Results**, then **continue** returns to the picker with the cursor on the OTHER fight, so the next visitor's first press starts the other one. The results panel has a strip worded per device (Cross / Enter, "continue").
6. **Reset** (hold **Home**, or **Select + Start** on a pad, for 1.5 s; from any screen): a ring fills at the top of the screen, and the game returns to the title for the next visitor with the previous visitor's run gone and every setting (the title-art pick among them) kept.
7. **Attract:** 60 s with no key, click, touch, wheel, real mouse movement or pad button on the title starts a self-playing reel of the real game; any of those inputs ends it and shows the title again.

### Why the reset reloads the page

Holding the reset key clears the run, fades the music and the picture to black (280 ms), halts the loop and reloads `location`. A soft jump to the title would have to unwind a battle, its presenter, timers and audio graph, an open pause menu, a story scene or a half-loaded battle, each through its own path; a reload stops all of them at once, and a booth tab runs a whole day (a hundred visitors), so a reset that also hands back every texture and shader program is worth its two seconds. `?demo=1` is kept in `sessionStorage`, so the page comes back in demo mode, on the title. The booth kit's own `F5` recovery (`docs/BOOTH-SCRIPT.md`) does the same without clearing the run; reset-demo.cmd wipes the whole profile; this is the in-page, no-keyboard-needed one.

### The attract reel's lengths (measured; constants in `attractPlan()`)

Measured in the browser (headless Chromium on the real GPU, 2560x1440, dev server, the machine loaded by two other lanes; the clip is `evidence/21-attract-loop-clip.mp4`):

| Step | What | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Idle on the title | the first segment's art loads while the title is still up | the reel's first frame at 60 s of idle plus the load: 62.8, 62.9, 66.0, 69.0 s over four runs |
| Seymour Flux (FFX) | the real fight on seed 7, the chapter's own line, normal speed, 40 s cap | 44 s from fade to fade (the cap plus the fade and the load) |
| Bahamut (FFX-2) | seed 56: 12 s at normal speed, then the presenter's **skip** speed to the frame the count opens (`bahamutCountdown` leaves 0; log events 51 to 882 in 10 s of skip, no overshoot: at most 35 events in a frame), the count 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 at **fast**, the Mega Flare at normal speed, ends 7 s after the cast starts | cast at 47.5 s into the segment (log event 1173 of about 1240), segment 55 s |
| One lap | | about 100 s plus the fades |
| Wake | a pad button or a key | the title again in 0.3 to 1.8 s (0.32 s with the machine quiet, 1.6 to 1.8 s under load and a recording); a press while the first segment's art is still loading (the title has not gone yet) leaves the title up and restarts the 60 s count |

Why Bahamut is 55 s and not 40: the Mega Flare comes at the END of the fight (engine event 1170 of 1240, about 85 s of engine time) and ends a 25 s count on the engine's own clock, which the presenter's fast speed does not shorten. Playing it from the start at normal speed took more than two minutes to reach the cast (measured: 5 to 4 took 25 s at normal, 10 s at fast). The reel therefore jumps (skip) over the middle, which is a visible time-lapse: the HP bars and Bahamut's gauge step down, the girls change dresses, and the "BAHAMUT 5" banner is up when it ends (`evidence/20-bahamut-after-skip-jump-count-opens.png`). If Bailey wants it shorter, `attractPlan()` is the one place: drop the count to skip as well (about 20 s less, the numbers would flash by), or end the segment at the count (about 25 s).

The reel never writes: `tests/unit/demo-attract.test.ts` drives the loop against a save that records any write; in the browser a storage spy over the idle count, the reel and the wake saw **no write at all** (0 `localStorage` writes, 0 `sessionStorage` writes; the save's raw JSON equal before and after). An earlier run saw 6,208 `sessionStorage` writes of the tab's `pyrefly.demo` flag in 140 s: `demoModeOn()` (release 60) rewrote it on every call and the HUD's one-row switch called it every frame; the flag is written once now and the HUDs read the switch once, when they are built.

## What is still open

- **Bahamut's reel is 55 s, not 40**, because it reaches the Mega Flare (above). Bailey's text was "about 40 s each, ideally reaching the Mega Flare"; the cast cannot be reached in 40 s of real time and the middle is run at skip speed. Lengths are constants in `attractPlan()`.
- **Nothing here was heard.** The music is lowered to 40 percent of its setting (`ATTRACT_MUSIC`) and restored after, which the unit test pins; whether 40 percent is right in a loud hall is Bailey's ear (hard rule 13). The skip jump plays no extra sound that was noticed in the recording, but nobody listened.
- **A real pad:** the pad path is the game's own (`navigator.getGamepads`), driven by a stub. A first run on a physical pad is worth two minutes at the booth: every button named here is a standard-mapping index (Cross 0, Circle 1, Select 8, Start 9, D-pad 12 to 15). On an Xbox pad the strips still say Cross and Circle (the whole game's wording).
- **An idle picker or results panel does nothing:** the brief puts the attract on the title only. A visitor who walks away from a results panel leaves it up until someone holds the reset key (or Esc on the picker). Easy to add (idle on the picker for N minutes goes to the title) if wanted.
- **The reset reloads the page**: outside the kiosk launcher (which lets sound play without a click) the title's music starts on the first press after a reset, as on any fresh load.
- **No production build was made** (disk). `vite` served everything in these runs; `tsc` is clean. The bundle gains about 16 KB of source (the QR encoder, the demo screens and `demo.css`), loaded in every build, inert without `?demo=1`.
