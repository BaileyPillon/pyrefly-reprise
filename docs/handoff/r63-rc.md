## R63-P1: the phone gate (the focused review's major, 2026-10-24; both games)

The focused review of `8ee3ca11b` held the ship on R63-P1: at 390 x 844 the pack (on by default in Chapters I and IV) drew the F1 HUD over the phone layout, so PAUSE was gone, the command names read ATTA... / SPE..., and the party numbers overlapped. **Case: both games** (FFX Chapter I and FFX-2 Chapter IV fail alike; the fix is the shared gate in `engine/motion/DemoGate.ts`).

**The gate.** `packDemo()` answers the default (no `pack` word) through `packDefaultOn()` = `PACK_DEFAULT_ON && packScreen() === 'desk'`. `readPackScreen()` says `phone` when the page is 599 px wide or less (the game's own phone breakpoint, `phoneBattle.ts` PHONE_BATTLE_QUERY and party prep), or when the primary pointer is coarse (`(pointer: coarse)`) on a screen whose short side is 1024 px or less (a phone held either way, any tablet); `desk` otherwise and wherever nothing can be read (every unit test, tools). `?pack=demo` forces the pack on anywhere (the booth address); `?pack=off` forces it off; each lane's own word still wins for its piece. **Phone means every lane off** (the whole pack is behind the one reader), so a phone plays release 62: the sound pass, the look, the camera and the booth screens too, not the HUD alone.

**Decided at load, once** (`screenClass` is read when `DemoGate.ts` is evaluated, the page's first script): turning a phone sideways, resizing a desktop window or a pointer appearing never swaps the HUD mid-fight; a reload decides again. The reverse edge is open and small: a desktop window that was wide at load and is then dragged under 600 px gets the game's phone layout (a live CSS rule) on top of the F1 HUD, as the review saw it; nobody plays that way.

**Tablets and a phone held sideways fall on the phone side, on purpose.** The same check at 768 x 1024 and 844 x 390 showed the pack has no PAUSE button there either (its key hint names P), so a touch player could not pause; with the gate they get release 62's HUD with PAUSE 96 x 24. A 1920 x 1080 or 2560 x 1440 touch monitor (a booth kiosk) stays desk. Open for the driver: a bare `?demo=1` (without `pack=demo`) on a touch-first screen of 1024 px or less goes off; the booth address in NOW.md is `?demo=1&pack=demo`, which is on everywhere.
