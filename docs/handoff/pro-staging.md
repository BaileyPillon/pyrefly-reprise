Lane "pro-staging" of the conference-demo push (build Friday and Saturday, freeze Sunday night, demo Monday 2026-10-26). Branch `pro-staging` from `origin/playtest-1022` (763086fcb). Presentation only: no rule, number, timing of a hit or save changes; nothing deploys.

Bailey 2026-10-22: "I kind of want to take the ffx/ffx-2 that I know and love and put an expedition 33 kind of spin on it." His picks that bind this lane (from the full-resolution phone mockups, options book `D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-10-22/options-book/`): boss big moves **Held breath, full painting**; win **Victory freeze frame**; loss **quick retry**; the Overdrive opening **Expedition 33-style** (a small name; the camera, timing and light carry the moment) for FFX, and FFX-2's **ribbon-spiral Change kept with a readable pearl name plate**. The HUD panels are being redesigned apart: nothing here restyles one (the one thing done to the HUD is its opacity while a staged beat lives).

## The switch and the key

| | |
|---|---|
| Address | `?staging=pro` or `?pack=demo` (the pack turns it on; `?staging=today` wins over the pack for A/B). Default OFF. |
| Per piece | `?stagingoff=breath,card,ending,opening` takes the named pieces back to today's. |
| Switchboard key | `\` (backslash): `pro` / `today` (`?pack=demo&switchboard=1`). A flip in a fight plays from the next beat: the layer is built as a fight begins (it is also built when the board is up with the switch at today's). The master key `0` sets it with the rest. Backslash was chosen as the key least likely to be taken by another lane: the board's key-list pin in `tests/unit/engine/demo-motion-int.test.ts` takes it. |
| Scope | Chapter I (`seymour-flux`, FFX) and Chapter IV (`ffx2-bahamut`, FFX-2) only (`engine/motion/DemoGate.ts`). Every other chapter is untouched whatever the address says. |
| Debug | `__pyrefly.staging('pro' | 'today')`, `.stagingOn()`, `.stagingPiece(piece, on)`, `.stagingState()`, `.stagingCue(piece, cue)`, `.stagingScrub({...})` (the held breath at an exact time), `.stagingAdvance(ms)` / `.stagingRelease()` (hold the layer's clock and step it: any piece at an exact time). |

## The four pieces: what they do, the game case, what is sourced and what is [ours]

Everything below is `[ours]` unless a source is named: the options book said so (the sources give a move no staging); the TIMES of every move are the game's own and no piece adds a millisecond to one (the tell is the lead-in the effect already has; the freeze is half a second added to a win, the Overdrive opening is 0.35 s shorter than today's banner).

1. **Held breath (breath)**, Total Annihilation (FFX only, Chapter I) and Mega Flare (FFX-2 only, Chapter IV). The ids are the engine's (`total-annihilation`, `mega-flare`; `x2-bahamut-mega-flare` is the same move); `bigMoveOf` never names the other game's (FFX shares `mega-flare` for the aeon Bahamut's Overdrive: never staged).
   - MARK (FFX: the mount's `Ready To Annihilate` caption, stage 2 of its ladder, a turn ahead; FFX-2: the countdown number `2`, or the move's own `action-start`; both read from the engines, `seymour-flux-rules.ts#announceCharge` and `bahamut.ts#countdownTurn`): a small name and a ring on the floor under the boss, the look's rim gain up (`bold.rim`, a look tweak, restored at the lift).
   - TELL (the first blow's effect starts; its length is the effect's own lead-in `vfx.land` says, 0.45 to 1.3 s): the arena drains toward ivory (FFX) or pale lilac (FFX-2) with windows cut for the party and the attacker (the colour kept), the ring gathers inward, light flows into the boss in motes, the HUD sinks to a sixth; the telegraph's text slab (`MomentOverlay.nameSlab`, `kind: telegraph`) and the splash cut-in (option C6's big banner) are retired for these two moves.
   - BLOW (the effect's mark lands: the same beat feel's hit-stop, punch and rumble fire on; the staging claims nothing and stops nothing): the core flashes, the ring pulses; Mega Flare's flood opens from the chest and leaves a window around each girl (the engine's own white-out would cover them: `flare.white` is set to 0 for the move, restored at the lift); the wash holds a hair under full through the rounds.
   - LIFT (the staged move's own action ends, or the boss falls; the boss's other moves between the countdown numbers do not lift it): the wash lifts over 0.7 s, the HUD returns with the new HP.
   - **FFX-2 menu rule (D-357: nothing plays over an open command menu): a judgement call, flagged for Bailey.** In Active ATB the player is usually in a menu when Mega Flare tells, so skipping the wash would skip the headline moment; the options book's own FFX-2 storyboard shows the menu sunk under the wash. With a menu open as the tell begins the breath therefore plays RESTRAINED: the wash at 60 percent, the HUD sinking only to half (the menu stays readable and can be worked), the flood capped at 55 percent; the name, the ring and the core in full. The victory freeze is skipped over an open menu (D-357 kept there). One constant each in `BreathPlan.ts` (`MENU_WASH`, `MENU_HUD_SINK_TO`, `MENU_FLOOD_CAP`) if he wants it stricter (all off) or looser.
   - Not done: the camera's 3 to 5 percent lean (the book's storyboard) and the music duck. The camera is the camera lane's (`pro-camera`, "Camera on every action"; a held push of ours would replace the 0.06 push `actionOpen` already holds and `FrameFit` would not guard it); the duck needs a bus the presenter's `AudioPort` does not have (`AudioManager.duck()` exists; agents cannot hear it, so Bailey judges it from the audition page).
2. **Boss card as a full-bleed painting (card)**, both chapters. The chapter's own backdrop full-bleed and lit high-key, the boss large on the right with a soft rim, the pill, the name in ink-navy (FFX) or plum (FFX-2) serif italic, a gold or pink rule and the chapter's own subtitle ("The mountain of the Ronso, and what was done to them", "Something she used to call by name": the existing `chapter.subtitle`, not invented text); a one-frame white blink in, a 2.6 s slow push, a hard cut out; any press still skips. The loading card over the entry transition (`transitions/loadingCard.ts`) takes the same style so one card carries on. The approved masters are only read, never edited. **Bahamut's cut-out still has loose pale shards at the right wing (gap G20): the rim and haze hide some, a repair of the master is owed before Chapter IV's card is shown close** (not touched here: masters are Bailey's).
3. **Victory freeze and defeat card (ending)**, both chapters.
   - Victory: the last blow of the fight's last link holds the FIELD half a second (option C's hit-stop clock, `FxStagePort.freeze`; with BATTLE SPECTACLE off only the presenter's timeline holds), a white flash, a shock ring out of the boss's chest, the HUD out; then the boss goes up in light as before. A quiet VICTORY and "<foe> felled" top right with the cheer. **FFX:** the cheer's hold is the longest real clip among the party (`data/motion/realMotion.ts` `poseMs`, Tidus WIN_00 3,666.7 ms, status PROVEN). **FFX-2 Chapter IV:** the sources withhold the pose, the fanfare and the quip after Bahamut (`research/ffx-vs-ffx2-presentation.md` 2.2, writing bible E6 and 5.4), so nothing is celebrated: the party stands (at least 2.4 s, the live build's own 2,366 ms), the title is quiet, and the silent pull-back (letterbox bars, a draw-back of the camera) is only the transition into the results. The cream wipe is replaced by a soft dissolve over the held scene (`playResultsWipe(..., { dissolve })`); the results panel itself is today's. No freeze under REDUCE MOTION (it is one of the four things that setting turns off), none on a chained link, none over an open FFX-2 menu.
   - Defeat: no black flash; the scene dims and cools, a caption ("The party has fallen") drops in, the HUD goes with the light; the flow pushes `ProDefeatScreen` over the kept battle screen (`App.pushOverlay`): TRY AGAIN focused, OTHER FIGHT, Back for the picker, the foe's latest move as the cause ("Felled by Total Annihilation"), the turn, the attempt and the time. It gives the old demo defeat panel's own choice words, so the flow after it (`noteDemoDefeatChoice`, `carryAfterDefeat`, `takeDemoNextFight`) is untouched. The cause line is the foe's latest move name; the caption and the card words are [ours] (the writing bible has no defeat line for the demo fights).
4. **Overdrive opening and Change plate (opening)**.
   - FFX only (Overdrives are FFX's): the splash banner, the name slab and the bars give way to the camera push the move already makes, a light change (the arena lightens toward ivory around the lit caster), his name set small at his side with "<name> · Overdrive", a glint along the blade and a white pop as the strike begins; the hold is 1.0 s (today's slab is 1.25 s plus 0.26 s of moves). ## Frame cost (measured, headless Chromium on the real GPU, D3D11)

- **2560 x 1440, page frame intervals** (`requestAnimationFrame`, 240 frames each, `evidence/jobA/results.json`): at rest (a menu open) 16.67 ms mean, p95 16.7 to 16.8, max 16.8; during a held breath at its tell and at its hold the same (mean 16.67, p95 16.8, max 16.8), in both chapters. The page is vsync-bound at 60 Hz here, so nothing is measurable on top of it; the layer's own cost is below.
- **The layer's own CPU time per frame** (`ProStageLayer.stats`, an average of the last ten frames, `updateMs`): 0.10 to 0.20 ms while a held breath is drawing (the canvas at 1280 x 720, half the frame, redrawn each frame: one fill, four to six cut windows, about 34 motes, the core), 0.01 to 0.05 ms for the ending and the openings, about 0 when nothing is staged (an early return; the HUD's opacity is untouched). The single worst frame seen was 3.4 ms (the canvas's first resize and draw).
- **Whole real fights at 1600 x 900** (autoplay, 5,346 frames in Chapter I and 2,316 in Chapter IV while a staged beat was alive, `evidence/jobC3` and `jobC4`): mean 16.7 ms, p50 and p95 16.7 ms, a worst single frame of 83 ms (Chapter I) and 67 ms (Chapter IV), a handful in each run: NOT isolated, so not attributed to the layer or cleared of it (a run with the staging at today's, taken the same way, was not made; the page's other work, texture uploads and shader warm-up, produces such frames).
- **Memory:** one 1280 x 720 canvas (3.7 MB) per battle screen, created once at its first draw and not resized again; nothing is held after the screen leaves (`dispose`).
- No console error in any run (`consoleErrors` empty in every results file); the one warning in every run is release 62's `[fx-b] depth plates unavailable` loader (the art mirror has no depth plates), already disclosed in the integration notes.
