## 2. Lulu tiny when hurt, and the other poses like her

How the game draws a pose: every pose is sized from the idle's pixel scale times a `scale` from the pose registration (`src/data/art/poseRegistrationFfx.ts`, generated by `tools/posescale/measure.py table` from `docs/target/pose-measure.json`, which is read off `tools/posescale/reviews.json` and `overrides.json`), then `HeadLock.ts` holds the pose's registered head box to the idle's head size on screen (within 0.9 to 1.1 of the table's scale). The rule, since release 39 (D-420), is **the head**: each pose is scaled until its head is the idle's head. A painting whose proportions differ from the idle's therefore changes height: Lulu's hurt and low-HP paintings are squat, big-headed figures of a very slender idle (their heads are 1.5 and 1.9 times the idle's at the same pixel scale), so matching the head drew her at two thirds of her height. D-298's stature floor (0.60) never bit, and D-457 (Bailey, "Lift it for those two") lifted it for Lulu's critical so the head would follow the idle's.

Measured **in the game** (headless Playwright, the dev server, 1600x900, the stage's own camera; `figure height` is the projected alpha box of the plane, the head is the registered head box the lock holds; before = the table of `8a3e8eff7`):

| pose | Chapter XXVII before | after | Chapter IX before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lulu hurt (height of her idle) | 0.666 | 0.906 | 0.669 | 0.903 |
| Lulu low HP (her resting pose under half HP) | 0.544 | 0.749 | 0.549 | 0.748 |
| Lulu attack, cast (not changed) | 0.967, 1.05 | 0.968, 1.051 | 0.964, 1.046 | 0.968, 1.051 |

After, in other chapters (measured the same way): Yuna hurt 0.904 and attack 0.905 (Ch IX), Auron hurt 0.909, attack 0.902, low HP 0.753 (Seymour Omnis), Wakka cast 1.095 and Rikku attack 0.890 (Chapter VIII, the airship). Frames: `docs/screenshots/r50-1/lulu-before-after-ch27-1600x900.jpg`, `lulu-before-after-ch9-1600x900.jpg` (idle, hurt, low HP, before above after).

The fix, with no painting touched: a new hand input, `tools/posescale/overrides.json` `stature` `{floor, ceiling, by}`, applied by `measure.py` (a pose is drawn at no less than `floor` and no more than `ceiling` of its idle's height); the record keeps the ruler's reading and says what the hold cost (`held.reading`, `held.headRatio`, `held.by`); the pose's registered head box is the idle's over the **applied** scale, so the lock, the table and `tools/pose-scale-check.mjs` agree and the lock does not undo the hold. Rule used: hurt, attack and cast between 0.90 and 1.10 of the idle's height; the low-HP slouch at 0.75 or more (a deep bend is legitimately lower). It touched nine poses:

| pose | was (height of idle) | now | head vs idle's |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lulu hurt | 0.665 | 0.90 | x1.35 |
| Lulu low HP | 0.546 | 0.75 | x1.38 |
| Auron attack | 0.701 | 0.90 | x1.29 |
| Auron hurt | 0.726 | 0.90 | x1.24 |
| Auron low HP | 0.609 | 0.75 | x1.23 |
| Yuna hurt | 0.774 | 0.90 | x1.16 |
| Yuna attack | 0.877 | 0.90 | x1.03 |
| Rikku attack | 0.801 | 0.90 | x1.12 |
| Wakka cast (a ceiling) | 1.116 | 1.10 | x0.985 |

**The trade, and what it does to CHK-026.** The check's head rule (3 percent) reads the same record's head boxes, which for a held pose are the applied scale's, so CHK-026 passes these poses by construction (as its item 6 already says of every registered pose); it does not see that the real heads are bigger. Nothing in the harness measures a figure's **height** across a swap, which is how a head-matched Lulu passed it while looking tiny. Recommendation (not built: it is a critic change, and ideas need a yes): add a height-continuity reading to CHK-026, or have it report `held.headRatio` beside the head. `tests/unit/engine/pose-stature-hold.test.ts` pins the nine holds, that every FFX party hurt, attack and cast stands inside 0.90 to 1.10 and every low-HP pose at 0.75 or more, and that `pose-scale-check.mjs` refuses a hold with no word, a wrong ratio or a head box that is the ruler's.

`tests/unit/engine/pose-scale-art.test.ts` is updated: only Rikku Berserker's ready (FFX-2) now stands under D-298's floor (Lulu's critical is held at 0.75; its record still says `gateLifted`).

Sidecars and approved art: no sidecar, no painting, no `approved-hashes.json` entry and no file of the art mirror was written; `docs/target/approved-hashes.json` covers Lulu's critical, defend, follow, od-fury, ready, sleep and idle@2x PNGs and no hurt file and no sidecar.

**Not changed, for Bailey to say: the KOs.** A KO painting lies down, and head-matching draws the fallen body very short: its length over the idle's standing height is Lulu 0.43, Auron 0.48, Wakka 0.57, Yuna 0.61, Kimahri 0.61, Rikku 0.63, Tidus 0.89. A hold for them would make every KO head x1.7 to x2.5 the idle's at the moment of the collapse; that is a bigger look change than anything above and wants his word. Other poses outside the brief's list that are more than a tenth off (`defend`, `ready`, `follow`, `sleep`, `item`, `od-*`, `victory`; for example Tidus victory 1.59, Yuna defend 1.64, Lulu sleep 0.62, Auron item 0.69) are not touched either: `node tools/pose-scale-check.mjs` and the record's `stature` column list them.

How to redo it: edit `overrides.json` (or add a pose), then `D:\Tools\ComfyUI\python_embeded\python.exe -s tools/posescale/measure.py measure <subjects> --poses <poses> --write` and `... table`, then `node tools/pose-scale-check.mjs`.
