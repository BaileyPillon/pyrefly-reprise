### P5.2 The Wendigo and the Guardians move by their own motion (item 1)

**Method.** The game's own banks (`chr/mon/m212` and `m213`, `resident1.mgrp`: 26 and 12 clips) are converted to glTF animations by the shared converters (read only), the FFX skeleton is mapped onto the Meshy rig by the bind pose (the automap, which needed one fix: a skeleton rooted at the pelvis, as both monsters' are, puts the hands below the root), and the clips are retargeted onto the Meshy Wendigo and the two Meshy Guardians by the party's own world-delta method (in place, `--handfollow`). Two new options: `--hipref idle1` (the pelvis of the idle's first frame is the reference: the monsters' bind pose has the pelvis at the origin and their clips carry its standing height, so the first run lifted the Wendigo 1.4 m) and `+` in a clip name (several of the game's motions played one after the other as one clip). The viewer's sheets of the real clips beside the retargeted ones are in the lane's scratch.

### P5.3 The four aeons are 3D models (item 2)

**What the roster is.** The chapter's preset owns Valefor, Ifrit, Ixion and Shiva (`research/re-ffx-wendigo-lake-macalania.md` 12); **Bahamut is not owned at this point of the story** (Zanarkand), so the fight can never summon him. The brief named five; **Bahamut was not built** (pick P24).

**Method (skinned to the real skeleton, so there is no retarget).** (1) The four real models (`s001` to `s004`, HD) are converted with all their clips; their bind poses are canonical (Ifrit a T-pose, Shiva an A-pose, Ixion standing, Valefor hanging with the wings spread). (2) Three renders of each in the bind pose, on a plain ground, front, left and back (1024 x 1024). (3) Meshy multi-image-to-3D (meshy-7.1, geometry 2k, 4k textures with PBR, 60,000 polygons, no re-pose): **35 credits each, 140 in all; the balance went 2,570 to 2,430** (the ledger is the lane's `cost-log.jsonl`). The four came back as faithful textured copies of the real designs in the same pose. (4) The Meshy mesh is fitted to the game's bind-pose mesh (yaw by bounding box, then scale and offset by closest points; the mean distance to the game's mesh after the fit is 0.14, 0.28, 0.16 and 0.11 model units for Ifrit, Valefor, Ixion and Shiva, 1.2 to 2.2 percent of their heights) and **every Meshy vertex takes its joints and weights from the six nearest vertices of the game's mesh** (inverse-square blend, the four strongest kept, then three smoothing passes over the Meshy mesh welded by position), so the file holds the game's skeleton (Valefor's 164 bones, Ifrit's 168, Ixion's 332 and Shiva's 248, of which 73, 71, 148 and 130 deform the mesh: wings, tails, mane, cloth and Shiva's hair crown among them) and **the game's own `s00N` clips drive it as they are**. (5) The textures are re-encoded to 2048 for the base and 1024 for the normal and metal-rough maps. Each aeon file is 8.2 to 9.8 MiB, about 38 MiB of GPU by the lane's estimate.

**The files' own clips:** `idle` (`idle1`), `hurt` (`dmg1`), `ko` (`dead_00`), `attack` (`attack1_00` + `attack1_01`; Valefor's `attack_00` + `attack_01`; the effect trigger of the first part: Ifrit 0.73 s, Ixion 0.63 s, Shiva 0.93 s, Valefor 2.67 s) and `cast` (`magic2_00` to `magic2_02`; Shiva's `skill_00` to `skill_02`; the trigger 2.47, 2.4, 2.47 and 3.27 s). The scene root of each carries the scale law's metres (`LAKE_AEON_METRES_PER_UNIT`, 0.3857 per model unit) and the lift that puts the lowest point on the ice.

**In the game:** the skin attaches a model to the aeon's actor when its file is in (the aeons load in the background after the bench, one at a time; until then the painting stands, as before; **and it stands for good when an aeon's file is missing or cannot be read** (fix pass of 2026-11-09, minor 5): the loader gives such an aeon no model, not a grey 3.4 m capsule, and lists it in `capsules` so a run still sees the failure), turns it toward the party's side, and drives its clips by the same beats as the monsters'. **Real keys** (`tools/lake-finish-capture.mjs`): Yuna's Summon row and each aeon's own row call Valefor, Ifrit, Ixion and Shiva in turn; each stands as a model (`carrying[id] === 'own'`), attacks by key, and the first fires an Overdrive chosen by key; Dismiss sends it away. The Overdrive plays the aeon's `cast` clip with the presenter's own effects (pick P27).

### P5.4 Sleep ends the Wendigo's stance on the stage (item 3)

The engine clears the stance flag for a sleeper only at the next hit (the battle lane's open reading), so the stage could not see it. The presenter now tells the actor every status it is given (`ActorHandle.noteStatus`, optional; a painted actor keeps a set; `status-add` and `status-remove` are the two calls), and the skin reads `sleep` from the actor: **`MonsterRig.noteSleep(true)` drops the stance on the spot, plays `sleep_in` and holds `sleep`; waking plays `wake` and settles in the idle; a sleeper does not act, raise its arms or flinch (P31); a fallen body sleeps no more.** **Real keys** (the capture): the Guardians fall, the Wendigo (HP set under half) raises its arms, a Sleeping Powder (put in the bag by a state edit, thrown by the Item row) lands, and the model plays `sleep_in` then `sleep` with the arms down; stills `sleep-0-arms-up` and `sleep-2-asleep`.

### P3.7 The ice break, and what the story's scenes can and cannot show

## 0. What changed against the preflight (read first)

## 2. What it is

Eighteen thousand HP, STR 40, no Defense and no weakness; a punch of 1,634 to 2,015 against cells of 850 to 1,900 HP, so **a member dies to a blow unless the blow is weakened**. Two Guado Guardians keep it that way.
