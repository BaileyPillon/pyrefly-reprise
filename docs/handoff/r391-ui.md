flipped a flag with nothing to show. Now: the room is counted as it is painted (`room x scale` against 56; `railRoom`), and asked whether the guide is up or not; where even that does not

written; 2 layout px of hysteresis so the girls' idle sway does not flicker it. In Chapter IV while Paine is cursed the status cure card takes the column and the sheet used to step
aside (`sgd--hint-alone`): the rail now folds to its tab for as long as that card is up instead of vanishing.

tall in IV and 254 to 508 px in VI with 0 overlap with any girl's or boss's box, and G again folds it. Chapters XI, XV and XVI at 130 percent (1600x900) now keep their 330x164 sheet
instead of losing it; V and the chapters that already fit are unchanged; nothing folds at 100 percent or in FFX (tested). Frames: `pr0389-guide-ffx2-bahamut-*`, `-leblanc-*` (16).
