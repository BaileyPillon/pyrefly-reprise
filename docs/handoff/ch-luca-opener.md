## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board. Type **`debut`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself, with no card and no sound before it. D is `right` (the first letter moves the cursor one step, as `daigoro`'s and `duelist`'s do), E is `start` (the board ignores it), and B, U and T are bound to nothing, so the last letter leaves no latched press. On a phone the long-press field on the "Chapter select" label takes the same word.
2. Party prep shows **the three girls at Lv 2, 2 and 3** (an estimate, section 3). Enter starts the chapter: a pre scene (our draft, section 8), then **act 1, Logos and Ormi** on the Leblanc stage, then a seam (the first sphere comes back to Yuna and its light closes the scrapes), then **act 2, Leblanc**, with **Yuna the Songstress and Darkness Dance her one command**, then the post scene and the results page. Act 2 is entered at full HP and MP with **no Save Sphere card**; items spent stay spent. A defeat on act 2 retries act 2; a defeat on act 1 starts the chapter over. Escape is off.
3. A clear goes to the experiments' own store (`pyrefly-reprise:experiments:v1`), never the save: no save change, no migration. Kill rewards are the rows': act 1 pays EXP 3 + 3, gil 60 + 60, AP 1 + 1; act 2 pays EXP 8, gil 180, AP 2; the results page pools the chain (14 EXP each, 300 gil, AP 4, a Hi-Potion in the smoke run).

## 3. The data and its sources (rule 6: nothing invented)

Every number is `research/re-ffx2-luca-opener.md` (the research lane, 2026-11-09: `monster.bin` rows 129 to 131 and the move rows of the three plain-text scripts, each equal to its compiled worker), tagged `[RE row n]` in the files. **`tests/fixtures/parity/ffx2/luca_opener_rows.json`** holds the three monsters, the seven move rows (the Die row included), both scenes' start positions and the scripts' constants as decoded fields with their file offsets, **written by a reader of this lane from the Steam HD kernel tables** (not copied from the note's prose; numbers only, no game text), and `tests/unit/chapters/luca-opener-data.test.ts` (40 tests) compares the enemy data, the monster records and the command records to it field by field. The guide follows Jegged's "Luca" page (one guide only; the second guide has no page for the prologue fights): the order, "three against two", Rikku's steals, Yuna held to the Songstress.

**Where the note's prose and the sources differ, the source wins** (listed so nobody trusts the prose):
- **The Potion's threshold is "18 or less", not "19 or less"** (`calc-52.out.txt` of the note said 19): the script tests `hp < max / 5`, `97 / 5` is 19 by integer division, and the test is strict. Pinned (`luca-opener-ai.test.ts`: 19 pistols, 18 throws it).
- **The special word's Bribe bit.** The note reads bit 8 as "no Bribe" (an earlier note's reading); this repo's `FFX2MonsterRecord.special` documents bit 9 as Bribe, and 0x19b has bit 9 clear, so **no `immune-to-bribe` flag** is set (the rows carry no Bribe item either way, so a bribe would pay nothing). The data test pins that bits 6 and 9 are clear, so Delay and Interrupt work.
- **Research item U2 is settled by the code, not the note:** a party of one or two girls has been legal since 2026-11-06 (`FFX2PartyBuild`), so the optional concert fight's two-girl party would not have needed engine work. The concert fight is still not built (LO1).

**Gaps, named (not invented):**
- **The girls' levels and bag at Luca (U1).** No source below the repo's Lv 20 anchor. Lv 2 / 2 / 3, ten Potions and three Phoenix Downs are **`[estimate]`**, measured. **The engine's stat table is not trustworthy below Lv 20 for the Gunner and the Warrior** (the fallback table extrapolates and is **not monotonic**: a Lv 2 Gunner reads 828 HP, a Lv 12 one 1,035 and a Lv 20 one 862; a Lv 3 Warrior has DEF 103). It is the engine's number and the bench uses it; the table's rows at Lv 1 to 12 are an engine fact for the FFX-2 engine's owner, not this chapter's and not touched here (section 13).
- **How the game holds Yuna to one command (U3)** is a scripted event, not read. This build holds her by the party template (LO3, LO9).
- **Logos' Potion at a fallen Ormi.** The script's test reads 0 HP for a fallen body, so it queues the Potion at him once; the game's handling is `[gap]`. The engine **redirects a heal aimed at a fallen friend to a living friend, Logos himself**, and Ormi stays down (pinned in `luca-opener-engine.test.ts`).
- **Ormi's lone-45-HP rule is not ported (LO5):** it applies when no friendly monster stands in the formation (a test formation); the real scene has Logos beside him, so he keeps his row's 97.
- **Leblanc's opening ATB wait** (her init presets it) is not modelled; the engine's own opening gauges apply. **The "speak and fall" Die row (0x405f)** is not built (research U4: its one status entry is unmapped); the fall is the engine's KO. **The thinking byte (30)** is never read (as for the Magus Sisters, the Den of Woe's note M8). **The props** (Ormi's shield, row 265; Leblanc's fan, row 139) are paintings, not combatants.
- **Item effects and prices.** Iron Bangle, Silver Glasses and Silver Bracer are held-only rows with **no price** (unsourced); their effects are the accessory table's (`battle/ffx2/accessories.ts` already wears Iron Bangle's +20 percent HP and Silver Bracer's +40 percent MP; Silver Glasses guards Darkness: `research/ffx2-leblanc-syndicate.md` §7.5).
