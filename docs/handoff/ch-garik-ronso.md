## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board. Type **`braveheart`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself, with no card and no sound before it. B, H and T are bound to nothing, so the first letter moves nothing and the last leaves no press latched; R (`r1`), A (`left`), V (`select`) and E (`start`) are later letters of a live match and are claimed by the word. On a phone, the long-press field on the "Chapter select" label takes the same word.
2. Party prep shows the three girls (Yuna Lv 32, Rikku Lv 33, Paine Lv 34) and **one more tab, TRUST, for this chapter only**: two rows, **Low** (the hostile fight, the default) and **High** (the friendly bout), the tooltip "In the game this follows how you answered the Ronso on Mt. Gagazet." A click or tap on a row sets it; **Q** (or a lone Shift tap; pad Y) flips it; **Tab is the browser's on this screen** and walks the focus to the rows, where Enter presses the focused one. The choice is kept in `pyrefly-reprise:garik-ronso-trust:v1` (never the save, never the experiments' records; the default leaves no key) and takes effect when the battle opens.
3. Enter starts the chapter: a pre scene (our draft, section 8), then **one fight**: the **hostile fight** (Garik and two Ronso Youths, 15,000 HP) or the **friendly bout** (Garik and one Youth, 10,940 HP), on Chapter I's Mt. Gagazet plate under the FFX-2 camera, then the post scene and the results page. Escape is off. A defeat retries the same fight.
4. A clear goes to the experiments' own store (`pyrefly-reprise:experiments:v1`), never the save: no save change, no migration. The rewards are the rows' own: EXP 820, Gil 130, AP 7 (hostile) or 600, 110, 5 (bout), a Shining Bracer from Garik (byte 128) and a Remedy from each Youth.

## 3. The data and its sources (rule 6: nothing invented)

`tests/unit/chapters/garik-ronso-data.test.ts` (50 tests) compares the enemy data, the monster records and the command records to the fixture field by field (the 13 moves: Attack 0x41da, Bane Lance 0x411c, Blind 0x406f, Mighty Guard 0x4084, Shell 0x30bb, Protect 0x30bc, the Youth's Attack 0x4000 and MP Blowout 0x411d, the four announcements 0x4232 to 0x4235 and Time Up Meteor 0x4236). **Rule 5 (hit rule):** exactly the two Attacks, Bane Lance and MP Blowout roll (`canMiss: true`; accuracy formulas 2, 1, 2, 1); every other move carries `canMiss: false`, set explicitly.

**Gaps, named (not invented):**
- **Item ids and names.** Garik's drop (Shining Bracer 0x905d), his steal (Icy Gleam 0x9024) and the Youth's steal (Mythril Bangle 0x9002) are the rows'. - **Positions.** Both scenes ship no start positions (`pos_mtgz10_228.psc` is empty; there is none for `_229`), so every near-weighted target search is the uniform pick, as in every other script of this kind.
- **The "own target" group** of MP Blowout's search (RE U3) is read as every living girl, the one with the most MP, ties broken by a search; the guide's wording is the same.
- **Which answer is which** at the eight conversations and whether the second visit starts from zero (RE U1, U2) are not read: the chapter offers the two outcomes.
- **The thinking bytes** (0 for Garik, 30 for the Youth) are recorded and not modelled: the engine's thinking base is 0 in play (`research/re-ffx2-atb-status.md` section 3.4).
- **Stature and action time** (animation length, model height) are unread; the Ronso stand at `[estimate]` heights (section 7).
- **A Black Mage that is KO'd** is read as not a Bane Lance target (the search keeps the living; the script's own handling of a fallen girl is not in the note, `[I]`).

**What that says, plainly.** (a) **With the right answer the fight is easy and short**: two Dark Knights casting Darkness (special damage that ignores Defense and Protect and hits all three Ronso at once) end the hostile fight in about 36 game seconds, usually before Garik's seventh turn (0.5 Mighty Guards a fight), and nobody is ever knocked out. That is the guides' "not too difficult". (b) **A careless line is a different fight**: with one Darkness caster and no Protect the same party wins 82 of 100 at bench speed and 34 of 50 at the live default, and **1 of 50 at the Active clock**, because **Mighty Guard's Regen and Haste out-heal a single caster** (26 guards a fight, 15 game minutes) until the "battle too long" block's Time Up Meteor ends it: the block is live in play and is what loses those fights. (c) **The Black Mage trap lands every fight** (1.00 curses) **and costs little with this party** (the advisor line still wins 100 of 100; a cursed Yuna cannot change back, 0.15 KOs a fight). (d) **The bout is a sparring match**: 100 percent on every line and pace. (e) **The Chapter 2 preset cannot win the hostile fight** (0 of 33, both lines, to the block) and wins the bout; the Djose preset is the right build. **Nothing was tuned.** The lever, if Bailey plays it and wants it harder, is the party (a lower level or no second Dark Knight) or the bag, never a boss number.
