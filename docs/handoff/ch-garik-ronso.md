## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board. Type **`braveheart`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself, with no card and no sound before it. B, H and T are bound to nothing, so the first letter moves nothing and the last leaves no press latched; R (`r1`), A (`left`), V (`select`) and E (`start`) are later letters of a live match and are claimed by the word. On a phone, the long-press field on the "Chapter select" label takes the same word.
2. Party prep shows the three girls (Yuna Lv 32, Rikku Lv 33, Paine Lv 34) and **one more tab, TRUST, for this chapter only**: two rows, **Low** (the hostile fight, the default) and **High** (the friendly bout), the tooltip "In the game this follows how you answered the Ronso on Mt. Gagazet." A click or tap on a row sets it; **Q** (or a lone Shift tap; pad Y) flips it; **Tab is the browser's on this screen** and walks the focus to the rows, where Enter presses the focused one. The choice is kept in `pyrefly-reprise:garik-ronso-trust:v1` (never the save, never the experiments' records; the default leaves no key) and takes effect when the battle opens.
3. Enter starts the chapter: a pre scene (our draft, section 8), then **one fight**: the **hostile fight** (Garik and two Ronso Youths, 15,000 HP) or the **friendly bout** (Garik and one Youth, 10,940 HP), on Chapter I's Mt. Gagazet plate under the FFX-2 camera, then the post scene and the results page. Escape is off. A defeat retries the same fight.
4. A clear goes to the experiments' own store (`pyrefly-reprise:experiments:v1`), never the save: no save change, no migration. The rewards are the rows' own: EXP 820, Gil 130, AP 7 (hostile) or 600, 110, 5 (bout), a Shining Bracer from Garik (byte 128) and a Remedy from each Youth.

**What that says, plainly.** (a) **With the right answer the fight is easy and short**: two Dark Knights casting Darkness (special damage that ignores Defense and Protect and hits all three Ronso at once) end the hostile fight in about 36 game seconds, usually before Garik's seventh turn (0.5 Mighty Guards a fight), and nobody is ever knocked out. That is the guides' "not too difficult". (b) **A careless line is a different fight, and it does not end**: with one Darkness caster and no Protect the same party wins 82 of 100 at bench speed, 34 of 50 at the live default and 1 of 50 at the Active clock (the same wins as the first build), but **Mighty Guard's Regen and Haste out-heal a single caster** (19 to 100 guards a fight) and **nothing ends the fight**: of the 18 non-wins at bench speed 16 are stalled at 60 game minutes and 2 are lost; at the live default 16 of 16 stalled; at the Active clock 46 of 49 stalled (3 lost). The first build's table called these Meteor losses; the game has no Meteor here. A stalled fight is left by the pause screen (not checked in this chapter), since escape is off. (c) **The Black Mage trap lands every fight** (1.00 curses) **and costs little with this party** (the advisor line still wins 100 of 100; a cursed Yuna cannot change back, 0.15 KOs a fight). (d) **The bout is a sparring match**: 100 percent on every line and pace. (e) **The Chapter 2 preset cannot win the hostile fight** (0 of 33 on both lines: the advisor line loses 25 and stalls 8, the naive line loses 13 and stalls 20) and wins the bout; the Djose preset is the right build. **Nothing was tuned.** The lever, if Bailey plays it and wants it harder or easier, is the party (a lower level, no second Dark Knight, or more) or the bag, never a boss number. **Disclosed (open issue 2):** a weak line can now stall a fight indefinitely, as in the game; the chapter adds no clock of its own (that would be a boss-side change and needs a measured option and Bailey's yes).
