## 1. What was asked, and what the evidence says

Round 33b read 21 swap jerks in Yunalesca's fight, 7 of them revive swaps stepping 50 to 131 px (14 revives in 1,294.8 s); release 69 had read 3 of 8 revives up to 89 px. The suspect in the brief was release 72's eased knock-out slide (`SlideEase` in `ProneLay`).

**It is not the slide.** Three independent reads agree:

- Release 69 read the same 88 px before `SlideEase` existed.
- The release-73 Yunalesca revive job (seed 2, 1,013 to 1,173 s of battle): live release 72 has 4 revive steps of 88, 89.7, 90.6 and 131.5 px in 8 revives (all Wakka, the tracked **feet**, dx -83 to -123); `?treated=pack` (release 70's draw, no eased slide) has 4 of 10 at 48.4, 88.4, 88.6 and 88.8; the candidate 1 of 8 at 88.8. The same 88 to 89 px for Wakka in every arm (the 48.4 is Yuna's head on a 2.4 ms frame): the step does not depend on the slide.
- Offline with the real `PaintedActor` and **no neighbour, so no slide at all**, in the page's world Wakka's revive reads **85.0 px** at 60 Hz (live 88 to 90.6).

**The cause is the revive's cross.** `living.revive` crosses the lying painting to the standing painting already toppled where the body lay; its head is set on the lying head (`meeting`, release 69: the head step is 2 to 7 px) but the body is longer than the curled lying one, and the harness's tracked "feet" of a LYING painting is the silhouette's lowest thick part (`estimateStance`; the pose table leaves a lying stance null), which for Wakka is at the head end of the painting. The two planes' tracked feet are 300 to 420 px apart (the review measured 331 px offline and about 400 on the live recording). The harness averages the planes' points weighted by their fades, so the tracked feet travel that distance as the incoming share goes 0 to 1. The page's old cross (`koCrossMs()` = 100 ms under the demo motion, the default; `1 - u` out, `u * u` in) is six frames at 60 Hz and takes at most .24 of the share in a frame (.31 in the headless 75 ms cross's four): 85 px for Wakka. The earlier fixes (the held slide, the slide target read once) were right and are untouched; this step was never theirs.

Two things stay true and are disclosed: (a) nothing the player sees moves 90 px, two paintings fade; the step is what CHK-027 gates; (b) the instrument's "feet" of a lying body is not where its feet are (open question 3).

## 2. The change

- **New `engine/motion/ReviveCross.ts`.** `REVIVE_CROSS_MS` = **200**, `REVIVE_RISE_MS` = **300**; `reviveCross(u)`: the lying painting's and the standing one's fades at progress `u`, built so the incoming painting's share of the pair is `u` itself (an even share: every frame moves the weighted points the same distance) with the old cross's cap on the lesser plane kept (`(1 - u) * g` and `u * g`, the dip `g` taking the sum to `2 * KO_CROSS_PEAK` in the middle: the lesser plane is 0.382 at most, as before, so ghost severity `lo * (1 - iou)` can never reach CHK-027's 0.40). The `?revive=` switch: `reviveWord()`, `reviveEvenOn()`, `setReviveWord()`, `REVIVE_DEFAULT` (`'even'`).
- **`KoFall.ts`.** `KoRunOptions.even`, `reviveRun(travel, eased = false, even = false)`, `REVIVE_EVEN_MS` = 500. Only a body that topples and gets up gets the longer cross and the 300 ms rise, and not the held `?kotopple=eased` revive (rise 420; 200 + 420 would pass the presenter's 520). With `even` false the run is release 73's, option for option. `KO_CROSS_MS`, `REVIVE_MS` (395 headless, 420 in a page) and the fall are unchanged.
- **`fx/mix/living.ts`.** Passes `reviveEvenOn()`; the header says 500 ms.
- **Why 200 ms and a 300 ms rise.** A step is the gap between the two paintings' points times the share a frame takes; an even share is the best a given length can do, so only length helps. In the page's world, worst of ten figures at 60 Hz: 160 ms 43.4 (Wakka's feet), 180 ms 38.7, **200 ms 34.8** (35.5 with the FFX-2 songstress's head). With the recording's frame times (15.4 to 18.2 ms) 44.7, 39.9 and **35.9** (37.3 for the songstress's head). 200 + 320 would equal the presenter's 520 wait exactly; the turn runs over cross plus rise (`KoRun.turn`), so a 300 ms rise only quickens the figure's tip by 4 percent and leaves a frame and a fifth to spare.
