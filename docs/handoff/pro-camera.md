# pro-camera: the directed camera for the two demo chapters (lane "pro-camera", 2026-10-23)

Branch `pro-camera` (from `origin/playtest-1022`, 763086fcb), tree `D:/pyrefly-anim-proto`, dev server :5362 (stopped). Nothing deploys. The plumbing (the layer, the switch, the ports) is shared by both games and reaches only Chapter I (`seymour-flux`, FFX) and Chapter IV (`ffx2-bahamut`, FFX-2); the game case of each piece is in the table below.

Bailey's picks (2026-10-22, from the full-resolution phone mockups): "Real game's shots, E33 polish" and "Camera on every action". The options book's directed-camera area is `D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-10-22/options-book/camera/`; the real shots are the real-motion lane's `final/shots.json` (retail camera programs read out of the game's own scripts, replayed against six PS2 recordings; Bailey's 2026-10-18 rule allows retail data; only numbers are in the repo, `src/data/procam/proShots.ts`, generated by `tools/procam/gen-pro-shots.mjs`).

## The switch

| what | how |
|---|---|
| on | `?camera=pro`, or the shared `?pack=demo` (the camera is ON with the pack); only in the two demo chapters |
| off | default; `?camera=off` / `?camera=today` beside the pack for the A/B |
| the key | `;` on the playtest switchboard (`?pack=demo&switchboard=1`), LAST in the list (the master key `0` includes it; the label reads `camera pro / today`); flipped OFF mid-shot the shot ends in 320 ms and the FFX-2 follower is home in 320 ms (the layer asks the switch itself every frame); flipped ON it plays from the next beat |
| soft background | on with the camera; `?camdof=off` judges the camera alone |
| the caster shot | variant 2 of the real five (the low push that keeps both figures whole, repeatable); `?castshot=0..4` fixes another, `?castshot=random` draws as the real game does (a fixed seed) |
| the debug surface | `window.__pyrefly.procam(on?)`: the switch, the layer's counters, the run in flight and its pose, the director's log |

Off means off. With the switch off, in any other chapter, at fast-forward `skip`, on an upright phone (A-12 rules), under the `steady` / `originals` comfort presets, or under an open FFX-2 command menu (D-357), the director answers false and every beat plays exactly as release 62 plays it. `procam-camera.test.ts` runs the same moves, punches, rolls and shakes through `BattleCamera` with and without a layer that answers null and compares every frame; with the switch off no layer is even built.

The comfort rule (`ProLayer.comfortMs`, D-291 "made me a bit dizzy"; the `calm` preset caps a rig move at 20 degrees a second): a blend whose first and last view differ by more than 18 degrees, or whose eye travels more than 0.55 of the distance to the aim, is a CUT (as the real FFX); a smaller one is a glide no faster than 28 degrees a second. Measured on the shot lab (camera speed, the camera's own motion plus the game's shakes): p50 2.7, p95 28.8 degrees a second, against 9.4 and 53 before the rule.

REDUCE MOTION: every real shot is a held first frame (a cut in, a cut out), the plain-attack move is not played, the follower stays on the resting rig. `steady` and `originals` presets: the director declines everything.

The soft background (`?camdof=`): the band the game softens round the figures is aimed at the shot's focus figures under the shot's own pose (`ProLayer.band`) and the blur at the frame's edge deepens from 6 to 9 px while the shot is up, both by the shot's weight. It costs no pass. **Two things draw a band and the shot reaches both** (`procam/ProBand.ts`): the look's own mask-aware blur (`look/LookRig.ts`; this is the one on screen in the two demo chapters, where the look is on by default, switches the stock tilt-shift's blur to 0 and rewrites its band every frame: the first slice wrote the stock pass only, so the soft background never reached the screen, review M7), through `Renderer.proSoft`, which `MaxMix` sets and the look reads; and the stock tilt-shift (`fx/mix/cinema.ts`) when no look is on. The figures keep their own pixels in the look's blur (its mask), so the hall softens and the party stays sharp. The options book's figure-masked pass (two re-renders) is NOT built. `?camdof=off` leaves both untouched.

## Not done, said plainly

- **The real game's three default views** (a three-quarter back view over the party's shoulder; a high overhead after Seymour's Protect; a low side view after his Reflect) are NOT adopted. They are the real grammar, but our resting rig is the composition the HUD, the arena light and the paintings are tuned to; replacing it is a look change Bailey has not seen. `shots.json` has them (`ffx.default.p0/p1/p2`, clamped coverage 0.86, 1.0, 0.77).
- **FFX-2 under Active ATB.** D-357 ("nothing new plays over an open command menu") holds the target frame whenever any girl's menu is open, and in Active mode a menu is open for much of a fight, so the director declines those beats: in the scripted Chapter IV fight 64 of about 105 actions got a shot, in the real-key smoke 4 of 8; a special or Mega Flare that begins under an open menu gets none. The lab shows what they look like with no menu. Letting specials and Mega Flare play over a menu would be a call for Bailey (it moves the frame the targeting tile is drawn on).
- Not built: the figure-masked depth-of-field pass; summon, defeat, forced-action close-up and Overdrive middle shots (the effect's own); Seymour's Mortibsorption and Slowga (no usable shot); a landing shot for white magic (the real game has none); a replacement for the telegraph and form-change holds (the Held breath lane owns the boss's big moves).
- Not testable here: 120 Hz on a real display, a gamepad, hearing anything. The attack lean's strength (`ATTACK_FFX`: push 0.16, follow 0.4 / 0.22) and the follower's blend (`FOLLOW_BLEND` 0.5) are taste: one constant each.
- Seams with the sibling lanes: the staging lane (`?staging=pro`: Overdrive opening, win freeze frame, boss big moves) also wants `overdriveStart`, `victory`, `telegraph`; the director answers `overdriveStart` and `victory` (the real close shots) and leaves `telegraph` and `formChange` alone. Whichever lane owns a beat at the integration should be the one the `ShotDirector` hook gives it to; `BattleMoments.ask` asks the scene's director first, then this one.
