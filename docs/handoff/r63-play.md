## The skeptic's fight-start stutter (M1): cause found, one fix tried and reverted (fixer lane, 2026-10-23 evening)

The skeptic (`skeptic/SKEPTIC.md`) found no blocker and one major: the first 6 to 9 s of most fights lose 0.1 to 1.2 s of frame time (single frames of 120 to 270 ms). Game case: **both** (shared presentation plumbing; Chapter I FFX and Chapter IV FFX-2 pay the same cold load); demo mode only (`?demo=1`). **Nothing changed in the shipped code: the served build is the skeptic's code.** What the fixer lane found:

**Cause, measured (`fix/tools/diagstart.mjs`: a WebGL tap that times link, first draw, texture upload and `getImageData`, plus a CDP CPU profile attributed per long frame; the served build, headless GPU Chromium, 2560 x 1440):** it is not the new lanes. It is the **cold load of the fight**. The demo warm (`app/demo/demoWarm.ts`) builds both fights' stages behind the title and the picker (paintings read, depth plates cut, programs linked and drawn offscreen). `main.ts` starts it 6 s after the first frame (the once-per-page stage warm at 4 s), and it finishes Chapter I at about 9.6 s and Chapter IV at about 11.9 s of page time (the log: `seymour-flux: stage built` at 9.6 s, `ffx2-bahamut: stage built` at 11.9 s, `breath rigs read ahead` at 18.4 s). A player who picks a fight before that finds the warm stopped (`stopDemoWarm`) with 0 or 1 of its 7 steps done, so the paintings' alpha reads (`getImageData` 5 to 14 ms each, dozens of them), the programs' first draws (`getProgramInfoLog` 20 to 60 ms each) and the texture uploads (`texSubImage2D` 11 to 80 ms) all land in the entry swirl and the fight's first 2 to 3 s. The skeptic pressed through the title and the picker in about 4 s, so every one of his fights was cold; the new lanes' extra programs only make the cold load longer. **With the warm complete** (the diag's "patient" runs, 18 s on the title) the fight's first 9 s lose about 0.4 s in a few frames: one late program at about +7.8 s in Chapter IV (115 to 235 ms; the largest is a 14,880 / 12,620-character alpha-tested, shadowed shader linked at +8.2 s), the figures' first texture uploads at +0 s and the `extentsOf` 128 x 128 reads at +1 s (10 to 20 ms each). Nothing pre-warms those.

**The pace of a player:** the warm needs about 3.5 s of work per fight after it starts and the title's own art loading shares that time, so the clean start needs about 12 s on the title and the picker (about 9 s with the warm started at 1.6 s). A first session that reads the legend and the controls for a while will get it; a quick press will not.

**What Chromium needs to start the game's audio from a pad** (`audio/padUnlock.ts` documents the mechanism; checked here as far as a harness can): the page's `AudioContext` starts `suspended` under Chromium's default autoplay policy and resumes only inside a **user activation**.
