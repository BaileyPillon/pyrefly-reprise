## PR-0459: Auron's head at the killing blow (the decision)

The two swaps (attack to follow x0.9238, follow to idle x1.0933, Chapter I's killing-blow close-up with the pro camera) are the same two in rounds 29 and 30. Read offline from the kept recording of release 63's Chapter I (`tools/continuity-reread.mjs ... --swap=auron:attack:follow`; the head readings match round 30's to the third decimal):

- **The pair reads 1.001 everywhere else** (the five other attack to follow swaps of that fight: 1.0009, 1.0014, 1.001, 1.001, 1.0011) and 0.9232 in the close-up: it is the camera.
- **It is not a transient.** The follow plane's projected head is 27.7 against the idle's 30.1 (x0.92) for 16 frames running (frames 24388 to 24404 of the recording); the attack plane reads 30.3 (x1.007). Both are the registered (virtual) head box through each plane's quad.
- **The follow card is at the head lock's band.** The head lock (`HeadLock.ts`, D-510) scales a plane about the feet, within a band of 0.9 to 1.1, until its registered head is the idle's on screen. At the close-up the card is a wide lunge (799 x 673, scale 1.657, head at the top right) seen so obliquely that its two vertical edges are 601 and 437 px (x1.38): its head is on the far side. Estimating the card's world size from its quad against the idle's at the same ground point (the screen magnification at the stance point over the paintings' pixel scales, no camera model; good to about 2 percent) gives a lock factor of **x1.099** for the follow (x1.099, 1.099, 1.098, 1.098 in the four frames where the idle plane is also in the recording, 24403 to 24406): the ceiling of the band, and the head still 8 percent short. The lock would want about x1.2.
- So the **card-space head** (head box times world size) is not x1.00 either: the follow is drawn 10 percent bigger than its table row says, and the card ratio of attack to follow is about 1.10 (the exact lock factors are what probe 3 records tonight). **"Only perspective, so the gate reads the card" would not turn this green**; "a registration fault" is not it either: the registered head box is where the head is (the same pair is x1.001 in five other moments). It is the band.

**Options (not built: each is a look or a number nobody can judge without a clip; rule 9 and 15):** (A) leave the engine, keep the gate on the projected head, re-scope PR-0459 to "the lock's band in an oblique close-up" (two swaps a fight under the flash; Chapter I's CHK-026 stays FAIL on them, now named `lock-band`); (B) widen `HEAD_BAND` to about 0.8 to 1.25 while the close-up is up: the head reads x1.00 but the follow's body is drawn 9 percent bigger than its design (stature 0.908 x 1.2), trading a head jump for a height one; (C) have the cards turn toward the lens by a fraction of the camera's yaw in the close-up: removes the cause, is a look, and belongs with the camera-lab test; (D) hold `attack` across the close-up (skip the follow swap at the killing blow): changes the stroke. **Recommendation: A now, C when the camera-lab has its test; B only if Bailey wants a green gate more than a steady body.** Tonight's `ch1-resid` run settles the factors: prediction in the job file (lock of the follow plane 1.094 to 1.101, class `lock-band`).
