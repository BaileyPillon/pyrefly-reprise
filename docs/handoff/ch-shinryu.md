**Reading it.**
1. **The fight is a race the preset wins before Shinryu has taken a second turn.** 21 blows of 99,999 take about 24 ticks; Shinryu's own turns come every 15. The preflight predicted "about two of his turns"; it is about one. Wakka's and Tidus's Celestial weapons carry Evade & Counter, so Shinryu's Attack now and then draws a 99,999 counterattack of the party's own (0.26 a battle), which shortens it a little more and is not answered (a counter never triggers another counter).
2. **The Eraser cannot lose the fight.** The script uses it only while more than one swimmer lives, so it takes at most two of three. The no-Break-Damage-Limit row shows it plainly: exactly two shattered in all 100 battles (Erasers 2.00, shattered 2.00), then the last swimmer finishes alone in 600 ticks, healing a claw of about 270 with a bag of 99 X-Potions. The "race against the third Eraser" of the preflight's section 2 is therefore not a race that can be lost; it is a race to **keep the party whole**. The intent-board line ("Win before the third") is true of the second.
3. **What decides winning is Break Damage Limit and the 99 X-Potions, not the Eraser.** Without the limit the same party needs 201 blows and wins by attrition; with a thin party (the Highbridge cells, 9,999 a blow) Shinryu's claws end it in 15 ticks. Between the two there is **no sourced lever that gives 60 to 80 percent**; every row is 0 or 100. The only way to a middle is a new mode (rule 10: Bailey's yes), for example a lower-level party with the Celestial weapons but not the caps, whose numbers no source gives.

"Too easy at the caps": **right** (and more than predicted). "Plain-weapon what-if the same 99,999 cap": **right** (200 of 200, the same ticks). "The no-Auto-Haste what-if is harder": **right in kind, wrong in size** (still 200 of 200, 57 ticks).

## 7. The T0 checks and what the engine run found (rule 3: proved by running, not by grepping)

1. **E3: does a chance-255 Petrify bypass Ribbon and Stoneproof? Yes. Settled.** The game's own status step (`research/re-ffx-ctb-status.md` section 6; `battle/ffx/kernel/status-inflict.ts#statusLanding`): "it lands when the chance is 255, **or** when the resistance is not 255 and ...", so a 255 chance ignores a resistance byte of 255, which is all Ribbon and Stoneproof are. Run on the engine (`shinryu-engine.test.ts`, three cases): with Ribbon, with Stoneproof and with both on every swimmer, the Petrify resistance byte is 255 (the precondition), a **chance-254** copy of the Eraser does nothing, and the real Eraser lands and shatters. This is also Jegged's line, "this status will ignore any Ribbon and Stoneproof auto-abilities".
2. **FINDING: the Eraser row carries its own shatter byte (100)** (`command_records` fixture, column `shatter`), so it **erases a swimmer even on dry land**; the water flag is a second, independent reason. The test removes the byte as a control: without it dry land leaves the Petrified swimmer standing and the water alone still breaks him. The preflight's SH5 ("shatter in water, E3") is true and overdetermined.
3. **The one-alive rule** (RE 2): with exactly one swimmer standing the draw 1 becomes the Attack, so the Eraser is never aimed at the last one (the real script over 60 seeded runs reaches that case in more than 50 turns and uses the Eraser in none).
4. **The counter on any HP-taking hit** (RE 2 against the preflight's "any hit" wording): exactly one Attack per blow that takes HP, none for a heal or a zero-damage action, none for the killing blow, none for a counterattack of the party's own.
5. **Shining's spread.** The note named it unmapped. The row's damage words (0x8006, 0x2) equal Evrae Altana's Photon Spray, whose eight hits each re-roll their target; Shining is built the same (`random-enemy`, 8 hits), and over 30 seeds it reaches all three swimmers. The numbers: 75 to 84 a hit against MDEF 255, the note's.
6. **The numbers match the note exactly:** a party blow is **99,999 in all 1,680 blows of an 80-seed probe** (and in every blow of the 60-seed sample `shinryu-engine.test.ts` pins); Shinryu's Attack and counter **263 to 296** (the note's 328 to 370 with Protect, less 20 percent from Defense +20 percent); Shining 75 to 84.
7. **Threaten.** RE 0 lists Threaten as landing; the record's byte is 0, which the engine reads as a success percentage (never), the convention every boss has (`threatenChance: 0`), as the Penance handoff found for the same byte.
