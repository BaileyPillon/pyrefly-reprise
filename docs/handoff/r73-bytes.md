## The short answer

- **Release 69's hold left four loads on the phone's line during the entry, none of them drawn or heard before the first command.** Release 70's live phone10 run (round 32b, G4) moved 8.8 MB (Chapter I) and 15.3 MB (Chapter IV) between the battle screen's first line and the last figure being built (`pyrefly:enter:begin` to `stage`, 8.6 s and 16.9 s), and the first command waited for them at 1.25 MB/s. The four, in the order of the bytes they hold in Chapter I: **the boss rig's variant looks** (attack, cast, hurt, telegraph, ko with their rim and normal pieces: 2.8 MB in Chapter I, 3.3 MB in Chapter IV, awaited whole before the rig could be built), **the five (four) idle lighting maps** (2.2 MB, 1.8 MB: asked for by the first draw of each figure, which the hold had not deferred), **a magnified figure's 2x master** (Kimahri's idle, 0.9 MB of 2.4 MB inside the window, the governor's on-demand upgrade under the load card) and **the chapter's results cues** (the victory and defeat music: 1.6 MB in Chapter IV, fetched behind the fight's own theme).

The same bytes in another order, nothing dropped, nothing changed off the phone class. Per chapter on / held: section 4.
- **Modelled, offline, from the art manifest, the rig files and the shipped-file list of release 71 (upper bounds: the model counts the opening poses and the cues in full in both arms, ignores the pre-press audio of section 1, and treats the line as fully used, which it was not: 72 percent in Chapter IV):** the window falls from **13.2 to 7.3 MB in Chapter I (10.6 s to 5.8 s of a 10 Mbit/s line, 45 percent)** and from **16.3 to 9.7 MB in Chapter IV (13.1 s to 7.7 s, 41 percent)**; every other chapter loses its results cues (0.8 to 1.0 MB, 0.6 to 0.8 s). ## 3. What a player can see (AGENTS.md rule 9; the call is delegated and flagged)

**The brief was "no look change"; this lane does not meet that on every line, and says where.** Review major 2 found the first version's claim ("the figures are lit by the time the field is shown", "the variants land about 2 s before the first command") rested on a guess the tests did not pin and a check no agent could run. What is true now:

- **Lighting.** The idle maps used to land under the load card. They now land after the last figure is built, first in the queue: 2.2 MB takes 1.8 s of a 10 Mbit/s line and the tail (HUD, shader warm-up) is 2.5 s in Chapter I, 1.45 s in Chapter IV, then the boss card (1.3 s) and the sweep (6.4 s) follow. At 10 Mbit/s the figures are lit by the time the field is shown in Chapter I and within about 0.5 s of it in Chapter IV, behind the boss card; **on a line slower than that, the field is shown first and a person can see an unlit frame** (the shader still compiles once). `docs/DEV.md` used to say the card covers the field until the map lands; it covers it only until the figures are built and the shaders have drawn once (corrected). The governor's 2x masters start when the hold opens in all 19 chapters (cut 4; Kimahri's idle in Chapter I is the measured case), so a magnified figure sharpens a few seconds later than before in every chapter, and a 2x master that was already on its way before the hold is not affected. It is the budgets-2 trade again (that lane's section 2).
- **The boss rig.** A boss move whose variant has not landed draws the part's own image for that pose (the bones still move; the arm is not the attack arm). Hurt, telegraph and attack, the three a fight shows first, now share rank 1 with the idle maps (ahead of the party's everyday poses; cast, ko and the rest stay at rank 2), so the first 3.9 MB after the hold opens (the maps, 2.2 MB, and about 1.7 MB of these) is 3.1 s of a 10 Mbit/s line against 10.7 s from the stage to the first menu in release 70's record. The price: the party's everyday poses (the attack paintings) start about 1.5 MB, a little over a second, later; nobody can choose an attack before the first menu. At 1.6 Mbit/s (round 30b's Slow 4G) 3 MB takes about 15 s on its own and the first enemy move of an Active-mode FFX-2 fight could still draw the part's own image. A variant that never lands (a dropped file) leaves that pose on the part's own image instead of dropping the whole rig to the painting set (the one non-additive change, logged in `CONTRACT-CHANGES.md`).
- **How it is checked now, without a person.** The build reports the books at the `shown` mark (every entry mark carries them as its `detail`) and the job at the first menu: `rigEarlyLanded == rigEarlyLooks` (R73-14, a gate) and `mapsLoaded >= idleMaps` (R73-22, a gate) at the menu; the same for the maps at `shown` (R73-23, informative), the rig looks still pending at `shown` (R73-24) and the margin between the last rig file and the first menu (R73-16). A miss on a gate stops the lane. The by-eye job r73-5 remains the look call on a slow line; it is a person's.
- **The results cues** are a sound that plays at the end of a fight; they have landed long before.
- **The 2x master** is Kimahri's idle sharpening a few seconds later than before.
- The by-eye job (r73-5) reads the first two on a slow line. Keep, or hold the releases back until the card is up: the driver's call; the switch is one address word.
