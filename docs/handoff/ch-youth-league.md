## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board. Type **`youth`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself, with no card and no sound before it. **Y, O, U, T and H are all bound to nothing**, the only word on the door table with no bound letter, so no letter moves the cursor or can start anything; the last letter leaves no press latched. On a phone, the long-press field on the "Chapter select" label takes the same word.
2. Enter starts the chapter: a pre scene (our draft, section 8), then **seven fights in a row with no menu and no heal between them**: act 1 Yaibal with two League Warriors, act 2 two League Mages and a Veteran, act 3 a Warrior and two Veterans, act 4 two Slashers and a Master, act 5 four Veterans, act 6 Elma with two Veterans, act 7 Lucil alone. After each of the first six a short bark (3 to 7 s, the player's hands off). The party is **carried whole**: HP, MP, a fallen girl, statuses, the dressphere worn and the bag all arrive as the last act left them. **Lucil's fall ends the tournament**: the results page and the post scene.
3. A defeat on act 2 to 7 retries **that act** from the state the party entered it (kept as it was: this chapter never restores the party); a defeat on act 1 starts the chapter over. Escape is off everywhere.
4. A clear goes to the experiments' own store (`pyrefly-reprise:experiments:v1`), never the save: no save change, no migration. The party is the Chapter V preset as it is (Yuna White Mage Lv 46, Rikku and Paine Dark Knights Lv 48 and 50).

## 3. The data and its sources (rule 6: nothing invented)

`tests/unit/chapters/youth-league-data.test.ts` (88 tests) compares the twenty fighters, the twenty monster records, the 33 command records, the kill rows and the chain to the fixture field by field, and the rows the repo already holds under other names (Demi, Dispel, the Curtains, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Osmose, the Guard, Flash Bomb, Hush Grenade) to these, generically: any other command record with the same game id must equal ours. **Rule 5 (hit rule):** `canMiss` follows each row's accuracy formula (misc word bits 3 to 5): `true` for the three Attacks (formula 2 for Yaibal's/the League's and Lucil's, formula 1 against 130 for Elma's), `false` for every other move, set explicitly.

**Gaps, named (not invented):**
- **Start positions.** The seven scenes ship empty position files (RE 4): the game's near-weighted aim picks are uniform here, as in the Tourist Trap and Leblanc scripts.
- **Defeat, retry and Flee** (RE 8, 11 item 1): what HP the exit leaves, whether beaten acts repeat and whether Flee is offered are unmapped; the chapter uses the chain's own conventions (no escape, a checkpoint per act) and says so.
- **"Search among the living girls" for Harbinger's target** (`searchr(all, stat_death_sentence, not)`): read as the living girls without Doom (`[I]`).
- **The Master's element** is drawn at its first poll, not at the start of the battle (the engine has no init draw); the number is one draw of stream 2 either way.
- **The props** (Sword and Weapon, rows 186 and 201: HP 1, no action) are visual links in the game (RE 3.2) and are not in the formations.
- **Item ids.** Five game item ids gained a name in `KILL_ITEM_ID` (Ether, Phoenix Down, Chocobo Feather, Muscle Belt, Circlet); two steals have no `ItemDef` and became held-only rows with no price (M-Bomb, Amulet; `UNSOURCED_PRICE_IDS`). Healing Spring, L-Bomb and Grenade had rows.
- **Action time, stature, the thinking byte (30, 10, 0) and the game's battle bgm number (879)** are unread or unmapped, as in the note.

### T0 findings (each pinned by a test in `youth-league-engine.test.ts`; the numbers are what the engine produced)

1. **E1, an enemy command that mends a girl: it works with no engine change.** Yaibal's Hi-Potion heals Yuna by the row's fixed 1,000 (500 to 1,500) and stops at her maximum; his Phoenix Down stands a KO'd Yuna up at a quarter of her maximum HP (622 of 2,488) with the Death cure; on a living girl it does nothing. A heal is a damage event with a negative amount. The engine mends whoever the command names (the script aims only at Yuna).
2. **E3, Doom with a counter from a row: it works.** Harbinger lands Doom at once with the counter 4 (40,000 units) on every seed (60 of 60 on each girl), the blow is 171 to 190 on a Dark Knight and 332 to 369 on Yuna (668 on a critical), and **the girl is KO'd 10.5 seconds after the Doom lands, whoever she is** (the engine's duration model: the speed of the girl does not enter). **Nothing in the data cures Doom** (a Remedy leaves it on; the sources' blanket cures all exclude it, `research/ffx2-combat-core.md` §2.8); the answers are to finish Lucil or to raise the girl with a Phoenix Down. This is the one thing in the chapter that kills a girl, and the guide, the tactic and the intent board say so.
3. **The status layer against the level gap** (a status lands when `roll < chance + 5 x (attacker level - target level) - resist`): **the League's Flash Bomb (Slasher Lv 30, Elma Lv 36), Hush Grenade and the Mage's Confuse (Lv 23) can never land on the Chapter V party** (0 of 40 seeds each, every girl). Elma's Confuse and Sleep (Lv 36) land now and then (8 to 15 of 60, more on Yuna, the lowest level), the Gold Hourglass's Slow (Lucil Lv 43) lands on most (35 to 53 of 60). So the Mage's Demi branch (when every girl is confused) is unreachable at this level; the Sensor lines say "it will not blind anyone at your level" and promise no status. (Same finding as the Farplane Cup's T0 a.)
4. **The sizing of the research note holds on the engine**: Yaibal's Attack 81 to 91 on a Dark Knight and 159 to 176 on the White Mage; Lucil's Attack 171 to 190 and 332 to 369; Elma's Attack 121 to 134 and Fira 165 to 184 on a Dark Knight; the Veteran's Critical Attack is a critical every time (136 to 152, 266 to 298); the Mage's Fira 100 to 111, the Master's party Thundara 154 to 172, the Flash Bomb 47 to 52, Sorry 470 to 529. **The largest single hit of the chain is Lucil's, as the note says.**
5. **A girl Elma confuses acts for herself** (her Confused turns pick her own commands, Darkness and a Megalixir among them): the only way a party that only defends ever wins (2 of 100 seeds of act 6).
6. **The Gems** are six hits each (24 over the four), every hit 93 to 160 (the girls' own elemental weakness lifts some above the row's 105); Not-So-Mighty Guard puts Shell, Protect and Regen on Elma and both Veterans and on no one else; Dispel strips the girl it is aimed at; Osmose takes MP from the named girl and gives it to Elma.

At a human pace (the Wait split, 1.5 s a menu, 0.5 s on the top list; 40 seeds): ladder 27 of 40, **intended 40 of 40, darkness 40 of 40** (acts 1 to 6 all 40 of 40 on every line; act 7 ladder 33 of 40 with 8.3 KOs). With three retries (the checkpoint) the ladder clears 40 of 40. **What that says, plainly.** (a) **The chapter is a walkover**, as the guides say ("easy fights") and the research note computed: the sourced line wins every fight of every act with no girl down in about ten seconds an act. (b) **Lucil is the one real fight, and it is Harbinger's Doom** (10.5 s, no cure): the shipped generic ladder lets a girl die every fifth action and loses 12 of 100; the tactic registered for this chapter wins every seed. That is why the tactic exists; it is not a boss change. (c) The preflight's bar (an expected chain win of at least 90 percent at a human pace on the preset) holds for the tactic and the sourced line.

**The party as the only lever** (what-ifs, measured and not built; 50 seeds a cell, wins per act 1 to 7): the preset at **Lv 40/42/44** wins 50 of 50 everywhere (the other honest reading of "early in Chapter 5"); the **Chapter 3 build (Lv 32/33/34)** wins 50, 50, 50, 50, 48, 50 and **40 of 50** (act 5, four Veterans, 1.1 KOs a fight; act 7, Lucil, 2.9 KOs a fight). So a lower-level party is the harder chapter, and the tournament is soft only for the build the plan names.

Also not measured: a Gunner or Warrior line, Reflect on all three (Elma's Dispel branch), the Stop trick on Doom (its timer is frozen while the target is Stopped, `research/ffx2-combat-core.md` §2.8; Lucil is immune to Stop but the girl is not).
