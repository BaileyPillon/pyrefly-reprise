## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board. Type **`wraith`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself, with no card and no sound before it. The first W (`up`) moves the cursor one step, as the first letter of `summon` and `daigoro` does; the later letters (R is `r1`, A is `left`, I, T and H are unbound) are claimed by the word. 2. Party prep shows **one girl**: Yuna, Lv 54, a White Mage (the Den of Woe kit). **What that says, plainly.** Both duels are **winnable and not trivial**, and what separates a win from a loss is **healing in time, not the build**. A player who heals under half HP wins every seed in 2.4 to 3.5 game minutes and is never seen below about a quarter of her HP (the worst seed of the Gunner line reached 8 percent, the others 25 to 33); a player who never heals loses one chapter in two on the Den kit (Paine is the killer: duel 1 is won 197 times in 200, the chapter 100) and every chapter on the plain Chapter V preset (97 duel-1 wins, then nothing). The White Mage that never attacks never wins: with the generic ladder the move card said Pray, which is why the tactic exists (YB11). **Nothing was tuned**; the duels teach one thing (the girl is alone, nothing heals between the fights, so the bag is the party), which fits the wiki's account of them.
