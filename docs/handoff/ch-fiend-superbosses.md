## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board and type **`legion`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself. `E` is the board's `start` and is claimed by the word (a later letter of a live match); L, G, I, O, N are bound to nothing, so the last letter leaves no press latched. The word shares the prefix `le` with `leblanc` (the table forbids a word that begins another, not two that begin alike; the door test types both). On a phone, the long-press field on the "Chapter select" label takes the same word.
2. Enter starts the chapter: a pre scene (our draft), **act 1: Almighty Shinra** (275,000 HP, Lv 99, every stat 255, Regen and Spellspring), a seam of about 13.6 s with the player's hands off (the arena mends the girls), **act 2: the Major Numerus** (four heads, 555,555 / 666,666 / 666,666 / 777,777 HP, and a hidden body, pod and tail), the post scene and the results page. The fight in act 2 ends when the last head falls. A defeat on act 2 retries act 2; a defeat on act 1 starts the chapter over. Escape is off. A clear goes to the experiments' own store, never the save: no save change, no migration. The party is Chapter XIII's Lv 99 three.
3. **What a player will find: the party loses.** Shinra's first move lands 2.6 s in; act 2's first move 2.9 s in with no girl asked for a command at all (section 5).
