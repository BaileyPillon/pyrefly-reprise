## 1. Play it

1. From the title, reach the chapter-select board and type **`legion`** (any case, letters at most 2 s apart; the board shows no hint). It opens the chapter's party prep itself. `E` is the board's `start` and is claimed by the word (a later letter of a live match); L, G, I, O, N are bound to nothing, so the last letter leaves no press latched. The word shares the prefix `le` with `leblanc` (the table forbids a word that begins another, not two that begin alike; the door test types both). 2. Enter starts the chapter: a pre scene (our draft), **act 1: Almighty Shinra** (275,000 HP, Lv 99, Strength, Defense, Magic, Magic Defense and Agility 255, Regen and Spellspring), a seam of about 13.6 s with the player's hands off (the arena mends the girls), **act 2: the Major Numerus** (four heads, 555,555 / 666,666 / 666,666 / 777,777 HP, and a hidden body, pod and tail), the post scene and the results page. A clear goes to the experiments' own store, never the save: no save change, no migration. The party is Chapter XIII's Lv 99 three.
3. **What a player will find: the party loses.** Shinra's first move lands 2.6 s in; act 2's first move 2.1 s in (1.6 to 2.6), after a first menu on 160 of 200 seeds (section 5; the fix pass opened act 2 as the game opens a fight).

## 11. Known gaps, doubts, and the live checks the note asks for

- **The intent slab against the blows (cause found; shared preview code untouched).** At act 1's first menu the slab prints Clione as 1,071 / 511 / 1,071 per hit at "10 % HP"; the advisor note says "Clione hits for about 5,100"; the engine's blows are 6,015 to 9,999 on the Lv 99 line's HP.
