## The code

- **`src/engine/BeatClock.ts`** (new, pure): the presenter's waits are entries in the clock's own time; `BattleScreen.update(animDt)` ticks it beside the scene (the 1/40 s clamp), so a long frame delays beats and pictures together, nothing ticks it while the pause overlay is up or the window is hidden (the remaining time resumes exactly), `releaseAll()` on exit, stall or restart leaves nothing parked. A wait of 0 stays a macrotask yield (a resolved promise is a microtask). `BattlePresenterDeps.beatSleep` (new, optional) carries the scaled `ctx.sleep` and the turn cut-in's wait; `deps.sleep` (the pause gate, wall) still serves the ATB pump, the HUD-event budget and the script budget.
- **`src/engine/BeatLead.ts`** (new): the part of a frame a finished beat or an awaited tween did not need. `Tween` credits it on its first update, a new beat subtracts it from its length, so a chain of waits and moves keeps its true length at 60, 120 and 144 Hz (TB-04). Inert unless a battle screen has claimed it. **For lane L2:** `Tween.overshoot`, and `onComplete(overshootSec)`, are exposed, but the lunge legs joined by `.then` need NOTHING from you: the continuation of an awaited tween already starts its next tween that far in. Do not subtract the overshoot a second time.
- `Tween.ts`: `onComplete` now receives the overshoot (seconds); existing `() => void` callbacks are unchanged.
- `TurnCutIn.ts`: on the beat clock the wait for a charge polls once a frame (16 ms, was 60) and its 0.8 s cap is counted in BEAT-CLOCK time (`deps.now` = `PresenterDeps.beatNow`, which `BattleScreen` passes unless `?beats=wall`), so a pause inside the wait does not spend the cap (the first build counted real time: a pause moved the next girl's cut-in to her next turn, review m1). Without a beat clock (`?beats=wall`, tests) it is the old 60 ms poll and the summed cap, so `?beats=wall` is now the pure A/B.
- **MF-16** `ui/inkgold/cutin.ts`: one `slab.animate` with the two phases as keyframes (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.3, 1)` then `ease-out`), the way out starts from where the entrance is and removes the slab on `finished` (a fallback timer settles the promise in a hidden tab). **Left alone, site by site (the review asked for the reason of each, m6):** `wipe.ts` and `swirl.ts` hold the cover through the swap's own main-thread stall (`animationPlayState` paused by a timer, then running), so a one-shot compositor animation would let the panel leave during the swap; `MomentOverlay.nameSlab` is one CSS transition per direction (`data-on` toggled) with timers that only decide the hold and when the promise resolves, no second phase starts from a timer; `CutsceneStage.shake` is deliberate stepped motion (six timed steps). Four sites DO have the shape the plan names, a removal timer counted from the moment it is scheduled and not from when the fade starts: `OverdriveSplashLayer` (`is-out` at total minus 170 ms, finish at total), `BattleStartBanner.dismiss` (`setTimeout(finish, LEAVE_MS)`), `ChainCounter.break_` (`BREAK_MS`) and `SpherechangeFlourish` (`NAME_HOLD_MS`). A main-thread stall that lands between the class change and the first style pass shortens that fade by up to the stall (nothing is held late or lost; a fade is cut short). They are not built here: no stall measure exists for them and the effect is a clipped fade; the follow-up is `animationend` / `finished` as the removal event with the timer kept as the fallback, as `cutin.ts` does.
- **MF-07** `ui/ffx2/AtbLive.ts` (new), `PartyRows.partyRowHtml({ live })`, `FFX2BattleHud` (net -1 line). The model extrapolates each fill between the pump's snapshots (80 ms cap, a held clock stops it); the drawn bar is `ease`d toward it (30 ms time constant, may lead the model by one logic step, never steps back, a spent bar or the ready flash is drawn at once; **review fix m5:** a gap under 0.04 is closed no faster than 0.0003 of the bar a millisecond (`SLEW_RATE`), so the first step off a held 0 and the first frame after a hold are under 1 px; the first build's 1.40 px was the engine's 2.6 px jump closed 43 percent in one frame) and the rate is smoothed over snapshot pairs. **Why the easing:** the engine's gauge moves in 33 ms logic steps and the snapshots come every 50 ms, so the bare extrapolation held one frame in six (stepped capture); a modelled pump at 60, 120 and 144 Hz is the test (`tests/unit/atb-live.test.ts`).

## What was found on the way

- `.ffxhud.hud-f1 .ig-ctb__row { transform: none !important }` (and the phone rail): any future transform animation on a turn-order row is silently overridden; use `translate`, `scale` or `opacity`. Worth a note in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` when someone owns the HUD CSS.
- The ATB bars do not move under a command menu in the whole-menu hold (`?wait=hold`) or below the top list under the split: the clock is held there (that is the game's rule, `research/ffx2-combat-core.md` section 1.5). They move at the top list and in Active. In the card capture Paine's bar holds for about two seconds after the third menu opens, moves for about two seconds, then holds again (Yuna's Cure is casting; the pump awaits an effect that plays, so the cause of the two holds is probably that, but it was not isolated).
- The first command menu of Chapter IV finds all three party bars full, so the plan's "first menu left open 5 s, bars filling" shows nothing moving: the cards film the moment after two members have acted instead.
