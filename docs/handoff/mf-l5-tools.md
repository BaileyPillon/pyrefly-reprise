## What the real runs found (the browser jobs ran for the first time in this round; the rig was held by L1, L2, L3 and L4, so each waited 15 to 95 minutes)

Each of these was invisible to the unit tests and to the review, and each is fixed and tested; they are why "the tool exists" is not "the tool works".

1. **The action never ended (the factory's first real card).** In the booth build the presenter's `playing` stays true while the next command menu is open, so "the action is over" never fired: every moment ran to its 14 s timeout and every clip window to the 8 s cap. `isActing(frame)` now reads the phase (`play:*` against `command:*`). A clip of one attack is 2.6 s.
2. **The log is not the picture.** In FFX the engine logs `damage` in the same instant as `action-start`, so a strip anchored on the log event is anchored on the key press. `align`/`strip.beat` anchor on the target's shake starting (Chapter I: 25 frames after the key). The example spec also named a figure that does not exist (`seymour`; the id is `seymour-flux`), so its crop tracked Tidus only: fixed and now tested. For a contact card on a boss the union crop is wide: set `focus` to the target, or a fixed `crop`.
3. **The chained beats counted a frame of quantisation twice** (Chapter IV: the first beat is seen at 17 ms at 60 Hz and 7 at 144, so a beat 9 ms apart in truth read 19 against 16.7 allowed). They are now measured from the key press (the same instant at every rate), and an interval's slack is a frame of the slowest display plus a frame of the fastest (both ends are quantised); a scan of 11,000 phases and lengths finds no false failure.
4. **FFX-2's first menu ran the ATB clock in real time.** The default Wait split holds the clock only below the top level of the command menu, and `reachFirstMenu` waited 2.5 s of real time at it and then paused 1.5 s later: each capture began from a different game state (the 120 Hz capture of the first Chapter IV matrix had Bahamut acting at 1.2 s and Paine's strike 0.7 world units away, 102 px, constant through the run; 60 and 144 Hz agreed with each other to 0.6 px). It is not a motion difference between rates. The clock is now paused as soon as the menu is open (poll 40 ms, pause 100 ms ahead) and a card's `setup` runs after the pause. **Chapter I (turn-based, waits at the menu) was never affected.** Validation: see the next paragraph.
5. **A bare 10 percent jolt gate flags noise** (round 1: 433 and 458 jolts, two small classes moved 7 to 13 and 11 to 14; the gate was given a count-noise rule). **Round 2 corrected the reading:** those two captures and the reviewer's later 398 were two TOOL STATES (before and after the ATB clock was pinned, fix 4), not noise; after the pin the fight reproduces (R2-M2).
6. **A pacing number needs a quiet machine** (T-01 above).

**Validation of fixes 3 and 4 (Chapter IV again at 60, 120 and 144 Hz, `out/hz-ch4-v2`, the tool read at `ab899be13`):** Bahamut's cast is at **1200, 1200 and 1195 ms** in all three captures (the first matrix had it at 120 Hz only); the three rates share **14 common beats**; wall times within **1.3 percent**; chained beats **9 and 11 ms** against 16.7 allowed; Paine's position at the shared times within **8.6 px at 120 Hz (was 102.4)** and 24.8 px at 144 Hz (she runs at about 2 px a millisecond and her start is seen 9 ms apart: quantisation, reported not gated); jolts 26, 22, 27. **EQUAL at every rate.** Two `atb` snapshots (periodic gauge-clock bookkeeping) fell at -300 ms in the 60 Hz capture and at +1550 in the others while every motion beat agreed: they are no longer beats (`NOT_BEATS`: `atb`, `overdrive-gauge`, `status-tick`). **Residual, said plainly:** the ATB clock's phase at the first menu is still not pinned to the millisecond (those snapshots show it), because the real-time part before the pause is shorter but not zero. No motion beat of this moment depends on it. A card that waits for the gauge (an enemy's turn timed from the menu) is not reproducible between captures; pausing the fake clock at the start of the battle and stepping to the menu would fix that and was not built.
