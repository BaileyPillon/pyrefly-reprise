## R77-1 (FFX): "Spherimorph's results pay far less than its data and vary run to run"

**Traced on the engine first.** `payKill` (`src/battle/ffx/adapt/loot.ts`) reads the enemy's `rewards`; Spherimorph has no loot record, so `lootOfLegacy` pays the fields as a record of the same shape: gil 4,000, AP 3,240, `apOverkill` 4,860, the Lv. 2 Key Sphere (two on an overkill), with `overkilled` the only switch. Forty seeds, both a plain kill (Fire) and a Fira that reaches 2,000 on some seeds and not on others: gil is 4,000 on all, AP is 3,240 or 4,860 by the overkill alone, the Key Spheres one or two, and the same seed pays the same twice (`tests/unit/chapters/r78-spherimorph-rewards.test.ts`). Nothing in the path varies.

**Cause: the page, not the engine.** `ResultsScreen` rolls its counts up over `COUNT_UP_MS` (1,150 ms of the screen's own clock, longer in wall time on a slow frame; the review's GPU was shared), and `ledgerValueText` prints `round(value * p)`. The review read the text 1.5 s after the screen appeared. Its four readings are the same fraction of each true amount (gil and AP always at one `p`):

- text, run 1: GIL 347 and AP 422 are p = 0.08675 of 4,000 and of 4,860;
- text, run 2: GIL 985 and AP 1,197 are p = 0.24625 of 4,000 and of 4,860;
- the screenshot taken a moment before run 2's text (`shots/spherimorph-1600-07-end.png`): 464 and 563, p = 0.116;
- the Omega Ruins: 50,217 and 15,217 (screenshot) and 71,739 and 21,739 (text) are p = 0.3043 and 0.4348 of its 165,000 AP and 50,000 gil. **The review's "AP 71,739" for the Omega Ruins and its "33,000/22,000" are both mid-roll or derived from the full 165,000, so the true finals are 165,000 and 50,000.**
**Fix (the cause): the page says the settled amount.** `data-final="<amount>"` on every rolling count (the desktop ledger's AP/EXP and GIL values, each member's award; the phone page's tallies and chips: `finalAttr` in `src/ui/common/resultsPage.ts`), and `ResultsScreen.snapshot().paid` = `{ ap, exp, gil, countProgress }` (what `window.__pyrefly.snapshotState()` returns). Nothing a player sees changes: the text still rolls, no pixel moves. **For the next review: read `[data-final]` or `snapshotState().paid`, or wait for `countProgress` 1; never the text of the first seconds.** Tests: `tests/unit/r78-results-roll.test.ts` (the four readings are the true amounts at one `p` each; `p` 1 is exact; gil and AP are always the same fraction; the attribute on every frame, on both pages, absent on text values and on a defeat) and `tests/unit/r78-results-screen-roll.test.ts` (the real screen in jsdom: mid-roll text is a fraction, `data-final` and the snapshot say 4,000 and 4,860, then the roll lands exactly).

## R77-4 (FFX): Omega Ruins results show a Sphere Level bar past its cap ("S.LV 186 · 33,000/22,000 AP")

**Cause.** The three links of the Omega Ruins are one results screen (`poolsChainSpoils` on the last link; `BattleChainSpoils`): AP 50,000 + 60,000 + 55,000 = **165,000** (gil 50,000), and the Sphere Levels each link reported are added per member. Each link counted its levels from its own AP and threw the remainder away (two levels of 22,000 each, 6,000, 16,000 and 11,000 dropped), so a member's six levels leave 165,000 - 132,000 = 33,000 on the printed bar: a bar of a level and a half. Yuna and Lulu (who sat out a link, levels 4) read 77,000.

**Fix.** `src/ui/common/sphereGridBar.ts` (new, pure) computes the bar: the engine's levels, then the whole levels the printed banked AP still buys (`carried`), then the rest, and a last guard holds the bar at its cap (as FFX-2's dressphere bar holds at its cost). `buildMemberRows` prints from it: the Omega Ruins now read **S.LV 187 · 11,000/22,000 AP** (Tidus 180 + 7), 182 (Yuna), 177 (Lulu), and the chip and `standing` agree (+7). `carry = false` in `sphereGridBar(...)` gives the pure clamp (S.LV 186 · 22,000/22,000) in one word if the driver prefers the engine's level count held. Only an eligible member carries levels; a member who earned no AP prints what he printed. FFX only: the Sphere Grid is FFX's, FFX-2's rows read `dressphereDetail` and are untouched.

**Where it is inert, and where it is not (CORRECTED at the r78 integration; this paragraph first said "Inert elsewhere, proven", which was false).** Proven inert (`tests/unit/r78-results-sphere-bar.test.ts`): every shipped FFX chapter's results for a SINGLE link and a sweep of AP (a result whose levels are the engine's prints exactly what the old arithmetic printed, the same `S.LV`, rest and cap, the same chip: over 300 rows), a sweep of 3,000+ (level, AP, levels gained) cases with a bar inside its cap, and a bar that is never past its cap for any input, with or without carry; the old arithmetic is kept in the test as the control that prints 33,000/22,000. **NOT inert on a pooled chain whose links drop a remainder.** The review of this branch replayed the five chapters whose links are one results screen (`poolsChainSpoils`) and three of them change, because each link counted its levels from its own AP and dropped the remainder, so each printed a bar past its cap before: **the Mirror Aeons (chapter 27, a LISTED chapter): at the pooled 28,000 AP the old page read `S.LV 180 · 28,000/22,000 AP` with no level gained; the new page reads `S.LV 181 · 6,000/22,000 AP`, +1**; the Dark Aeons (34, hidden): the old page read `S.LV 182 · 106,000/22,000 AP`, +2, the new page `S.LV 186 · 18,000/22,000 AP`, +6; and the Omega Ruins (43, hidden): at the links' base AP of 145,000 the old page read `S.LV 185 · 35,000/22,000 AP`, +5, the new page `S.LV 186 · 13,000/22,000 AP`, +6. The Sinspawn Gui and the Flooded Ways print exactly what they printed. So the change DOES alter one listed chapter's results page (FFX only: the Mirror Aeons) and two hidden ones; it is a correction (the old bar was past its cap), not a new behaviour, and it is declared in `docs/CONTRACT-CHANGES.md` and in the lines for the CHANGELOG below. Pinned by `tests/unit/r78-results-sphere-bar-chains.test.ts` (the five chains, old against new; no new row past its cap; the two unchanged ones equal; the three changed ones exact; the AP there is each link's base AP with no overkill, an overkilled link pays more). FFX-2's rows read a dressphere's ability bar and are untouched.

**Known and left (not this finding):** a pooled chain prints every eligible member's award as the full pooled `result.ap`, as it always did; a member who sat out a link earned less in the game (`BattleResult` carries no per-member AP). The levels shown for him follow the printed AP, so the page agrees with itself; a per-member AP in `BattleResult` (an additive contract field) would make it true. Not built: it was not asked for.

## R77-5 (FFX-2 only, Chapter IV): Rikku's Dark Knight rig, worst one-frame jerk 137 px against 32 px

**The frame** (`continuity/ffx2-bahamut-win-6429ff15e/continuity/continuity.json`, jerk 0, strip `strips/jerk-000-rikku.jpg`): frame 19765, 333.18 s, Rikku, tracked point the head, step 137 px (dx 133, dy 32), at the `idle > attack` swap, **a rig swap blended over 4 frames (no hard cut), no camera cut in the run (`cameraCuts.count` 0), a normal frame (15.9 ms, `longFrame` false), the whole figure travelling (`commonMode`: the pose's own share of the step is `relPx` 28.8)**, `kind: dash`, which the harness reports and does not gate (CHK-027: "a mid-move dash is reported"; CHK-027 still PASS). The strip shows the lunge itself: Rikku stepping forward as her attack key folds in.

**What it is.** The rig run has 46 Rikku dashes (worst 137) and the painting run 0; the painting run has 26 Rikku **planned-smear swap jerks** (worst 88) and the rig run 4. Same swaps, classed differently: the painting set cuts at the lunge's fastest frame (a planned smear, excused), the rig blends across it, and the harness calls a travelling step during a blend a dash. Matching them swap by swap: `idle > attack` 76 to 88 px on the painting set (9 swaps), 120 to 137 px on the rig (10); the other swap kinds read alike (`idle > cast` 43 to 49 on both). The rig's head adds the attack key's forward lean on top of the body's travel: the same swaps' FEET step 93 px on the rig. The worst-jerk figure compared a smeared cut with a travelling dash, and the number moved with the class. The first analysis of the same dashes is in `docs/handoff/r77-art-install.md` ("Rikku's 40 dashes, read"); this closes the 137.

**Not the rig's.** `RigActor extends PaintedActor` and overrides none of the motion members; the lunge's fastest frame is at 0.19 of the move and is a large share of the reach by design (`PaintedActor.lunge`, "measured in the real game"). `tests/unit/rig/r78-rig-lunge-same-as-painted.test.ts` runs both actors with the same lunge and the same key change in the same frame and compares the body's travel frame by frame: identical to 9 decimals; the fastest step is identical; and it pins that the rig overrides none of `lunge`, `strike`, `recoil`, `hop`, `shake`, `squash`. **No change to the rig, its keys, the engine or the harness.** Left open, from the earlier reading and still true: the two vertical steps of 110 and 82 px at a turn start (frames 3008 and 3020 of the lane's run; here the no-swap 110 at frame 2979 and 82) want one look in motion (`?partyrigs=on` against `?partyrigs=off`, Chapter IV, Rikku's first turn); they are `dash` and not gated.
