The m3 sheet's MF-26 row says the same as their own clip notes: one clean figure on every frame where the painting set ghosts, **not smoother between two poses**: rigid pieces turned between two paintings and a cut.
2. **What v2 is.** The same method re-cut on the live paintings (r64) into new folders, so both pilots exist side by side for the clips: `D:/pyrefly-art-rigs/tidus-v2` and `D:/pyrefly-art-rigs/rikku-dark-knight-v2`, read only under `?partyrigs=v2`. 3. **The honest read (offline).** Each key IS its painting's costume (key-against-painting diff sheets, gate overlap 0.89 to 0.99). Heads are the idle's size at every key (mesh 0.9956 to 1.0048, opaque 0.9984 to 1.000). Stature and feet sit in the pose ruler's bands. The key changes read as a figure folding from one drawing into the other, one set of pieces, no ghost; the audit's 28 and 20 key changes show a few single frames where a piece comes away (worst: Tidus's far leg in follow>idle, 24 percent of the area, one frame). The rig does not beat the painting set's *drawing* quality between poses; it beats its see-through frames (the painting set's dissolve of the same pairs costs 2.6 to 4.6 frames of a second figure each). Whether that is a win is Bailey's call on the clips, in motion, as his rule says.
4. **Two things I could not settle offline** (open questions below): the dress-change twirl (a rig has no twirl keys and hides its carrier planes, which is where the flourish paints), and the stray pieces.

## Offline proof

- **Each key is its own painting's costume.** `party/keydiff.py` (sheets `D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-10-29/rigs-v2/keydiff-tidus-v2.png`, `keydiff-rikku-v2.png`): the rig's key drawn and compared with the painting in the idle's frame: mean colour difference 8.8 to 14.3 of 255 (Tidus), 9.3 to 25.2 (Rikku); the difference is the head (grown to the idle head's area: the CHK-026 rule, so a close-up KO head is smaller than its painting's), the 1 px edges of pieces stored at 0.3 to 0.6 of the master, and nothing else.
- **No bending of the idle into another costume**: no key is fitted from the idle (the retarget is exact on the keypoints; every piece of every key is cut from that pose's painting; the idle is the idle's).
- **Move audit** (`party/move_audit.py`, `audit-v2base-tidus-all.json`, `audit-v2base-rikku.json`, strips in `strips-v2base*/`; 28 and 20 key changes, easing and flip as the engine's, silhouettes at 2.5 px of the 1x painting): Tidus 16 of 28 changes have a frame with a stray piece (22 frames), 19 crack frames; Rikku 2 of 20 (2 frames), 1 crack frame. The strays by part (shard attribution): `legB:attack` (the cloth tail, ready>attack, idle>attack, attack>follow: 2 frames each of 4 to 5), `legB:follow` (the far leg, follow>idle, 24 percent of the area, 1 frame), `armA:own` and `sword:own` (the idle's own arm and sword, in idle>item, item>idle, idle>defend, defend>idle, hurt>idle: 4 to 11 percent), `armB:hurt` and `armB:critical` (hurt>critical, critical>hurt: 9 to 10 percent), Rikku `sword:cast` (idle>cast, 19 percent, 1 frame) and `legB:sleep` (idle>sleep, 45 percent, 1 frame). Mean cut pop (1 minus the silhouettes' overlap at the flip frame, same bones, the two pieces' sets): 0.45, from 0.34 to 0.60: **a cut is a different drawing; no flip point makes it small** (swept 0.1 to 0.9: mean 0.45 to 0.47). The painting set's dissolve of the same pairs costs 2.6 to 4.6 frames of a second figure each (`ghostEq`; 80.7 over Tidus's 24 first pairs), a cost a rig does not have.
- **The gate is blind to all of the above, on purpose of the first pilots' method**: joints on the outline, so `231 to 378 poses scanned for holes at 0 joints`. Making the pieces overlap more (underlap 12 to 28 and 5 to 24 px of the 1x painting) cut Tidus's stray frames from 22 to 9 and the worst stray from 24 to 6 percent (`audit-v2u-tidus.json`) **but the pieces then enclose six joints and the gate REFUSES the rig: 9 keys carry a hole at a joint** (a chest of 565 px in `hurt`, an upper arm of 461 px in `item`: the paintings are open there). The next lever is therefore not more overlap but joint discs and fills in the pieces, which is a rig-cut lane's work and the gate's call (see "Where it could go").
- **Pose ruler / identity**: the rig's gate stature, feet and head are the table above; the painting set's own pose ruler (`pose-measure.json`) is untouched. The identity measure of `tools/pose-identity.mjs` judges READY paintings of the painting set; for a rig the keys ARE the paintings (diff sheets above).
