3. **The honest read (offline).** Each key IS its painting's costume (key-against-painting diff sheets, gate overlap 0.89 to 0.99). Heads are the idle's size at every key (mesh 0.9956 to 1.0048, opaque 0.9984 to 1.000). Stature and feet sit in the pose ruler's bands. The key changes read as a figure folding from one drawing into the other, one set of pieces, no ghost; the audit's 28 and 20 key changes show, for Tidus, 15 single frames where a piece comes away (worst: his idle's own sword in hurt>idle, 11 percent of the area, one frame; the idle's own arm in the item, defend and attack moves, 4 to 5 percent) and none for Rikku. The rig does not beat the painting set's *drawing* quality between poses; it beats its see-through frames (the painting set's dissolve of the same pairs costs 2.6 to 4.6 frames of a second figure each). Whether that is a win is Bailey's call on the clips, in motion, as his rule says.
4. **Two things I could not settle offline** (open questions below): the dress-change twirl (a rig has no twirl keys and hides its carrier planes, which is where the flourish paints), and the stray pieces.

## Offline proof

- **Each key is its own painting's costume.** `party/keydiff.py` (sheets `D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-10-29/rigs-v2/keydiff-tidus-v2.png`, `keydiff-rikku-v2.png`): the rig's key drawn and compared with the painting in the idle's frame: mean colour difference 8.8 to 14.3 of 255 (Tidus), 9.3 to 25.2 (Rikku); the difference is the head (grown to the idle head's area: the CHK-026 rule, so a close-up KO head is smaller than its painting's), the 1 px edges of pieces stored at 0.3 to 0.6 of the master, and nothing else.
- **No bending of the idle into another costume**: no key is fitted from the idle (the retarget is exact on the keypoints; every piece of every key is cut from that pose's painting; the idle is the idle's).
- **Move audit** (`party/move_audit.py`, `audit-final-tidus-v2.json`, `audit-final-rikku-dark-knight-v2.json`, one strip per key change in `strips-final-tidus/` and `strips-final-rikku/`; 28 and 20 key changes, the engine's easing, frame count and half-way flip (pinned by `rigs-v2-audit-agree.test.ts`), silhouettes at 2.5 px of the 1x painting): **Tidus 11 of 28 changes have a frame with a stray piece (15 frames in all) and 17 crack frames; Rikku 0 of 20 and 1 crack frame (0.018 of the area).** The strays, by the part that makes them (the audit names it): `armA:own` (the idle's own sword arm coming off the shoulder, 4 to 5 percent of the area, 8 frames in idle>item, item>idle, idle>defend, defend>idle, idle>attack), `sword:own` (the idle's own sword, hurt>idle, 11 percent, 1 frame), `legB:attack` (a cloth tail, ready>attack and attack>follow, 2 percent, 1 to 2 frames; the strip shows it as a blue fragment under the figure), `armB:critical` (1 percent, 2 frames). Mean cut pop (1 minus the silhouettes' overlap at the flip frame, same bones, the two pieces' sets) 0.47 for Tidus and 0.45 for Rikku, 0.35 to 0.62 over the pairs: **a cut is a different drawing; no flip point makes it small** (swept 0.1 to 0.9 on the first pilot: mean 0.45 to 0.47). The painting set's dissolve of the same pairs costs 2.6 to 4.7 frames of a second figure each (`ghostEq`), a cost a rig does not have. Largest one-frame centroid step outside a revive: 0.109 of Tidus's height (21 px at 190) and 0.137 of Rikku's (23 px at 170); the revive (`ko>idle`) 0.25 and 0.27 (48 and 46 px).
- **The gate is blind to all of the above, because of the first pilots' method**: joints on the outline, so `231 to 378 poses scanned for holes at 0 joints`. What v2 changed with it: the pieces of a pose reach 28 px (1x) under their neighbours instead of 12 (`underlap` in `gen_v2_cfg.py`; the idle's own pieces keep 5). Measured on Tidus before it was adopted: stray frames 22 to 15, worst stray 24 to 11 percent, crack frames 21 to 17 (`experiments/`: `tidus-v2x` is this; `tidus-v2w`, 20 px: 20 stray frames; the baseline numbers are `audit-v2base-*.json`); on Rikku 2 stray frames to 0. **Past that the gate refuses the rig**: with the idle's own underlap at 10 the pieces enclose six joints, the gate starts to watch them and REFUSES (`tidus-v2y`), and at 24 nine keys carry a hole at a joint (a chest of 565 px in `hurt`, an upper arm of 461 px in `item`: the paintings are open there; `tidus-v2u`, which also cut the stray frames to 9). So the idle's own strays (`armA:own`, `sword:own`) cannot be cut further this way: the next lever is joint discs and fills in the pieces, a rig-cut lane's work and the gate's call ("Where it could go"). Cost of 28: 0.7 Mpx and 1.0 MB more for Tidus, 0.6 Mpx and 0.9 MB for Rikku.
- **Pose ruler / identity**: the rig's gate stature, feet and head are the table above; the painting set's own pose ruler (`pose-measure.json`) is untouched. The identity measure of `tools/pose-identity.mjs` judges READY paintings of the painting set; for a rig the keys ARE the paintings (diff sheets above).
