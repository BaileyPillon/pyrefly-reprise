## 4. The three lighter options (`?skygrade=a|b|c`; nothing plays by default)

The word reaches Chapter V and Chapter XI only, only while the treatment reaches them (`?treated=pack` and an address that chose a look by hand answer release 68), and plays the held `hd2d` look and `arena` grade with one change on the room's pro table (the table that failed the read; the shipped rows are never written: a test reads them back after every option). `__pyrefly.skyGrade('a'|'b'|'c'|null)` switches live on a page that opened with the word (the LUT key carries it); `skyGrade(null)` plays the r70 grade unpatched on the same page and the same frame: the **control arm**. `?pro=off` with the word reads the bold look alone (the brightest row of the r70 read).

| option | idea | change on the room's pro table | what it does to the picture (predicted) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a** exposure cap | one lever | exposure capped at 1.0 (the rows are 1.45 and 1.5) | the whole sky falls: the pro colour transform is steep (a gamma of 0.26, the exponent 3.85), so 1.0 against 1.45 is about a quarter of the light at the same input; the burst's core is what is left bright |
| **b** highlight roll-off | one lever | a soft knee on the colour transform (`ProColour.shoulder` 0.5, tanh above it; the rows have none) and exposure capped at 1.2 | no clip at all up to four times the live sky: the burst keeps its gradation, the plate reads as a moody dusk |
| **c** dimmer bold row | one lever | the added-light terms come down: beams 2.0 to 0.8, stage light 1.7 to 1.2, bloom strength 0.85 to 0.45 and threshold 0.66 to 0.8, figure key 1.5 to 1.3, the cone 0.7 of itself, exposure capped at 1.2 | the burst throws less light into its own sky; the grade still clips where the sky's own pixels are bright |
