# Art handoff — group `ffx2-bosses`

The Final Fantasy X-2 endgame: the possessed Bahamut of the Bevelle
Underground, Vegnagun's four fight parts, Shuyin, and Lenne. Everything here
was rendered through `tools/gen/comfy.mjs` against the shared style contract
(`STYLE_TAGS` / `QUALITY_TAGS` untouched) on Animagine XL 4.0 Opt, cut out with
`isnet-anime`, and judged per `docs/ART-PIPELINE.md` §6.

*(Draft — final choices are logged in the tables below as each subject lands.)*

## `cast.json` changes made first

Per the task, `tools/gen/cast.json`'s single `vegnagun-core` entry was replaced
by two subjects, and the four parts were reordered into the fight order the
bible gives (tail → leg → body → head):

| Subject | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `vegnagun-tail` | the segmented abdomen that plants into the terrain to draw Farplane energy |
| `vegnagun-leg` | the colossal jointed limb, flanked by the three coloured Nodes |
| `vegnagun-body` | **new** — the colossal torso: organ-pipe cannon bank, grated chest, violet core, two casting arms |
| `vegnagun-head` | **new** — the horned machina skull with the mouth cannon |
