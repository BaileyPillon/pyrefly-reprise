## Live tuning and switches (the captures use them)

`__pyrefly.look.param('living', 0)` takes the whole living cathedral away; `living.beam`, `.lamps`, `.ripple` scale one part, and `.gain`, `.size`, `.glow`, `.spill`, `.amp`, `.ramp`, `.glint` one number each. `flare` is the Mega Flare's master (`flare.flood`, `flare.white` each part). `__pyrefly.look.stats().living` and `.flare` read the state.
