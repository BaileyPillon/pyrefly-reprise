# Booth script: Echoes of Spira

One page. Read it once. Keep it by the keyboard. (How the PC is set up: [DEMO-KIT.md](DEMO-KIT.md).)

**Start of day:** double-click `start-demo.cmd`. The game opens full screen on the title. Press Enter. The board opens on Chapter I.

## The 20-second pitch

"This is Echoes of Spira. It is my unofficial fan tribute to the boss fights of Final Fantasy X and X-2. It is painted, 2.5D, and lit like a modern HD-2D game. It runs in a browser. Nothing to install. I have two fights today, one from X and one from X-2. Want to drive?"

## Fight 1: Chapter I, Seymour Flux (Final Fantasy X). A few minutes.

1. **The opening.** Enter on the chapter, Enter on START BATTLE. A story scene plays first. Hold Enter to skip it. Tidus moves first. That is the demo setting.
2. **The look.** Do not press anything yet. Point: the ice behind is soft, the fighters are sharp, there are shafts of light and drifting sparks, and each fighter has a rim of light.
3. **A spell.** Cast Lulu's Black Magic or Yuna's Cure. The light on the floor and on the party changes colour. Say: "The spell lights the room."
4. **The Overdrive moment.** When a fighter's Overdrive bar is full, the OVERDRIVE row lights up. Use it. Let the visitor watch.
5. **The win.** The results panel comes up. Press Enter once. The board returns, already on Chapter IV.

## Fight 2: Chapter IV, Bahamut (Final Fantasy X-2). Two to three minutes.

1. **The opening.** Same keys. Yuna, Rikku and Paine. The bars fill, and a command holds them. The room is violet with amber lamps. The floor shines. Embers drift over the lamps.
2. **A spell.** Cast one. Same pool of light.
3. **The Mega Flare moment.** Bahamut counts down 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, then fires Mega Flare at all three. Say: "You always see it coming." Use the free turns to heal. With sensible healing the girls come through it.
4. **The win.** The results panel is quiet on purpose. Chapter IV ends without a fanfare. Press Enter. The board returns on Chapter I.

## The Pilgrimage (only if someone asks for more)

It is a hidden prototype. On the chapter board, type `pilgrim`. Nothing appears on screen. A road of four boss fights opens. After each win you take one of three boons for the next fight only. Lose and the run ends. Press Again to go once more. Say: "This is a prototype. I am testing it. It is not final."

## Controls

- **Move:** arrow keys or W A S D. **Pick:** Enter. **Back:** Esc.
- **Pause:** P, or Esc at the top of the battle menu. **Skip a story scene:** hold Enter. **Speed up a fight:** hold R.
- **Mouse:** click anything. **Gamepad:** A picks, B goes back, Start pauses.
- No help cards appear in the demo. You are the guide.

## If something goes wrong

- **A fight looks wrong or stuck:** press F5. The page reloads on the title with the demo setting kept. That fight is gone. Nothing else is lost.
- **Still wrong:** show the other fight. Chapter I and Chapter IV do not depend on each other.
- **The screen is frozen or black:** press Alt+F4. Double-click `reset-demo.cmd`. It takes about five seconds. The game opens fresh on the title.
- **The keyboard does nothing:** press Ctrl+Alt+Del, open Task Manager, end the browser. Double-click `reset-demo.cmd`.
- **A visitor loses:** the results panel offers RETRY. Take it. The retry is a different roll of the same fight.

**Between visitors:** `reset-demo.cmd` gives the next person an empty save. **End of day:** `stop-demo.cmd`.
