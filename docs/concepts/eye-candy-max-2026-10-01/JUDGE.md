# Judges' verdicts, MAX options round (2026-10-01)

Four independent judges scored the four prototypes (candy-max-proto: A abaf3e6d, B c54cf203, C d3b6ad36, D 9ef54c22) against release 33 (main f302f163). Each looked at the stills, strips, wide frames, on/off sheets and clips. Scores are out of 10. This is the record; the page for Bailey condenses it.

| Judge | A Living Canvas | B Painted Motion | C Diorama Director | D Sculpted Figures (departure) | Pick |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Models, animation, camera | 6.5 | 7.7 | 7.6 | 6.0 | C + B |
| Showpiece and fidelity | 6.0 | 6.8 | 7.4 | 4.8 | C as frame, A after the blackout fix, then B in art rounds |
| Comfort and readability | 7.6 | 7.2 | 6.2 | 4.6 | A as base after the blackout fix, plus B's spherechange keys and hero shot, plus C's static master fixes |
| Craft and cost | 7.3 | 6.2 | 7.0 | 3.6 | A + C base, plus B's no-render parts |
| **Average of four** | **6.85** | **6.98** | **7.05** | **4.75** | Mix; not D |

Consensus: nobody picks D. Nobody finds the fights "maxed" today; a mix gets there. A has one critical prototype fault (the scene blacks out on hits) that must be fixed before it ships. C's masters need re-staging in Ch I, Ixion, Evrae and Vegnagun. B's pilot keys drift off-model and need identity locking, and B's painted sets are a phased art program.

Caveats common to all four: the Tidus Overdrive stills used an injected gauge (labelled in the manifest); no real phone GPU was measured (phone rows are a 4x CPU throttle only); all four prototypes are real code and nothing was faked.

## Judge 2: showpiece and fidelity

Verdict: none of the four prototypes would make Bailey feel the fights are "maxed" today. A mix can get there. Looked at every option's stills against today's at desktop size, plus phone stills, the wide frames, the strips, C's on/off sheet, and frames from all eight clips at 10 fps (`D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-09-30-visual/judge-showpiece/`).

- **C (Diorama Director), 7.4.** The largest perceptible gain: Natus and Evrae read as colossi, the Syndicate stands up, and the FFX-2 spherechange gets a close pink pop shot of Yuna as Gunner. Lighting and defringe help integration. Strengths also: colossus masters (low camera, up to 9 degrees up) with FFX's weighty monumental feel; the FFX-2 Leblanc field diorama; Overdrive hero shot; normal-map relighting, contact shadows, boss light spill, fog bands, defringe (Vegnagun's halo gone, VP-17) and SMAA; cuts held at least 1.2 s with the HUD pinned (D-291); Ch I's gap -216 to +71 px; no new paintings. Must fix: at Vegnagun's rest the party is out of frame (desktop and phone; the master must keep the party whole and on the left, checked by an automated framing assert at rest); at Evrae the party sits behind the command menu; Ixion's 30-degree tilt shrinks the party to specks; side shots and ALL ON drop Flux out of frame; the VP-15 white column survives and overlaps the HUD coach card, and the Bahamut Impulse card stacks on the coach card; Natus: Mortibody's scythe overlaps Kimahri and the NEXT BEST MOVE card sits over Natus's head (add a figure/HUD-card overlap check); the party reads small in BFA, Yojimbo and Evrae; C's own Sin master rendered black; phone side and windup shots are framed for the full canvas; verify phonefill with a capture; wire the flags to an EYE CANDY settings sub-page (mockup first, D-297).
- **A (Living Canvas), 6.0.** The only option that brings figures to life without new art: breathing, hair and cloth springs, blink layers; the KO buckle before the hard cut and the victory dip/lift/settle read well; defringe, the 1.15x stretch guard and the Ch I/IV gap fix (-243 px to +48); FFX-2 at 0.8x periods with lighter springs inside today's action length; REDUCE MOTION zeroes everything; about 0.1 ms; stacks with C. Must fix: CRITICAL, the canvas blacks out on hits for about 400 ms (clip A FFX frames 21-24 and 31-35, mean luminance 15-21 vs 60; the FFX-2 boss strip at +1254 ms; the Vegnagun wide frame is fully black, mean 1.1; prime suspect the a2c 4x-MSAA composer targets interacting with eye candy D's Spectacle hit or impact pass; prove it gone on every chapter's hit); FFX-2 Ch IV staging pushes White Mage Yuna half out of frame and strands Paine (D-167); Ch I spacing crowds Tidus into Kimahri; the gains are too subtle in stills (show a side-by-side loop, raise amplitude where comfort allows); Yuna's Gunner blink leaves residue; no arm-only motion; capture the uncaptured Ch VII, II, V and VI moments.
- **B (Painted Motion), 6.8.** Highest ceiling for animation and enemy models. Stepped painted keys on twos (anticipation, contact, smear, follow-through) are the true anime vocabulary; the Overdrive hero cut and splash crops come from approved paintings; FFX-2's spherechange gets stepped twirl keys and a gold ring, and the white plane, wash and column (VP-15) are removed; the smear lands on the hit frame (VP-06); the 2x Tail is crisper with a thinner halo; about zero per-frame cost; REDUCE MOTION degrades to cuts. Must fix: the pilot keys are off-model (Tidus's orange vest, different costume, long staff-like blade; Yuna Gunner loses her second pistol), so no key may be shown as an end state or installed until identity matches the approved idles; in the hero cut the target label stays pinned to its old screen spot and floats over Yuna's staff (hide or re-anchor world-space labels during held cuts); only Tidus and Yuna Gunner are keyed, so most chapters look like today; the Vegnagun Tail img2img makes blocky noise at every denoise, and the Tail is still below the 1.5 texel/px floor at 2x DPR; the OD overlay names Slice & Dice (VP-16); the spherechange time halt only freezes figures while the ATB bars still fill (Bailey's decision); KTX2 for about 250 paintings needs an encoder download and phone-tier memory planning.
- **D (Sculpted Figures), 4.8.** The departure, and it hurts the fights. Real self-shadowing (colossi shadow the party via a 2048 shadow map) is the closest any option gets to "models"; wing, tail and fin hinge bends; keypoint in-betweens on Tidus; about +0.1 ms; labelled a departure. Must fix: it departs from the settled painted 2.5D direction (rule 9); the three-quarter shot at command loses Seymour Flux entirely; the shoulder or reverse shot puts the boss on the left (D-167) and visibly smears the paint; paint smears off-axis in the in-betweens (Tidus's frames at +148 and +245 ms read as an orange blob); the phone and LOW EFFECTS tiers keep flat paintings; at rest the wide frames look almost like today's; keypoints hand-placed for 7 paintings only.

Game feel (rule 14): C's colossus masters and low angles suit FFX's weight and its spherechange close shot suits FFX-2's pop; B's Overdrive choreography is FFX and its spherechange keys are FFX-2, both on-brief; A's FFX-2 tempo (0.8x periods) respects Active ATB; D treats both games identically, against rule 14's spirit.

Pick: **C as the frame, A layered on top once the blackout is fixed, then B's paintings in phased art rounds**, each shown to Bailey before install and on-model. Not D. This matches the design doc's recommendation with stricter gates. Provisional sub-area outlook if C+A ship clean: camera about 7.5 to 8.6, enemy models about 7.1 to 8.2, animation about 6.2 to 7.4; B's art rounds are needed to push animation and enemy models past 9. Measurement caveats: no real phone GPU measured; the Tidus Overdrive stills used an injected gauge.
