Vegnagun's core. Charges energy, then unleashes a devastating attack; can also revive both Bulwarks — make it the primary target.

Body and Bulwarks
Two Darknesses clear both Bulwarks and chunk the Core. Do not hit a Bulwark twice with the same damage class — they mirror it back.

When you see “Charge Core”
Three unimpeded Core turns and it fires — killing a Bulwark makes the Core waste its turn on Full-Life and stops the counter.
