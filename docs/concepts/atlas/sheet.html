An interactive learning site: three things to take apart

Pyrefly Reprise · end-state options for Bailey · pick one, mix them, or say what to change in each

Every option keeps the same nine-part pattern from the two reference sites: one specimen on a stage, a parts panel on the left, the pull-it-apart slider at the bottom, a detail card with its source on the right, isolate, search, a view rail and credits. Only the subject and the look change.

A
Pyrefly Atlas: the bosses, taken apart
Takes apart a boss (here Vegnagun, chapter V) into its parts, moves, statuses, immunities, turn patterns and rewards · you learn what the boss is made of, which pieces are dangerous and how to answer each one.
FFX-2 specimen · pyre pinkLight, clinical lookBuild: medium

A2Five parts apart, move cards on threads, Body / Core selected with “How to answer it” open.
A3119 pieces: 12 part tiles, then a wall of move, status and immunity chips.
B
Pyrefly Studio: one turn, taken apart
Takes apart one turn of battle (Tidus attacks Seymour Flux, chapter I) into eight rule steps, computed by the game’s own engine · you learn why a fight behaves as it does: turn order, hit, damage, status, Overdrive.
FFX specimen · Yevon goldDark studio lookBuild: large
B1One turn as one object, eight numbered pins. Small pink card: how the list changes for FFX-2.
B2Eight step cards around the stage; Turn order selected, with a live “drag Tidus’s Agility” control.
B3137 rules as text tiles in three sizes, hover card on Zombie. No pictures on this wall.
C
Pyrefly Reprise, exploded: the game, taken apart
Takes apart one finished frame of the game (Lady Yunalesca, chapter II) into painted layers, the HUD, the engine, the presenter and the sound · you learn how a painted 2.5D browser game is put together.
