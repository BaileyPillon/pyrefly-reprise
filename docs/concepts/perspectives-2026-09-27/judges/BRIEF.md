# Blind judge panel: battle perspectives (brief)

Three judges, each with one player persona, look at the same frames in a shuffled order under neutral
labels (`F01`...`Fnn`). They are not told the option names, the shot spec, the cost or the main
session's own ranking, so the scores estimate how a player would perceive each frame and nothing else.

## Personas

1. **The veteran.** Played FFX and FFX-2 on PS2 and again in the HD Remaster. Cares that it feels like
   those games, and that a boss fight feels like an event.
2. **The modern JRPG player.** Recently played Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, Metaphor: ReFantazio,
   Honkai: Star Rail, Octopath Traveler II. Judges production value against those.
3. **The first look.** Has never played either game. Sees one frame as a store screenshot or a trailer
   still, on a phone or a laptop, and decides in two seconds whether it looks premium.

## What each judge returns, per frame

- `eyeCandy` 1-10: would I screenshot or share this; does it look like a premium 2026 game.
- `readability` 1-10: can I tell who is who, which side is mine, what I would target, where the menu is.
- `cardboard` 1-10: 10 = the painted figures sit in the world; 1 = they read as flat cut-outs.
- `bossPresence` and `heroPresence` 1-10.
- one sentence: the first thing I noticed, good or bad.

Then a forced ranking of the top five and the bottom three, with a reason each, and one paragraph:
which two frames would you want the whole game to look like, and why.

## Scoring rules

- Judge the frame, not the art quality of any one painting: some frames use new candidate paintings.
- Do not reward a frame for being darker, blurrier or emptier unless that genuinely looks better.
- A frame that hides the menu or the party's health is still judged as a battle screen.
- 10 is rare. Use the whole scale.
