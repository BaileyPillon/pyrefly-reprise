How to play · page 1 of 2 · the two games
Same world, different clock
Chapters I–III · Final Fantasy X
Turn order
The list on the right. It is the fight. A slow move pushes you down it; a fast one can buy two turns before the boss gets one.
Overdrive
A bar that fills when you take hits. Full, it unlocks one very large move on that character's turn.
Aeon
Yuna calls one in and it fights alone, with its own health, until it falls or you dismiss it.
Chapters IV–V · Final Fantasy X-2
The gauge
There is no turn order here. Each girl has a bar that fills in real time; full means she may act. All three fill at once.
Dressphere
A job she can change into mid-fight. Different commands, different bar length — the change itself costs her that turn.
Chain
Hits landed close together count up together. Keeping the count alive is worth more than any single big move.
