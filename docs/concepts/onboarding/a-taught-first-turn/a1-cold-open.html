An unofficial fan tribute
Five boss fights,
two different games
Each chapter drops you into one famous fight, already at full strength. No grinding, no map. Pick a chapter, read the room, win it.

Chapters I–III — Final Fantasy X

Turn-based. Nothing moves until you choose. A list on the right shows who acts next, and it changes with what you pick.

Chapters IV–V — Final Fantasy X-2

Real time. Each girl has a gauge that fills whether or not you are ready. Three at once, and it does not wait.
