# Macalania Temple — Seymour & Anima (FFX) — end-state options

**Nothing here is built or wired into the game.** This is hard-rule-9 concept art for
one of the three new chapters Bailey approved on 2026-09-19. Pick or mix per row; a
pick approves only the properties named, not the whole image (AGENTS.md rule 15).

`sheet.png` — four rows, options A/B/C left to right. `options.json` has the full
canon notes and anything off-canon spotted while judging.

## Questions for Bailey, one per row

1. **Backdrop** — which light treatment for the antechamber outside the Chamber of
   the Fayth: A (warm brazier gold), B (cold blue, deep ramp), or C (twilight, red
   accents)? Or a mix (e.g. A's braziers with B's depth)?
2. **Boss — Seymour** (robed, human form, **not** the Flux mount) — A (cold blue), B
   (arms crossed, warm rim), or C (cold cyan, high contrast)?
3. **Cast — Guado Guardian** — A (bare-faced reroll, closest to the research's "elf
   ears, pronounced veining"), B (masked, carries a lantern), or C (masked, cool
   silhouette)? Note: B and C hide the face, which loses the steal-a-potion comedy
   beat the research calls out — flag if that matters to you.
4. **Cast — Anima** — no new options rendered. The research states Seymour's Anima
   is the **same creature**, just a shorter in-game model than Yuna's. Recommend
   reusing the already-approved `public/art/characters/anima/idle.png` at a smaller
   scale rather than painting a new one. Say yes/no.

## What went off-canon during rendering (full detail in `options.json`)

- The Guado Guardian's first roll (tagged `1other`) came back as a fox-eared
  kemonomimi creature with an animal mask — not a Guado at all. It was **discarded**,
  not shipped as an option, and re-rolled once (the one reroll the brief allows) under
  `1boy` — that reroll is option A.
- Guardian options B and C drifted toward a masked/helmeted temple-guard look rather
  than the bare-faced Guado retainer the research describes.
- Nothing else seen was disqualifying.
