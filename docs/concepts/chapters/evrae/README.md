# Evrae — the Fahrenheit's foredeck (FFX) — end-state options

**Nothing here is built or wired into the game.** Hard-rule-9 concept art for the
approved Evrae chapter. Pick or mix per row; a pick approves only the properties
named, not the whole image (AGENTS.md rule 15).

`sheet.png` — two rows (this chapter has no supporting-cast render — see below).
`options.json` has the full canon notes and anything off-canon spotted while judging.

## Questions for Bailey, one per row

1. **Backdrop** — which foredeck composition: A (reroll, still reads as a distant
   flying city rather than the deck we stand on — flagged, not fixed), B (looking up
   at the hull from the rail), or C (golden hour, ship visible past the rail)? B is
   the closest canon match; A is shown for completeness but is not recommended as-is.
2. **Boss — Evrae** — A (NEAR silhouette, head-and-claws filling the frame), B (FAR
   silhouette, long diagonal streak — the research explicitly wants this one for the
   **chapter card**), or C (throat-charge framing, for the Poison Breath telegraph
   beat)? These three could also map directly onto three different in-game states
   rather than being a single either/or pick.

## What went off-canon during rendering

- The backdrop's first roll and its one allowed reroll (option A) both came back as
  a bird's-eye shot of a flying city with the airship reduced to a distant speck,
  not the open foredeck with a rail in the foreground that the brief asks for. Not
  re-rolled a second time, per the one-reroll-per-item cap — flagged instead.
- No cast render was queued: per the brief, Evrae's chapter has no supporting cast
  beyond the airship itself (Cid is invisible in the real encounter).
