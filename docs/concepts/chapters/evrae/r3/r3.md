## The decisions and why

1. **Backdrop: install, not rebuild.** B needed nothing. The scene's roll (-11.6 degrees)
   was fitted to the old plate's rail. With B, the rolled plate still reads as sky past our
   rail (captured, sheet row 5). If the rail geometry should be re-fitted to B, that is a
   `src/scenes` change for its owner. It was not done here, and the framing does not need it.
2. **Breath-charge: re-derive, not transplant.** The old sac is the part the judge failed
   (flat vector disc, style 6), so transplanting it would carry the defect over. The derive
   keeps every pixel on model. **One seam-band repaint was tried** (`seam_repaint.py`,
   Animagine XL 4.0 Opt, masked img2img at denoise 0.35 on a 1024 crop, seed 830301, 8.6 s).
   It invented a sparkle and a row of tooth-like dots inside the sac, so it was dropped and
   the second seed was cancelled. The installed file has **no generated pixels**. What
   remains: at 2x, a soft horizontal band where the glow meets the teal side scales. The
   lower edge of the sac is the idle's edge stretched 1.8x and cleaned by a morphological
   open. **No source gives the glow colour.** Research §12.2 asks for "a swelling, a colour
   shift" and keeps the saturated accent for the throat, but names no colour. The orange
   follows the earlier candidate, and Bailey may want another colour (hard rule 6).
3. **Hurt: the derived file is kept over "none".** The old hurt was judged 6 (below 7), so it
   was replaced. At game size (sheet row 3, the two in-game crops), the pose swap moves the
   head about 60 screen px up and back: a visible recoil on top of the engine's flash and
   shake. "None" is the idle under the same flinch with no pose change. The agent judged
   the derived file the clearer hit. The risk is that a raised head can also read as
   rearing, so this is a close call for the independent judge. If the judge scores a tie,
   move `hurt.png` and `hurt.json` out (back them up, then run `node tools/gen/manifest.mjs`)
   and the engine's `hurt -> idle` fallback applies.
4. **Cast candidate** (`cast-candidate.png`, NOT installed, for Decision 1). The head is
   reared 60 px and pitched snout-down at the party, with the idle's own open jaws, by the
   same layer derive. Every Evrae action is kind `ability` and maps to the cast pose, which
   today falls back to the idle. Installing it is Bailey's call.
