# Pose continuity: the permanent fix (paper preflight, 2026-10-16)

Proposal only: nothing is built, every part needs Bailey's yes (rule 10). This is the rule-15 preflight and the method check PR-0380 is owed. Inputs: `angle1-art.md`, `angle2-runtime.md`, `angle3-process.md`, `r50-1.md`, the Quality Leap survey. Tree r50.1 `6b638df95`, art r50. Costs are agent-day estimates.

## Why it keeps coming back
Nobody owns the contract "one figure, one size, one pair of feet in every painting". Generation checks the cutout, install is 17 scratch scripts (none registers), deploy checks bytes, the one pose check sees only subjects already measured, and the engine corrects each pose with one scalar, which matches one length: the head (D-420: Lulu hurt at 0.67) or the height (r50.1: head x1.35). 55 of 151 out-of-band poses cannot satisfy both. CHK-026 reads the engine's own table and passes by construction; new art ships under three exemptions (new subject, "major", regression "unknown"); the owner override cleared 24 of 25 deploys. On r50: 205 of 622 game poses break a band, 105 have no row, five Lady Luck rows plant the figure at the knees (drawn at 0.43 to 0.49), and the Dark Aeons had no stance (239 swaps over the feet line, FOC501-01).

## 1. One source of truth per figure, with bands (both games)
- **Design.** The chain stays (ruler readings in `pose-measure.json`, generated `poseRegistration*.ts`), but each row also stores what the game draws, computed at install by one model of `PaintedScale` + `PoseRegistration` + `HeadLock` (angle 1's: within 0.05 of the record on 335 of 341 poses, Lulu hurt 0.907 against 0.903 in game): `class`, `scale`, `stanceX`, `feetRow`, `head`, `stature`, `headReal`, `lengthRatio` (KO), `iou` with the idle, `status` (`ok | waived | blocked`). The runtime sizes a character painting from that row alone; the sidecar `scale`/`anchorY` and the KO table become install inputs.
- **Ruler per class, chosen once.** Install derives `scale` from one ruler per class (head, height or their geometric mean) and checks both readings against the band. Bailey picks the ruler once, on pictures (Lulu hurt, Paine Songstress hurt, Tidus victory, Yuna defend at each ruler); this ends the swing between r39, D-457, D-511 and r50.1.
- **Bands (proposed).** Standing, strike (head to soles, props out) and hurt: stature 0.90 to 1.10, real head 0.85 to 1.15. Low (critical, sleep, kneel): 0.75 to 1.10 (0.70 sleep, kneel); a recorded hold may widen the head to 0.80 to 1.45, as today. KO: length 0.70 to 1.30 of idle height, never stature (undecided; Lulu is 0.42). Parts and wide foes: stance only. The 55 poses no scale can fix stay `blocked` until repainted or waived with his word.
- **Generator bug.** `measure.py` writes a `feetRow` from a silhouette that lost the boots; refuse one more than 2 percent of height above the alpha baseline unless a ruler marked it. Repairs the five Lady Luck rows (FFX-2 only, shown before and after).
- **Files.** `tools/posescale/measure.py`, new `pose-model.mjs` (sharp, shared with the gate), `poseRegistrationTypes.ts` (additive, CONTRACT-CHANGES), `PoseRegistration.ts`, `PaintedArt.ts`. **Tests.** Model against the game (Playwright, 40 poses, within 0.02); day-one table equals today's draw except the five rows. **Cost.** 2.5 days.

## 2. Fail-safe runtime: absent means blocked (both games)
- **Design.** `tryLoadMeta` stamps each character painting `trust` from its row's `status`, or `absent` with no row (compiled in; nothing is hashed at run time). `PaintedActor` passes `has(p) && trusted(p)` to the existing `resolvePoseName`, so a blocked pose falls down its chain (attack, ready, idle) and ends on the idle, correct by definition; the flinch, recoil and lunge still carry the beat, as for Biran (one painting). A blocked KO goes down as a figure with no KO painting already does (`KoFallback`, VP-1001-04). Each case logs once (`[pose-trust] subject/pose`), is listed by `__pyrefly.poseTrust()` and read by the critic probe. There is no run-time "computed hold": for a registered pose out of band, install writes the hold into the row (ruler scale clamped into the band, head cost recorded, as r50.1 did).
- **Day one changes nothing.** `waived` draws as today; only art with no row, such as the Dark Aeons, falls back.
- **Files.** `PaintedArt.ts`, `PaintedActor.ts` (two `has` sites), debug API. **Tests.** A Dark-Aeon-shaped fixture (attack with no row) draws the idle and logs; twirl keys and FF7 exempt; KO path; waived rows unchanged. **Cost.** 1.5 days.

## 3. Gates: install and deploy refuse (both games)
- **Design.** New `tools/pose-gate.mjs` and a **waiver ledger** `docs/target/pose-waivers.json` (subject, pose, painting sha, reason, whose word). It fails when a shipped character painting has neither a fresh `ok` row nor a waiver bound to its sha (a re-installed painting loses its waiver); when a row's stored readings differ from a recompute from the pixels (the independent second reading that would have caught the Lady Luck rows); when a pose is out of its band unwaived; or when the ledger grew (ratchet). `--new` becomes the default.
- **Where.** (a) The last step of a new in-repo installer `tools/art-install.mjs` (the common core of the scratch installers, then measure, table, gate); (b) `npm test`, unskipped when `public/art` exists; (c) `deploy-pages.mjs` preflight, on `public/art` before the build and on the built art list after it, beside the art identity step, and **not covered by `--owner-override`**: only a ledger row with Bailey's quoted words clears it.
- **Day one.** Fails on the four Dark Aeons (16 poses; stature already 0.96 to 1.00, so stance and head only, 0.5 day), Valefor, Ixion, X2 Ixion, Seymour Guest, Pterya, Paragon. Beasts placed by the rest rule, Rikku Berserker ready and the hidden Leblanc chapter become legacy waivers.
- **Files.** `tools/pose-gate.mjs`, `tools/art-install.mjs`, `deploy-pages.mjs`, `AGENTS.md` (hard rule: not installed until the gate passes), `docs/ART-PIPELINE.md` §5 to 6. **Tests.** Fixtures that pass today and must now fail: unregistered subject, stale sha, knee-planted `feetRow`, 0.30 stature with a matched head, ledger growth. **Cost.** 3 days.

## 4. Swap smoothing, behind a switch (both games; Bailey picks on clips)
- **Today.** No scale or anchor jump at a swap (the incoming plane is placed and head-locked at opacity 0). The eye sees the dissolve moving the weighted point between heads 110 to 300 px apart, a lunge whose first frame covers 28 percent, the flinch stacking three moves, a painting dropped by `PoseBlend` rule 2, and a 50 ms frame clamp multiplying all of it.
- **Design.** (a) `swapAlign`: the head lock already fixes head size, so only position is eased: both planes' anchors move so the heads coincide at mid-blend and settle to their own places at the ends. (b) Per-pair policy written at install from `iou` and anchor distance: overlap 0.30 or more uses the aligned dissolve; the 129 poses under 0.30 (40 under 0.20) swap on a held frame (menu open, hit-stop, the 180 ms follow hold) as a 2-frame cut, never a two-painting ghost. (c) A bug fix outside the switch: `PoseBlend` re-points the weaker plane only below fade 0.05. (d) Offered in the same clips: the lunge's first frame on the `reachOffset` curve; `maxDelta` 1/20 to 1/40 (jerks of 40 px or more fall from 6.3 to 2.8 a minute in FFX at 1/60).
- **Switch and clips.** `?swap=today|aligned|held`, default `today` until his pick. Virtual 60 Hz clock (Quality Leap method), three moments (Kimahri strike FFX; Lulu hurt and Auron attack FFX; Paine on Bahamut FFX-2), arms side by side with the CHK-027 numbers under each.
- **Files.** `PaintedActor.ts`, `PoseBlend.ts`, install policy field, `tools/swap-clips.mjs`. **Tests.** Pure functions of (pair, t); head travel under 2 px a frame at mid-blend; the dropped-painting case. **Cost.** 3 days.

## 5. The critic sees height and feet, on every candidate (both games)
- **Design.** CHK-026 gains a **height reading** from the probe's alpha masks and plane quads: each pose drawn against the idle in the same frame (stature; length for KOs), failing outside its class band unless waived, and a **feet reading** from the silhouette stance compared with the registered one. The head reading stays, labelled "control". Bailey's words today cover this.
- **Every candidate.** A **static census** (the section 3 recompute over every shipped painting, no browser) goes into `alwaysAtDeploy` and the artifact record. The planner maps `poseRegistration*.ts`, `PoseRegistration/HeadLock/PaintedScale/PoseBlend.ts`, `tools/posescale/**`, `docs/target/pose-*.json` to CHK-026 and CHK-027 (`tools/**` and `docs/**` are "no product effect" today). The browser harness keeps its schedule and gains a virtual-clock mode plus a separate frame-time number.
- **His to rule on.** An unregistered new painting as a ship hold; CHK-027 split into jerks at a swap (gateable) and mid-move dashes (reported); the harness fixes (a point per plane, projected camera correction).
- **Files.** `critic/runner/lib/continuity-{pure,silhouette,probe}.mjs`, `critic/policy.json`, `critic/CHECKS.md`. **Tests.** Replay Lulu at 0.67 and the Dark Aeons (both must FAIL); r50 and r50.1 each with its own record. **Cost.** 2.5 days.

## 6. Structural end state: the 2.5D-plus rig
One painting per figure cut into about 18 parts on a bone rig; the approved paintings are the key poses; in-betweens, smear and impact frames at 60 Hz. No swaps, so no registration between paintings, no ghost, no costume drift (Kimahri: four paintings in one blow; Paine, Chapter IV: three). It is the engine form of D-439 (adopted 10-05, never scheduled); Bailey has not picked it. Per the survey: a Kimahri and Biran prototype (5 days, clip only), then about a day a figure, party first. **How 1 to 5 carry until then and stay:** the spec is the rig's manifest (rest height and stance from the same idle scale; each key checked against its approved painting, overlap 0.85 or more); a rig that fails its gate falls back to the painting set; the census covers rig rows. Foes, bosses and unrigged figures stay painting-per-pose, so 1 to 5 are the permanent floor.
