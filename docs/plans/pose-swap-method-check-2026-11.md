## 1. The short version

1. **The three issues are sums of different things, and the sums hide that most of the work is not in the paintings.** Round 32's CHK-026 headline, 445 swaps with a head over 3 percent, is **427 swaps of Lulu's rig head** (96 percent; 0.1 to 0.2 percent where a painted head is registered), 8 of Braska's aeon, 4 at Braska's victory close-up, 4 of Braska's party and 2 of Auron's in Chapter I's killing blow. Of the 54 stance misses, 34 are one constant 6.1 to 6.6 px reading on Yunalesca's idle and hurt paintings (a line at 6 px) and 17 are Braska's. Of the 25 height misses, 23 are Braska's, which read 0 in round 31. Of the 34 gated swap jerks (all 34 placed, section 3.4), 17 sit at a victory pose, **but only 6 of the 34 sit on a swap the harness marks as a camera cut, all Braska's Auron**: the other 11 victory jerks are a hard swap between two paintings on a frame that ran 104 to 355 ms, with no camera cut in the strip; and **16 of the 34 fall on a frame over 34 ms**, which the harness reports and never gates (18 on a steady frame). PR-0531 and PR-0380 cannot close while their numbers are sums of that kind: that is why five reviews left them at one severity.
2. **What moved since release 66, measured with one tool (section 4).** See-through time, weighted by battle seconds over the same 19 chapters: **3,474 to 1,689 to 1,623 ms a minute** (rounds 30, 31, 32; releases 66, 68, 69 and 70). Gated swap jerks 115 to 66 to 34. Height-band misses 15 to 5, then 25. The two conference chapters: Chapter I 101.7 ghost frames a minute (release 64) to 30.3, Chapter IV 147.4 to 35.8; Chapter IV's ready-idle-ready flicker (22 triples in release 64) is 0 in all 19 chapters. **What did not move:** the number of Lulu rig-head swaps the harness flags (489, 476, 487), Auron's two Chapter I swaps (x0.915 to 0.924 and x1.093 in every round), the three held chapters' see-through (Yunalesca 5,611 ms a minute, Ixion and Sin's face with it: 24.7 percent of all see-through time from 9.1 percent of the battle time), Vegnagun's 201 px tail swap (200.5, 200.8, 201.6 px in three rounds), Paragon's feet (16 swaps, 10.4 px, since round 30).
3. **Why, in four sentences.** (a) *Measurement:* the harness changed in every round (three ids, three pose tables, three policy hashes), no round kept a recording, so a number cannot be re-read by the next tool; and the count of a class is dominated by a reading nobody owns (Lulu), by a camera cut (Braska's and Chapter I's victory and killing blow, and nowhere else) or by the frame clock (16 of the 34 gated jerks). (b) *Registration data:* the few paintings that really are off (Yunalesca's hurt feet, Paragon's cast feet) have never been re-registered, while Evrae's, which was, fell from 3,186 to 1,258 ms a minute. (c) *Method:* every remedy since release 64 went to a class by switch word and chapter list, which worked where it was applied (16 of 19 chapters), and the three chapters it was held out of (Yunalesca, Sin's face, Ixion) are the three worst see-through chapters; their lifts have written jobs and predictions that have never been run. (d) *Ownership:* the family is three umbrella tickets; each release since 69 spent its pose-swap time on regressions of the booth treatment (PR-0581, PR-0582, two focused-review HOLDs before the SHIP of release 71), which the machinery caught, and nobody owns the old residue.
4. **Choice (RUBRIC section 8): change method.** Split the measurement from the paintings from the presenter, in that order of cost: report Lulu's rig head apart as a named class with its count, and write the camera-cut rule (both offline, no look, and **by construction, not by re-measurement**: the first takes 427 of the 445 head counts out of the gate and flips CHK-026 to PASS in five chapters, the second takes the 6 swap jerks on a camera-cut swap and a few of Braska's victory counts out; section 6 items 2 and 3 say exactly what each leaves in); re-register the two figures whose registration is really off; run the three held-chapter jobs that already have predictions; trace Braska's and Den of Woe's treatment regressions against their own `?treated=pack` controls; and only then take the one lever that holds the rest of the see-through time, the five at-rest classes, to Bailey as paired clips (section 6, items 1 to 12).
5. **Two things go to Bailey, each as clips he can judge in motion, and one only if a picture says so:** at-rest pose changes, cut or short blend or today's dissolve (this also answers PR-0530's open question about the planned cuts); and whether Yunalesca's, Sin's face's and Ixion's returning figure slides in or cuts (the held-chapters anchor). Auron's close-up head is the driver's call unless the two-up shows a pop. Exact questions and pictures are in section 6.
