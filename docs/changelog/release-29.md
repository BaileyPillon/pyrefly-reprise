# 2026-09-29 · Release 29

Address: https://baileypillon.github.io/pyrefly-reprise/ (main 49005f73)

- **FFX:** Chapter VII (Seymour and Anima at Macalania Temple) is open for play, with its own scene
  music. The board now has 16 chapters.

  ![Chapter select on a phone before: Chapter VII shows COMING](../screenshots/r29-load/board-phone-cold-15s-before.jpg)
  ![Chapter select on a phone after: Chapter VII is open](../screenshots/ch7-unlock/390x844-01-chapter-select.jpg)

  *On a phone, before then after: Chapter VII was a COMING card (0 of 15) and is now a card you can pick (0 of 16).*

  ![Chapter VII selected on the desktop board](../screenshots/ch7-unlock/1600x900-02-chapter-vii-selected.jpg)

  *Chapter VII selected on the desktop board.*

- **FFX:** Chapters II and III use the sourced Zanarkand and inside-Sin aeon stats, so no aeon is
  weaker later in the story. Chapter II plays easier, and Chapter III's possessed-aeon gauntlet runs
  about 37 percent longer.

  ![Bahamut at 3,218 HP in Chapter II](../screenshots/aeon-hp-2-3/ch02-2-bahamut-hp.jpg)
  ![Ifrit at 2,585 HP in Chapter III](../screenshots/aeon-hp-2-3/ch03-2-ifrit-hp.jpg)

  *Yuna's aeons at the sourced HP: Bahamut with 3,218 in Chapter II, and Ifrit with 2,585 in Chapter III.*

- **FFX-2:** Chapter IV's pause portrait is the approved painting of Yuna and Bahamut again, in
  place of a darker re-render.

  ![Chapter IV pause portrait before](../screenshots/r29-plate/before-1600-pause.jpg)
  ![Chapter IV pause portrait after](../screenshots/r29-plate/after-1600-pause.jpg)

  *Before, then after: the Chapter IV pause portrait was a dark, downcast re-render; it is the approved painting of Yuna again.*

- **FFX-2:** a gamepad now drives the command menu.

  ![FFX-2 menu with a gamepad, before](../screenshots/r29-input/before-pad-1-target.jpg)
  ![FFX-2 menu with a gamepad, after](../screenshots/r29-input/after-pad-1-target.jpg)

  *With a gamepad's d-pad and A button: before, nothing happened (the cursor stayed on the first row); after, White Magic opens and the d-pad moves through its list.*

- **Both:** a cold first load is much faster. On a 25 Mbit/s line the loading card used to hold for
  about 18 to 26 seconds in Chapters I and IV and now holds for 1 to 2 seconds. Each screen's art
  loads before the board's strips, in the order you need it.

  ![Chapter IV's first line on a cold phone load, before](../screenshots/r29-load/ch4-phone-cold-first-line-before.jpg)
  ![Chapter IV's first line on a cold phone load, after](../screenshots/r29-load/ch4-phone-cold-first-line-after.jpg)

  *Chapter IV's first line of dialogue on a cold phone load: before, a black backdrop and an empty portrait box; after, both are painted.*

- **Both:** audio fixes: a quick Esc-Esc no longer leaves the pause music over the fight, a gamepad
  press now starts the sound in Chromium browsers, and new profiles start with sound effects at 35
  percent (they were 90). Existing saves keep their level.

- **Both:** on a phone, a tap on overlapping target brackets picks the one under your finger (a
  Hi-Potion tapped on Yuna could land on Auron), and the first-time coach line no longer blocks taps
  on party reticles.

- **Both:** coach lines stay off the fighters' faces and weapons on desktop, and the battle PAUSE
  chip is back in Ink & Gold style at 14 px.

  ![Chapter I's first coach line, before](../screenshots/r29-input/before-coach-seymour-flux-1600x900.jpg)
  ![Chapter I's first coach line, after](../screenshots/r29-input/after-coach-seymour-flux-1600x900.jpg)

  *Chapter I's first coach line (Auron): before, it sat over the party; after, it sits clear of the fighters' faces and weapons.*
