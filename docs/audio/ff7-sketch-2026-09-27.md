Scorpion encounter. Nothing in FFX or FFX-2 plays it or changes because of it. The board, the

## The composition: "Reactor Alarm"

`tools/audio/scores/2026-09-27/ff7-guard-scorpion.mjs`. F minor coloured by the Phrygian flat
second (Gb) as a structural chord, with a real major dominant (C) at every turn back home.
4/4, 154 bpm, 32 bars (128 beats), 52.9 s file: 6.2 s intro, a 43.6 s loop body from bar 5,
then the 3 s run-on of the loop head (the shipped file layout).

This harmonic language is deliberately **neither house world** (THEMES.md "Harmonic language,
by world"). It is not FFX's plain Aeolian with four rationed dominants, and not FFX-2's Dorian
pop. `themes.ts` and `motifs.ts` are not imported: this is a different game.

It is built from three cells written for this file:

| Cell | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| PISTON | bass ostinato on the synth bass: root, root, octave, root / root, octave, fifth, root in eighths, the off-beats pushed (velocities 0.60 to 0.90); sixteenths at the climax and in the last four bars | from bar 3 to the end |
| SIREN | a two-note alarm a semitone apart (C5 and Db5, the fifth and the flat sixth), two beats each, on the hollow square lead | intro; back in bars 29 to 32 as the tail charges |
| ALARM | the eight-bar tune: it climbs 1 - b3 - 5 - b6, falls back through the flat second (Gb), and ends its answer on the raised seventh (E) pulling up to F. It has rests in bars 3, 4, 6 and 8, so the stabs can speak | violins over horns (A), trumpets with violins an octave up (C), low horns on its first half (D) |

| Bars | Section | Beats | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | intro | 0-16 | siren, reactor clangs, taiko, a tremolo bed on F and C; the PISTON enters at bar 3; a snare roll and two stabs (Gb, C) into bar 5 |
| 5-12 | A | 16-48 | ALARM on the violins over the horns an octave below; stabs 0.80 on the downbeat, 0.95 and 0.90 on the and-of-2 and and-of-4 (THEMES.md boss accents) — **loop start** |
| 13-16 | B | 48-64 | "the tail rises": a climbing line over Db - Eb - Gb - C, quarter-note stabs, timpani on each root, four-on-the-floor kick, a snare roll |
| 17-24 | C | 64-96 | the climax: ALARM on the trumpets, the violins an octave up, sixteenth-note bass, sixteenth string runs, the hats in sixteenths |
| 25-32 | D | 96-128 | the room drains (low horns on the tune's first half, kick on 1 and 3), then the siren returns over Gb and C with the runs, the stabs and an eight-beat snare crescendo back into A |

Instruments (all house presets, the same resolver, seating, hall and master as
`tools/audio/render.mjs`): synth-bass, strings-low, strings, brass (horns), trumpet,
brass-stab, pwm-lead, strings-trem, strings-short, kick, snare, hat, taiko, metal-hit, tom,
crash, cymbal-swell, timpani. Humanisation follows THEMES.md's table (bass 4 ms, sections 14
to 16 ms, solo-register lines 10 to 12 ms). No channel plays at a constant velocity: the tune
uses two four-bar arches (0.60 to 0.84, then 0.62 to 0.88), the climb a crescendo, and the
bass a velocity shape inside each bar.

## How originality was checked

1. **Written from nothing but the three cells above.** No recording, score, MIDI or
   transcription of any retail track was opened, played or used as input. None exists on this
   disk. The retail cue was known only by its name in the research file. No retail audio went
   into the model: the restyle's only audio input is our own render.
2. **The restyle's words name no game, composer, title, place or character.** The renderer
   refuses to run if the tags match `final fantasy | ff7 | vii | uematsu | square enix |
   bombing | those who fight | midgar | mako | shinra | cloud | barret | scorpion`. The words
   are in the table below.
3. **Measured against every note array we own**
   (`tools/audio/scores/2026-09-27/originality-check.mjs`, report
   `docs/audio/ff7-sketch-2026-09-27-originality.json`). Every melodic line of the sketch
   (top voice, onsets merged) was compared with 363 reference lines: the six themes and the
   motif cells, every channel of every shipped cue, and every earlier sketch. The check
   finds the longest shared run of intervals, pitch only and with the rhythm too. The flag
   rule was set before the check was run: 6 or more shared intervals with the rhythm, or 8
   or more pitch-only. A trivial figure does not count: a pedal (at most two real moves
   among repeated notes and octaves) or a one-way scale run. **Result: 0 flags.**
   - ALARM: at most 3 intervals shared with the rhythm. The longest pitch-only run is 7
     intervals against the Yojimbo violin line: Bb Ab Gb F Eb F Ab Bb, a scale falling and
     turning back, with a different rhythm.
   - The piston bass: 4 intervals with the rhythm, 7 pitch-only (`battle-ffx` bass riff:
     root, octave and fifth moves).
   - The brass-stab top voice matches up to 11 intervals with the rhythm, but those are
     repeated chord tones with at most two moves, so the check counts them as trivial.
   - The check changed the score before any audio was made. Three sixteenth-run shapes in
     turn shared 6 to 7 intervals of chord-arpeggio shape (with the rhythm) with other cues'
     ostinati (`boss-evrae`, `battle-ffx`, `victory-ffx`). The shape was changed to
     `[0,1,3,1,2,1,3,2]` (longest shared run: 4).
4. **What this cannot prove.** The check compares the sketch with our own music, not with
   Uematsu's. For the retail side, the proof is how it was made (points 1 and 2), plus the
   restyle keeping our notes (a structure fidelity of 0.432, below).

## What the measurements say (not heard)

- **Structure kept 0.432**: 6 of 8 four-bar windows correlate, chroma similarity 0.897
  against a two-bars-apart baseline of 0.808, onset F 0.535. The notes and the form are
  largely kept; the model re-articulates the rest.
- **Mode, the one to listen for.** The key detector reads our render as F minor (0.715) and
  the pick as C major (0.775). Six of the nine takes read major (F or C). The words ask for
  F minor. Only seed 505 still reads F minor (0.394 kept, onset F 0.560, drift -4 ms/min).
  A C-major reading can come from C being the dominant chord of this cue, or from the model
  brightening the mode; only an ear can say which. If Bailey hears it as too bright, 505 is
  the swap. The pick rule was written before any take was measured and was not changed
  after.
- **Timing drift**: the pick's lag slope is -74 ms per minute. Over the 43.6 s loop that is
  about 50 ms by the wrap, which the crossfade covers. Its tempo reading of 157.75 is 2.4 %
  over the written tempo, inside the rule's 3 %.
- **Brightness**: spectral centroid 418 Hz against the source's 496 Hz: darker, as the other
  Direction B cues went.
