**FFX-2 only** (rule 14: a Special dressphere is an FFX-2 idea). Lane look-a:game, 2026-11-09, for the approved card A ("the game's own reading, faithful to the FFX-2 form"). Records only: nothing here changes a number or a file in the game or the repo. Rule 6: every claim carries its source and a tag, and what no source settles is written as a gap, never guessed.

Tags: `[verified: the game's files]` is read by this lane from the Steam HD build's own data (text banks, headers, tables, motion banks, the decompile dump of the executable); `[verified: 2 sources]`; `[single source: name]`; `[frame: video id, time]` is a frame read from a public long-play (ids and times in section H); `[gap]` is in no source. ## 0. Read this first: the eight things that matter most

1. **The game shows ONE figure, not three bodies.** In every frame read (all three Specials, about 60 battle and sequence frames) the form is a single connected model standing where the party stood; the other two girls are gone from the field and the HUD shows three rows. The two "parts" are not drawn as separate bodies beside the main: the game's job-to-model table gives the three Special main jobs one model per girl (c020 for Yuna, c040 for Rikku, c060 for Paine) and **gives the six pod jobs no model of their own** (their table rows are 0). `[verified: the game's files]` for the table, `[frame]` for the look. Card A's "three bodies take the front" is therefore the driver's reading of "three party members"; the game's picture is one big figure per girl with three plates. Which part of the figure is which pod is not shown by any frame we found (section F, gap 1).
2. **The girl is in her Special.** Yuna stands in the middle of the bloom, Rikku rides on the machine's back, Paine's face appears between the wings in her finisher's cut-in. `[single source: FF Wiki profiles]` plus `[frame]` (sections B and C). The portrait/cut-in behaviour is unchanged by this (the driver's inference stands).
3. **The change is long and unique.** Measured from frames, the full sequence **from the moment the girl is alone (one HUD row) to the cut to the field is about 16 s for Yuna (14 to 16 across three clips), 17 s for Paine and 20 s for Rikku; from the grid's confirm to that cut it is about 19 s for Paine, 20 to 24 s for Yuna and 20 to 24 s for Rikku** (corrected 2026-11-09, see "Checked 2026-11-09" at the end: this line first said "from the grid's confirm to the first menu ... 15 to 16 s for Yuna, about 18 s for Paine", which labelled the shorter middle stretch as the whole); each girl has her own sequence (a bud that blooms; golden light and blue crystal shards, then the machine assembling; a blue silhouette in a cloud with six swords, then wings). `[frame]`.
4. **The Spherechanges setting does reach the Special.** The game's Config has three choices, Normal / Short / OFF ("Display full spherechange sequences" / "Display shortened spherechange sequences" / "Skip spherechange sequences"). Reading the executable: entering a Special under Short plays the same shared short effect an ordinary change does; OFF skips it; the first time a girl enters (and the first time she leaves) her Special, the setting is overridden to the full version. `[verified: the game's files]` (section A.3).
5. **The other two girls "leave" and the HUD says so by the fourth second.** The game's own tutorial says the character "will have to fight alone" with two support units. `[verified: the game's files]`.
6. **The end is spelled out by the game's own tutorial text:** you change out automatically if KO'd or when the battle concludes; to change out during battle you open the Garment Grid and change back to the dressphere you were using before. `[verified: the game's files]`. Frames show the manual exit takes about 4.5 s (section E).
7. **The HUD in a form is three rows**, main first, then the right part, then the left part, each with its own name, HP, MP and gauge; a KO'd part shows its name in red with HP 0 and the form carries on with the others. `[frame]`.
8. **A correction to the repo's own notes (not about the pods' look):** `data/ffx2/hit-reaction-frames.ts` says motion-set slots 16 and 17 "are the pods". The game's motion banks say the numbers in those slots (0x44 to 0x46 and 0x3ce to 0x3d0) belong to the Psychic and Festivalist models, not to any pod. `[verified: the game's files]` (section B.5).

* Preconditions (game text): she has changed into all the dresspheres on her equipped Garment Grid this battle; then "press [grid button] to open the Garment Grid, then press [next button] to access the current character's special dressphere." ### A.3 Full version, short version, and what setting chooses

* **The setting** (Config, "Spherechanges"): three labelled choices, "Normal: Display full spherechange sequences.", "Short: Display shortened spherechange sequences.", "OFF: Skip spherechange sequences." `[verified: the game's files]`. The code distinguishes four stored values (0 to 3): 0 and 1 play the dress's own sequence (value 1 puts the third effect in place of the first), 2 plays one shared short effect (id 734), 3 plays nothing; **which label writes which value was not traced** (gap 4, no effect on the design).
* **What the executable does for a Special** `[verified: the game's files, the animation-choice routine read in the decompile dump; the helper that says "is this job a Special" returns yes for all nine Special jobs]`:

| Setting | Enter a Special | Leave a Special (by the grid, or by KO) | Ordinary change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal (0 or 1) | the Special's own sequence (one id per girl: 679, 680, 681) | its own leave sequence (one id per girl: 762 to 764 in the ordinary case; a second set, 765 to 767, when a per-character flag is set, which the lane reads as "KO'd" from the tutorial text: the existence of the second set is verified, its trigger is inferred) | the dress's three effects |
| Short (2) | **the shared short effect** | **unchanged** (no short variant of a leave exists) | the shared short effect |
| OFF (3) | none (the routine reports "no animation") | none | none |

* **First time forces the full version** (unless a debug flag is set): for an ordinary change, when the dress left or the dress entered has never been used; **for a Special, when that girl has never entered it (and never left it)**. So the first Special Dress Up and the first exit of each girl always play in full whatever the setting `[verified: the game's files; the counters' indexing was read only as far as the helper]`. This agrees with `[verified: 2 sources: FF Wiki and a Steam answer]` on "the first time is always shown in full", with the correction that for a Special it is per girl and Special.
* **Where this leaves the design:** the full Special sequence is the one the player sees first and should be the one worth stopping for; a "short" version exists in the game only as the shared short effect (about the length of an ordinary change, a few seconds); the leave sequence is always full unless OFF. The length of the shared short effect is **not** in any file we can read (gap 3).

After: the form, with the HUD's three rows. `[frame]`. The party is immune and time is halted during the sequence `[single source: FF Wiki, Dressphere]`, with the "Wait Mode" tag on screen for all of it `[frame]`.

## B. The form on the field

1. **One figure, standing in the party's half of the field**, in the middle of that half, the enemies on the other side; the camera usually looks at it from the front or the front-left. `[frame: dG5MFlFYEZ4 98 to 130 s; Zq71lZepMqE 0 to 50 s; IB8BB9OSRuw 90 to 108 s]`. Its exact stand point is not stored (the formation tables for the Leblanc rooms and others are empty, and the form is a single body); the ordinary party's three places in the Bahamut fight are (0,0,-20), (-17,0,-25), (17,0,-25) in game units (other fights differ) `[verified: the game's files: the size lane's table]`, and the form fills the space of all three. In the manual exit the girl in her old dress appears at the place the form stood `[frame: IWjm5Pq8NWc 63 to 64 s]`. Whether it stands on a particular girl's slot is a gap (gap 7; **second pass 2026-11-09: the exit frames put the form at the main girl's own x, the left end of her three, not in the middle of the half**).
2. **Which model, how big.** The game's table from job to model gives: Yuna c020, Rikku c040, Paine c060 for the Special body (the same three rows for the main in all of her Special jobs); the two pod jobs of each girl have **no model row** (0). `[verified: the game's files: the girl-model table in FFX-2.exe, rows 15 to 23]`. Mesh size in game units (Tidus 18.15, the girls' ordinary dresses 16.5 to 17.9): c020 **39.7 tall, 35.1 wide**; c040 **26.2 tall, 30.3 wide, 53.2 deep**; c060 **44.3 tall, 58.0 wide** `[verified: the game's files: the size lane's tables, FINDINGS-sizes]`. (These are default-pose mesh extents, which overstate an idle pose by a few percent, per the size lane.) So the form is about **2.3 times a girl's height for Yuna (and 3 times her width), 1.5 times for Rikku (and 3 times her height in length), 2.6 times for Paine (and 5 times her width)**, and in the frames about twice a human enemy's height. The skeletons: 446 bones (c020), 347 (c040), 147 (c060), every clip animating every bone, i.e. the whole figure is one rig `[verified: the game's files]`.
3. **How it looks from the player's camera** `[frame]`: Floral Fallal is a **front-facing fan**: a vertical disc of layered petals with Yuna in front of its centre; from the side (the camera swings during a cast) it is thin. Machina Maw is a **gold quadruped**, tail low, with a red-brown structure on its back and Rikku small on top; it is seen side-on or three-quarter. Full Throttle is a **horizontal barrel (red bands, brass caps) on two red blade legs, with a fan of long dark blade feathers on each side**, six swords floating above it; seen from the front it is symmetric.
4. **Idle and motion.** The first clip of the main resident bank (the slot that is the idle for the girls) is **140 frames (4.67 s at 30 fps) for c020, 45 frames (1.5 s) for c040, 50 frames (1.67 s) for c060** `[verified: the game's files for the lengths; that it is the idle loop is inferred from its slot]`. Frames show: Yuna's dress and petals fluttering continuously (`[single source: FF Wiki]`: "her clothes are continuously blown about"); the feather fan opening and closing and the swords orbiting for Paine; the beast breathing and shifting its weight for Rikku `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE; IB8BB9OSRuw]`. The Special models have 27, 44 and 42 clips in their second bank (the ordinary Gunner models of the three girls have 36, 38 and 35) `[verified: the game's files]`.
5. **A correction to `hit-reaction-frames.ts`.** The game's motion-set numbers 0x44, 0x45, 0x46 live on the Psychic models (c068, c069, c070) and 0x3ce, 0x3cf, 0x3d0 on the Festivalist models (c974 to c976); the Special body's sets are 0x14, 0x28, 0x3c (one clip bank each, c020, c040, c060). The pods have **no motion set of their own in the girls' list**. `[verified: the game's files]`.
6. **Is the girl visible in the main?** Yuna yes (white dress, hair and face readable inside the bloom in the battle camera as a small figure) `[frame: dG5MFlFYEZ4 98 s; IWjm5Pq8NWc 58 s]`; Rikku yes, riding `[single source: FF Wiki]`, small on the beast's back `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 66 to 70 s]`; Paine is inside the barrel-and-wings figure and **is not readable at battle distance**, but her face and the crossed swords fill the screen in the Sword Dance cut-in `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 46 s]`. Victory poses (`[single source: FF Wiki]`): Yuna throws her arm to her side; Rikku jumps off and strokes the machine's head; Paine hops, extends the wings and kneels on one knee with the wings bent in front of her.
7. **The pods.** `[verified: the game's files]` for: the dev names of the child records ("Yuna child 1", "Yuna child 2" and so on, record ids 3 to 8), **child 1 is the right-hand part and child 2 the left** (the finisher-part commands are named "parts' big technique, right" and "left"; Homing Ray has a right and a left row; the child records take the two jobs after the main's in order, per the repo's note, so child 1 is the first of each pair: Right Pistil, Smasher-R, Dextral Wing), and the pods have their own judgement actors, motion actors and voice actors. `[frame]` for: there is no pod drawn apart from the figure. `[gap]`: which sub-mesh (the fan petals, the missile launchers, the wings) is which part, and whether a pod flashes or moves when it acts or is hit (section F).

## C. The HUD in a form

1. **Three rows**, bottom right, in this order: the main (under the girl's name, not the Special's), then the **right** part, then the **left** part: `Yuna / Right Pistil / Left Pistil`, `Rikku / Smasher-R / Crusher-L`, `Paine / Dextral Wing / Sinistral Wing`. `[frame: IWjm5Pq8NWc 58 s; Zq71lZepMqE 40 s; IB8BB9OSRuw (JP names) 98 s]`. (The grid card's plate names the main by the Special: "Full Throttle", then the wings.)
2. **Each row:** the name, an "HP" label and the HP number, an "MP" label and the MP number, with a thin gauge running under or behind the row. Numbers seen: Floral Fallal at one moment Yuna 6,140 HP and 473 MP, Right Pistil 4,682 and 370, Left Pistil 4,682 and 370; Full Throttle Paine 6,352 and 61, Dextral Wing 8,017 and 312, Sinistral Wing 8,624 and 286 (current values in a fight, not maxima) `[frame: IWjm5Pq8NWc 58 s; Zq71lZepMqE 4 s]`. The main is the girl's own pools, the pods have their own `[verified: the game's files: separate records]`.
3. **Gauges.** Each part has its own ATB gauge `[verified: the game's files: the exe's pods are separate battle bodies]`. In the frames the **thin bar behind each row's HP number is that part's gauge**: an empty dark track with a purple fill growing from the left for a part still charging (Paine's row at 4 s), a green fill for another (Dextral Wing), and **a full pink-purple bar for the part that can act** (Sinistral Wing, whose menu is open in the same frame) `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 4 s, full size]`. The corner tag at top right reads "Active Mode" or "Wait Mode".
4. **The command menu of a part** (top left, under the help line; a D-pad icon and the title "MAIN COMMANDS"): the main of Floral Fallal shows `Attack / Fallalery / Libra` with an `L1  Spherechange` hint bar under it `[frame: IWjm5Pq8NWc 58 s]`; the Right Pistil shows `Right Pistilplay` (and Right Stigma once learned) with no L1 hint `[frame: 60.5 to 64 s]`; Paine `Attack / Throttle / Fright`; Sinistral Wing `Sinistral Arts / Stamina / Mettle / Reboot` with the help line "Use Sinistral Wing skills." `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 4 s]`; Machina Maw's main `Attack / Machinations` (Revival and Vajra once learned), Smasher-R `Homing Ray / Smash / HP Repair / MP Repair`, Crusher-L `Homing Ray / Crush / HP Repair / MP Repair` `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 66 to 90 s; IB8BB9OSRuw 98 s, Japanese labels]`. No Item and no Escape in a form `[verified: the game's files: the repo's note section 3]`; the L1 hint appears only on the main's menu.
5. **What marks whose turn it is.** In Active mode the menu opens for the part whose gauge is full, titled by the help line ("Use Right Pistil skills."), and the next part's menu follows the first's `[frame]`. During a target pick the **name of the targeted row turns yellow** `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 40 to 41 s]`. The ability's name flashes in a bar at the top `[frame]`.

## D. Targeting

1. **Player picks an ally** (Stamina on the main from Sinistral Wing): the target row's name goes yellow; the heal number rises **above the centre of the figure** (the number 2,467 over the middle of the top of the drum) and that row's HP changes `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 40 to 45 s]`. There is no separate pointer on a pod seen in any frame: whether a pod pick moves a cursor to a part of the figure is a gap.
2. **Enemy attacks and the player's targets across the parts.** The HP of all three rows falls during a fight (Paine 6,352 to 1, Dextral Wing 8,017 to 5,954, Sinistral Wing 8,624 to 6,499 between 4 s and 38 s of the clip, read at full size) `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE]`, so enemies do hit the pods as well as the main; how the enemy picks among the three (even, or weighted) is not seen (gap 6).
3. **A KO'd part:** its **name turns red, HP shows 0 with an empty bar, MP stays shown**; the figure does not change; the others act on `[frame: IB8BB9OSRuw 90 to 104 s]`. The form carries on with the main alone when both pods are KO'd `[frame: IB8BB9OSRuw 98 to 102 s]`.
4. **Revival of a part.** Revival (Machina Maw), All-Life (Floral Fallal, party, can target KO) and Reboot (both wings) raise a KO'd part (`[verified: 2 sources: the game's tables, GamerGuides "revives the allying parts with half of their maximum HP"]`). Auto-Life brings a KO'd main back: at 90 s all three Machina rows read 0, at 94 s the figure is wrapped in green and at 98 s Rikku has 200 HP and the pods are still KO'd `[frame: IB8BB9OSRuw 90 to 98 s]`. That is consistent with "every special dressphere part has Ribbon plus Auto-Life innately" `[single source: combat-core 3.15, GamerGuides]`; the clip does not say what raised her (a green revive burst).

## E. The end of the form

1. **By choice** `[verified: the game's files: the tutorial text]`: open the Garment Grid and "change back to the dressphere you were using before". The grid in the form shows the card with that one dress ("Mascot ... Become a magical mascot!") `[frame: IWjm5Pq8NWc 60 s]`. By the code of section A.3 this is the game's own leave sequence (Normal and Short both play it; OFF would have skipped it; the first exit would be forced full), so **about 4.5 s is the length of the leave**, not a shortened form of something longer.
2. **By KO** `[verified: the game's files: "You automatically change out of a special dressphere if KO'd"]`, and the executable plays a **separate leave effect for the KO case** (one id per girl, 765 to 767) `[verified: the game's files]`. The game's text says "KO'd" of the character; it does not say what a standing pod does. The Machina clip shows the main KO'd twice in a form (the first time brought back by Auto-Life) and no change-out until the second, final fall (section D.4, D.5). What the clip shows after the final fall: the leave-after-KO picture (greying heap, green-white dissolve) and **the girl left lying KO'd in her old outfit at the form's place** (D.5); the other two girls did not return in that clip (it ends in Game Over four seconds later). Closest reading: the main's final KO ends the form with that effect and she lies KO'd where it stood.
3. **By the battle's end** `[verified: the game's files: the tutorial text and the exe: at the next battle's start a girl in a Special is put back into the dress she wore before, as the repo's note section 2 item 6 already says]`. In the clips the results screen follows the victory with the three girls listed `[frame: Zq71lZepMqE 22 to 28 s]`; the victory poses of section B.6 play in the form.
4. **The dress she returns to** is the one she stood on before the form `[verified: the game's files]`, her pools following the ratio rule `[verified: exe, the repo's note]`; the gate and node marks were wiped on entering `[verified: exe, the repo's note]`.
5. **The other two girls return** at the end of the change-out, in their places `[frame: IWjm5Pq8NWc 65 s]`.
