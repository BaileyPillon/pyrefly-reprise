## What this candidate is, and what it is being compared with

The live build is `fd0ae96` (Build A.2). Opened for one purpose in this review — to answer the
regression question — it still serves the old `.ig-title-screen` title and a chapter board with
zero `.fe-card` elements. **The entire front end in this candidate is new.** That single fact
decides most of the ship tagging below: an element that does not exist live cannot break there.

The candidate was built in the clean worktree with `npx vite build --outDir dist-gate
--emptyOutDir` and served with `vite preview` on 127.0.0.1:5471 under the production base
`/pyrefly-reprise/`. No dev server and no main-tree build was reviewed. Every capture ran
headless Chromium with `PYREFLY_BROWSER=gpu` (ANGLE d3d11); no canvas came back black, so no
fallback was needed.

`node tools/critic-plan.mjs --json` returns `depth: deep`, `deepBeforeDeploy: false`,
`focusedBeforeDeploy: true`, `deepAfterDeploy: true`, with `src/engine/ArtManifest.ts`,
`tools/gen/manifest.mjs` and `src/ui/common/face-crops.json` naming the shared systems. Because
`deepBeforeDeploy` is false, this focused pass is the right review before the deploy and the
deep review follows on the live build.

## The changed flow, end to end, with the player's own input

Every claim below came from a real event. The debug API was used only to read state and pump
frames; it never opened a screen or started a chapter.

**Keyboard.** Enter on the title reaches the chapter select at 1600x900, 2000x1012 and 390x844.
On the board, ArrowRight ten times cycles exactly the five playable ids — `yunalesca`,
`braskas-final-aeon`, `ffx2-bahamut`, `ffx2-vegnagun-shuyin`, `seymour-flux`, and round again —
and never lands on a COMING card. ArrowUp/Down moves between the two game groups. Each of the
five chapters was then started with arrows and Enter alone (0 to 4 presses) and reached
`party-prep`; a single Escape returned to `chapter-select` every time.

**Mouse and touch.** A click and a tap on the PRESS ENTER chip both reach the board. On the
board the two-step rule works for both: the first click or tap on a rail card moves the
selection (`seymour-flux` → `braskas-final-aeon`), the second, on the plate, starts it.

**Locked cards.** A real click on the "Seymour and Anima — Coming" card leaves `selectedId`
unchanged and starts nothing. The guard holds.

**Cleared state.** `save.recordClear('yunalesca', …)` followed by a full page reload brings the
board back with `cleared: ["yunalesca"]` in its snapshot, one `.fe-card--cleared` element and one
visible check mark. It reads from the save, not from session memory.

**Onboarding.** Every chapter start lands on `party-prep` directly; no briefing appeared in any
of the runs. The switch is off, as the brief says it should be.

**learn/.** Absent from `dist-gate`, and nothing under `src/` references it. It is not part of
the shipped bundle, so it is out of scope for this review.

## The shared-system half

262 distinct `/art/` requests across all five chapters played to an outcome: zero responses at or
above 400, zero image responses served as `text/html`, zero images with `naturalWidth === 0`, and
zero console errors or page errors in any of the eleven browser contexts this review opened. The
new `art/title/` folder resolves through the manifest; both title planes are one request for
`art/title/keyart.2x.webp`, which is what the handoff claims.

`maxRgbOfPng` over `dist-gate/art` scanned 363 PNGs: none all-black, none undecodable.

`grep -rn '\.[0-9]\.png\|\.raw\.png' src/ --include=*.ts` returns one hit, a doc-comment
reference to the concept source in `titleMarkup.ts`. No shipped path sits on a promotable
variant.

Poses fire per chapter and per game: `characters/paine-warrior/idle.png` in chapter 4 and
`characters/paine-dark-knight/idle.png` in chapter 5, with `rikku-dark-knight` and
`yuna-white-mage` beside them; FFX chapters bring Tidus, Yuna, Kimahri and Auron. No monogram
fallback was visible in any staged battle. Paine's HUD chip is her painted portrait, not a letter
tile — the re-measured `face-crops.json` rows did not knock the crop off her head, though they
left it tight (FE-004).

## Game-aware case (AGENTS.md rule 14, CHK-021)

**Both.** The case is written down in `docs/handoff/frontend-showpiece.md` and repeated in the
doc comments of `ChapterSelectScreen.ts` and `chapterGrid.ts`, with its reasoning: the title is
the front door to both halves and the board holds both games' encounters, and CHK-020 makes
shared plumbing "both" by default. The build matches: both groups are present and reachable, all
five chapters start from the same board with the same keys, and the only per-game difference in
the DOM is the `.ig--ffx2` accent on the FFX-2 heading and its cards — the existing `tokens.css`
swap. Nothing true of one game leaked into the other, in either direction.

## Ship decision

`tools/critic-policy.mjs shipVerdict` agrees with the tags: **SHIP**, with FE-001 disclosed. No
critical defect, nothing blocking, no regression. `changedArea` is FAIL and `ship` is SHIP on
purpose — RUBRIC §3 contemplates exactly this: the phone legibility failure keeps the changed
area from being accepted, and the candidate is still plainly better than what is live.

## Honest limits of this pass

No performance or load measurement was taken on the new parallax title. The `reduceMotion` static
fallback was not exercised. Gamepad, Safari, Firefox, any physical device and any real controller
have no evidence path in this project at all (RUBRIC §10) and are unknown, not passed. CHK-002's
2560x1080 and 3840x2160 legs, the pause screen, results, prep tabs and HUD panel geometry against
the painted actors were not measured here and belong to the deep review that follows the deploy.
