## The two majors

**FOC-05 (major, disclosed).** The FOC-01 fix made the advisor card visible on the FFX first turn and then covered it. It does not hold the build.

**FOC-06 (major, disclosed, inherited).** Walking all 86 battle-HUD text nodes at 1600x900 through their accumulated transform, eight are under the 12 px floor and every one is in the move advisor card: "Guide's pick" 9.75 px, the four stat chips 10.5 px, the "N hide moves" chip 11.0 px, "Next best move" 11.5 px. Opening the live build in the same session gives the identical numbers, so this is inherited from live, not introduced: `introducedByCandidate: false`, `regressionVsLive: false`. The arithmetic FOC-04 used applies unchanged (4.8 css px x 2.5 = 12.0).
