## How it was checked (and what limits it)

- **Real input, headless Playwright from node, the real GPU (RTX 5070 Ti, `--use-angle=d3d11`). Never the Chrome extension, never the in-app pane.** The title, the board, the party prep and the scene were walked with real key presses through the repo's own critic libraries (`critic/runner/lib/route-evidence.mjs`, `route-ui.mjs`: `openRoute`, `makeInput`, `makeChooser`); the seed was set with `__pyrefly.setSeed(n)` before the first key (the critic runner's one non-key hook). `?coach=off` (a UI-only toggle) was used for the runs that only look; the two real-key turns used the full coach flow.
- **A dev server, no production build** (D: is short on space): `vite` on `127.0.0.1:5391` serving the worktree `D:/pyrefly-r39-looks`, the cache and the file watch kept out of the shared tree, over the r39 hi-res PNG masters (the worktree's `public/art` junction, read only). The worktree's `public/` has no `fx/` (the Living Paintings depth maps, untracked): a small scratch middleware serves them from main's `public/fx`, read only, so the plates exist as they do in the game. Dev serves the PNGs (a production build ships lossless WebP for part of them); nothing in this check depends on that.
- **The machine was shared the whole time.** The GPU with ComfyUI's art lane and other agents' headless browsers (10 headless Chromium processes, the GPU at 100 percent in bursts), the CPU at 90 to 100 percent from other agents' full unit suites. Every timing reading below says whether the GPU was quiet. For the governor's 4K repeat the driver's `PAUSE-GPU` file (NOW.md: "while the critic measures frame times") was created at 22:05:37 and moved out at 22:08:50 (to `F:/pyrefly-parked/2026-10-04/r39-looks-check/`); the art lane's watcher resumed its batch. Nothing else of another agent's was touched.
- **What was checked on which tree.** Items (a) to (d) ran on the branch with the lunge fix present; the fix changes only FFX Chapter III strikes (a registry that is empty everywhere else), so it cannot move (a), (b), (d), nor the clearance numbers of (c) (figure positions are not touched). The lunge fix's own proof is in `docs/handoff/r39-looks.md`, "Follow-up".
- Harness and raw numbers (not in the repo): `D:/Tools/pyrefly-scratch/2026-10-04/r39-looks-check/` (`cap/`, `lib/`, `out/*.json`).

## What a reader should take away

- The F plus default is real, on by default, 2x, with both SMAA passes off, and the plates really read 16 on the GPU (the one claim a property read could not show).
- The governor does what it says: down only, once per window, floor at `a2`, pinned frames stand it down, the phone is never fed. Its one cost is that it cannot forgive a bad window (M3).
- Chapter III's picture is clear of the boss for the whole menu in the Tidus-first case and for all but the first 2 s in the Yuna-first case, against live's touching on every frame; turning the switch off restores live's numbers.
- Nothing was run that this note does not say: no e2e (Playwright) suite, no `CHK_STRICT=1`, no Safari, no Firefox, no integrated or weak GPU, no battery-saver 30 fps case, no held Overdrive shot with supersampling (the Overdrive frame in `docs/screenshots/r39-looks-lunge/` was taken at the strike's apex through the real presenter, not at the Overdrive's hold shot).
